## Summary

This PR adds a landing page at `/r/replay-vision` for people who click a Google ad for "session replay".

Brian Young runs Google Search ads for Session Replay that go to Replay Vision. About 881 US searches a month are for "session replay" and 7 are for "replay vision". At the moment, people who search "session replay" arrive on the Replay Vision page and do not see the term they searched for. This page shows "Session replay + Replay Vision" above the H1. Then it explains how Session Replay and Replay Vision work together, and why Replay Vision is worth a try. Brian will split the Google Ads traffic 50/50 between the current `/replay-vision` product page and this page.

The page has these sections:

- A hero with the real "[Website] Opportunity miner" observation screenshot.
- How it works.
- The scanners it can run.
- Setup with the wizard, or in the app.
- The PostHog MCP.
- Questions.
- A footer CTA.

The page is `noindex` and is not in the nav. The current `/replay-vision` page does not change.

This page replaces PR #20362, which was closed and not merged. It follows Joe Black's feedback on that PR:

- It uses the standard product page parts and styles, the same as `/session-replay` and `/replay-vision`. It has no custom paper or grid background, no mono uppercase eyebrows, and no "01 / 02" labels.
- It uses one CTA pair everywhere: "Get started free" (signup) and "Install with AI" (goes to the install section).
- It has no "Further reading" section. The Questions section replaces it.
- The final CTA uses the current product page footer CTA layout.

Copy notes:

- The 2,500 Replay Vision credits are a limited-time launch offer (see `/replay-vision/pricing`). The copy says "for a limited time" and gives the usual free tier of 500. When the offer stops, the copy must change.
- We removed claims that the docs do not support: citations on every observation, triggering a workflow from observations, Replay Vision on mobile recordings, and a "New" badge on Experiment.
