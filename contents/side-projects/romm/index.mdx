A self-hosted ROM manager and player for retro game collections. RomM scans your library, pulls metadata and cover art from more than 10 providers, and lets you play right in the browser with built-in emulation – including save syncing across devices. It supports over 400 platforms, multi-user setups with SSO, a full REST API, and runs anywhere you can run Docker.
