**Product analytics** is the measurement of how people use a software product, based on [events](/hogpedia/event-based-analytics) recorded while they use it. PostHog's glossary defines it as analytics that track identified and anonymous users and how they interact with a product, and notes that product analytics tools usually offer more detailed analysis than web analytics tools because they can follow an identified user.<Ref id="1" />

**Product Analytics** is also the name of the PostHog product that does this.<Ref id="3" />

## What problem it solves

A team that ships a feature cannot tell from the code whether the feature worked. Server logs show that a request happened; they do not show that a person finished what they came to do, or where they stopped.

Product analytics answers four questions that a log cannot:

- **How many?** How often does a thing happen, and is that number moving?
- **Where do people stop?** At which step of a sequence do people drop out?
- **Do they come back?** Does a person who did a thing once do it again?
- **What did they do first?** What path did a person take to reach a point?

## How it works in PostHog

An event is a named record of a thing a person did, with properties attached, tied to a person. A PostHog SDK sends those events; autocapture can record clicks and pageviews without a developer naming each one first.<Ref id="2" />

From that one event stream, PostHog builds four main insight types:

- **Trends** count events over time, broken down by a property.
- **Funnels** measure conversion through an ordered sequence of steps.
- **Retention** measures whether people who did one thing return to do it again.
- **User paths** show the routes people take between events.

Anything the insight editor cannot express can be written as SQL against the same data, in a dialect PostHog calls HogQL.<Ref id="4" />

## How it relates to other PostHog products

Product analytics is the layer the other products read from and write to.

- **[Session replay](/hogpedia/session-replay)** answers *why* a funnel step loses people. A funnel gives the number; a replay shows one person failing at that step.
- **[Feature flags](/hogpedia/feature-flags)** record which variant each person saw, so an analytics query can be split by it.
- **[Experiments](/hogpedia/experiments)** are built directly on funnel and trend insights: an experiment metric is an insight, measured per variant.
- **Surveys** attach what a person said to what that person did.

Web analytics, a separate PostHog product, reports on the same event stream but is organized around traffic, sources, and pages rather than around identified people.
