You should be able to find answers to most of your payroll questions here. If you can't, see [questions](#questions) at the end of this page.

## When you get paid

**Employees**

- US (PostHog Inc): paid on the 15th and the last day of the month. Your payslip is in BambooHR.
- UK (Hiberly Ltd): paid on the last working day of the month. Your payslip is in Sage.
- Germany (PostHog GmbH): paid on the last working day of the month. Your payslip is in Deel.

**Employed through Deel (EOR)**

- Paid on the last working day of the month. Your payslip is in Deel.
- Canada is the exception: you're paid every two weeks.

**Contractors**

- Paid monthly through your Deel invoice.

If payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, you're paid on the working day before.

## When changes show up in your pay

- Pay review changes take effect from the 1st of the month after your review. See [pay reviews](/handbook/people/compensation#pay-reviews).
- Any change needs to reach payroll before that month's cutoff. If it misses the cutoff, it will be paid in the next payroll and backdated.
- If you're employed through Deel or you're a contractor, you need to sign the pay change in Deel before it can be applied.

## Your first payroll

- Complete all your onboarding tasks in Deel (and BambooHR if you're in the US) during your first week. If anything is missing, you may not be included in your first payroll.
- In the UK, send us your P45 from your previous employer, or fill in the [HMRC starter checklist](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/paye-starter-checklist) if you don't have one. Without it, you may be put on an emergency tax code.

## Keeping your details up to date

**Bank details**

- Germany, EOR or contractor: update them in Deel.
- UK: send them to Milagros through a 1Password share link.
- US: update them in BambooHR.

We'll never ask you to change your bank details through an email link or a message from someone you don't know. If you get a request like this, check with us in Slack first.

**Home address**

- Update it in Deel.
- In the US, also update it in BambooHR. Your address decides which state tax is withheld.

**Moving**

- If you're moving to another state or country, tell us before you move. It can change your taxes or the entity that employs you.


## Retirement contributions

- US: to change your 401(k) contribution, ask us and we'll update it in BambooHR. Changes made only in Vestwell will be overwritten by the next payroll. The PostHog match is calculated per paycheck, with no true-up at the end of the year.
- UK: see [pension](/handbook/people/benefits#pension).

## Why did my net pay change?

The most common reasons:

- A pay change, or a bonus or commission payment
- A new tax code (UK), often after a taxable benefit is reported
- A taxable benefit, like [free merch](/handbook/people/benefits)
- A change to your pension, 401(k) or salary sacrifice deductions. Some salary sacrifice deductions follow the provider's invoice, so they can show up a month later.

If none of these explain it, ask us.

## Tax documents and letters

- US: your W-2 is in BambooHR.
- UK: your P60 and P45 are in Sage.
- Germany and EOR: your tax documents are in Deel.

For an employment verification letter, request it in Deel if you're employed through Deel or in Germany. Otherwise, ask us.

## If something looks wrong

Tell us as soon as you notice. If you were underpaid, we'll fix it in the next payroll. If you were overpaid, we'll contact you and agree how to recover it.
