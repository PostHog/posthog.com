Community moderator is a role we give to a small number of trusted community members. It is not the same as the staff moderator role that PostHog team members get.

The tools are deliberately narrow. You get four actions, all of them reversible, and none of them can delete anything. If you click the wrong thing, someone can undo it.

This page is the guide we send to new community moderators. <SmallTeam slug="builder-relations" /> owns it.

## What you can do

| Action | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Mark a reply as the solution, and undo it | On each reply |
| Add or remove forum topics | Moderator tools panel, below the question |
| Hide a reply, or make it visible again | On each reply |
| Archive or restore a thread | Top of the thread |
| Escalate a thread to PostHog | Moderator tools panel |

You also see things other people cannot. Hidden replies are visible to you with a note that says only moderators can see them. Treat what you find there as private.

Every action you take is recorded with your name. We review the log now and then. It is there to back you up when someone questions a decision, not to catch you out.

## Mark as solution

This is the most valuable thing you can do. Solutions show up in search, and they stop the next person from asking the same question.

**We want the person who asked to mark their own solution.** They know whether it actually solved their problem. You do not.

So the order is: nudge first, mark second. Reply and ask if that answer worked for them. Give them time to come back.

Rules for when you do mark it yourself:

- **Wait a few days.** Do not mark a solution the same day a reply lands, and never within a few hours of it. The person who asked deserves the chance to answer first.
- **Only mark it if you are confident it solves the question.** "Max AI said it" is not confidence. Neither is "looks good to me." If you have not read the answer closely enough to know it is right, you are not ready to mark it.
- **Do not mark your own reply as the solution.** Ask another moderator to check it instead.

If you are unsure, ask. Post the thread in Discord or in <PrivateLink url="https://posthog.slack.com/archives/C0AB78YBCNA">#team-builder-relations</PrivateLink> with something like: "I think this reply answers the question and nobody has replied in a few days. Can I get a sanity check before I mark it solved?" Nobody will mind.

## Forum topics

You can add and remove the topics on a thread. Use it when a thread is untagged or tagged wrong.

Tag what the question is about, not the product the person happens to use. Two or three accurate topics are better than six vague ones.

**The topic list is a work in progress.** We are reorganizing it, so expect it to change. If you see questions that do not fit any current topic, tell us. That feedback is more useful to us than the tagging itself.

Do not remove a topic a PostHog team member added. Ask first.

## Hide a reply

Hiding takes a reply out of public view. It does not delete it, and you can put it back.

Use it for spam, promotion, abuse, or a reply that exposes private data such as an API key or customer information.

Do not use it on a reply that is only wrong. Reply and correct it instead. A wrong answer with a correction under it helps the next reader more than a gap does.

If it is urgent, such as leaked credentials or targeted abuse, hide it first and flag it in Discord straight after.

For Discord bans and moderation complaints, see [community channels](/handbook/community/channels#bans-and-grievances).

## Archive a thread

**Use this one rarely.** It is the heaviest tool you have.

Archiving removes a thread from the forum listings and from search. It also turns off the reply box, so nobody can add anything to the thread again.

That last part is why we ask you to be careful. If you reply with a link to a better thread and then archive the post, the person who asked can still read your reply through their original link, but they cannot reply back to you.

Archive a thread when it is spam, or when it is not a real question at all.

Do not archive a thread because it is old, unanswered, or a duplicate. Unanswered questions are a signal we track, not clutter. We are building a way to merge duplicate threads properly, so leave duplicates alone until that ships.

## Escalate to PostHog

This button tells PostHog to pay attention to a thread. Today it is labeled "Escalate to support."

Use it when:

- The thread looks like a real bug.
- Someone needs account or billing help that only PostHog can give.
- PostHog should answer directly. Feature requests count. So do questions about where we stand on something.
- You cannot answer the question yourself.

**Be liberal with this.** If you read a question and cannot answer it, escalate it. Somebody else in the community might answer it first, and that is fine. We would rather be over-notified than leave a person stuck. If it turns out we get too many, we will tune it later.

## What you cannot do

These are staff-only:

- Delete a reply or a question
- Block or unblock a person
- Edit a post that another person wrote
- Mark a reply as unhelpful
- See the email address of the person who asked
- Open the thread in Strapi

If you try one of these, the site refuses the change. A mis-click cannot do damage.

## Getting the role

Ask in Discord or email [community@posthog.com](mailto:community@posthog.com). We give the role to people who already answer questions well and often.

PostHog team members do not need this role. See [answering community questions](/handbook/community/questions) instead.
