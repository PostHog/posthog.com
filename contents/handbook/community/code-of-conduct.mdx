This covers our Discord server, the forum at [/questions](/questions), and PostHog events in real life. It applies to everyone in those spaces, including the PostHog team.

If you are contributing code, read the [contributor code of conduct](/docs/contribute/code-of-conduct) instead. It covers pull requests, issues, and the repositories.

## The short version

Be a person. Assume the other person is one too.

Most of what follows is that one idea applied to specific situations. If you only read this far, you will probably be fine.

## What good looks like here

We are a community of people who build things. The best version of that looks like this:

- **Show your work, including the parts that broke.** A post about what went wrong is more useful than a post about what went right, and it is much harder to write. We would rather read yours.
- **Answer a question when you know the answer.** You do not need to work at PostHog. Most of the best answers on the forum did not come from us.
- **Ask the obvious question.** Somebody else has it and has not asked. Nobody here thinks less of you for it.
- **React to things.** A reaction tells a person that somebody read what they wrote. It costs nothing and it is the single easiest way to make this place feel alive.
- **Say when you disagree, and say why.** Disagreement is welcome and useful. Contempt is neither.

## Where the line is

These get your post removed, and repeated they get you removed:

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in direct messages. This includes sexual attention that is not wanted.
- Attacks on a person rather than an argument. Insults, slurs, and abuse aimed at who somebody is rather than what they said.
- Posting private information about another person without their permission.
- Spam and scams.

We do not keep a longer list because we do not need one. If you are close enough to the line to wonder, ask a moderator first.

## Promoting your own work

You built something. You want people to see it. Good, that is most of the point of this place.

**You can:** share what you are building, link your project, post your blog, ask for feedback, and mention your company when it is relevant to the conversation.

**Ask a moderator first for:** recruiting, market research, and anything where the community is the audience rather than the conversation. Usually the answer is yes. We just want to know.

**Do not:** post the same message across channels, direct message people who did not ask you to, or use the community as a mailing list.

The test is whether you are here. Somebody who takes part and occasionally mentions their thing is a member. Somebody who only shows up to post links is an advertiser.

One specific thing, and we would rather say it plainly than pretend it is not a rule: do not use these spaces to recruit the PostHog team, or to organize something that directly competes with PostHog. We pay for this place. If you want to work with us on something, email [sales@posthog.com](mailto:sales@posthog.com) and we will talk about it properly.

## A note on AI

Many of us build with AI, and Max AI answers questions on the forum. That is fine and expected.

What we ask is that you do not paste an AI answer you have not checked. A confident wrong answer costs the person asking more time than no answer at all. If you are passing along something a model told you and you have not verified it, say so.

## What happens if something goes wrong

We would rather talk to you than ban you. In practice it usually goes:

1. **A quiet word.** A moderator sends you a message explaining what the problem is. Most things stop here.
2. **A warning.** We tell you what needs to change and what happens if it does not.
3. **A timeout.** You are muted or removed for a set period.
4. **A ban.** For serious behavior, or for a pattern that has not changed.

We skip straight to a ban for harassment, threats, and anything targeting a person. There is no warning ladder for that.

If we ban somebody from Discord, we write the reason in the ban dialog so they can see it, and we include an address to appeal to.

## Reporting something

Email [community@posthog.com](mailto:community@posthog.com). It reaches the Builder Relations and Marketing teams.

You can also message any moderator directly in Discord if that is easier.

Please do not send this to support. Support handles product and account problems, and community reports get lost there. If you are banned, you may not be able to open a support ticket anyway.

We keep reports private. If you tell us about something, we do not tell the other person who reported it.

## Appeals

If you think we got a decision wrong, email [community@posthog.com](mailto:community@posthog.com) and say so. A different person from the one who made the call will read it. We do get these wrong sometimes.

## Moderators

Moderators are a mix of PostHog staff and trusted community members. The community moderator role is deliberately limited, and everything it can do is reversible and recorded. See [community moderator tools](/handbook/community/moderator-tools) for what the role can and cannot do.

Moderators are held to this document too. If a moderator is the problem, [community@posthog.com](mailto:community@posthog.com) still works.

## Changes
