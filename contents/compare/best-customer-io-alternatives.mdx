Customer.io is a good marketing automation tool. People leave it for one of three reasons: the $100 a month floor is steep for a small team, the product data has to be piped in before a campaign can use it, or the team already has that data somewhere else and does not want a second copy.

This list is for product and growth teams who send broadcasts and drip campaigns, ranked by how each tool fits a team that already tracks its product.

The right pick comes down to what you care about most:

-   **If you want campaigns triggered by what people do in your product, with the conversion rate next to each one:** [PostHog](/messaging)
-   **If you are an enterprise with a dedicated lifecycle team and multiple channels:** Braze or Iterable
-   **If you are a SaaS team that only sends email and wants a flat price:** Loops
-   **If you want transactional and marketing email on a cheap plan with a real free tier:** Brevo
-   **If you sell to consumers and want templates and a huge integration catalog:** Mailchimp

### What's the best Customer.io alternative for product teams?

For teams that already track their product in PostHog, it's **[PostHog Email & Messaging](/messaging)**.

Broadcasts and drip campaigns run on the events, cohorts, and person properties you already send to PostHog. There is no sync to build and no second copy of your users to keep in step. A campaign starts when a person does something, waits, branches on what they do next, and the conversion rate sits next to each step.

The part Customer.io cannot do: every send lands on the person as an event, next to their replays, funnels, and flags. So you can put "opened the win-back email" in a funnel with "came back and upgraded", or watch the replay of what someone did after the onboarding nudge.

**What makes PostHog good for product teams:**

-   **Triggers:** Any product event, a cohort or person-property audience, or a schedule, with a live estimate of the reach.
-   **Builder:** A canvas with delays, wait-until conditions, conditional and random splits, and PostHog actions. Broadcasts have a five-step editor for one-off and recurring sends.
-   **Channels:** Email from your own verified domain, mobile push through FCM and APNs, Slack, and webhooks.
-   **Opt-outs:** Message categories (marketing or transactional), one-click unsubscribe, a preferences page, and an [importer for Customer.io opt-out lists](/docs/workflows/import-customerio-optouts).
-   **Results:** Conversion goals per campaign, open and click rates, and every engagement event queryable in insights.
-   **Agents:** Ask PostHog AI or the [MCP server](/mcp) to build the sequence and write the emails.
-   **Pricing:** 10,000 emails free every month, then from $0.003 per email. [Transactional emails](/transactional-emails) share the same free tier and meter.

**Not ideal if:** you need SMS, WhatsApp, or in-app messages today, or you want an email tool with nothing else attached.

**Alternatives to consider:** **Braze** or **Iterable** for enterprise multi-channel programs, **Loops** for email-only SaaS teams.

### What's the best Customer.io alternative for enterprise lifecycle marketing?

**Braze** and **Iterable** are the picks when a dedicated team runs programs across email, push, SMS, in-app, and WhatsApp, and procurement is fine with a contract.

Both are built for scale: Braze Canvas has decision splits and experiment paths, and Iterable journeys have attribute splits and A/B splits with up to 10 branches. Both trigger on custom events from their own SDKs and APIs, and both have MCP servers in beta.

**What makes Braze and Iterable good for enterprise teams:**

-   **Triggers:** Custom events from the vendor SDK or API, segment membership, and schedules.
-   **Channels:** Email, push, SMS, in-app, and web push. Braze adds WhatsApp, LINE, and KakaoTalk.
-   **Results:** Up to four conversion events per Braze campaign, one per Iterable journey, with funnel reports on campaigns.
-   **Transactional:** Iterable has a transactional endpoint in the same product. Braze sells transactional email as an add-on.
-   **Pricing:** No public pricing for either. Braze has no free plan. Iterable has no free tier or trial listed.

**Not ideal if:** you want to sign up and send today, or you are a small team paying by the email.

**Alternatives to consider:** **PostHog** for the same event-triggered journeys on data you already have, with public pricing.

### What's the best Customer.io alternative for SaaS teams that only send email?

**Loops** is the pick if email is your only channel and you want one flat price.

Loops is built for SaaS teams: marketing and transactional email in one product, triggered by events from its API, with branching loops and experiment nodes. The Growth plan is $49 a month for 5,000 contacts with unlimited sends.

**What makes Loops good for SaaS teams:**

-   **Triggers:** Events from the Loops API, contact property changes, and list membership.
-   **Builder:** Branching loops with filters, and an experiment node for percentage splits.
-   **Channels:** Email only. No SMS, push, in-app, webhooks, or Slack steps.
-   **Agents:** An official MCP server that creates and manages campaigns and loops.
-   **Pricing:** Free for your first 1,000 contacts with 4,000 sends per 30 days and a "Powered by Loops" footer. Growth is $49 a month.

**Not ideal if:** you want to branch on what a person did in your product beyond the events you send to Loops, or you need any channel other than email.

**Alternatives to consider:** **PostHog** when you want the sends next to your product analytics, **Brevo** for a bigger free tier.

### What's the best Customer.io alternative for small teams on a budget?

**Brevo** is the pick for a small team that wants marketing and transactional email on one plan, with a free tier that lets you send 300 emails a day to up to 100,000 contacts.

**What makes Brevo good for small teams:**

-   **Triggers:** Custom events from the Brevo tracker or REST API, website activity, and form submissions.
-   **Builder:** Automations with conditional splits and percentage splits. More than two branches needs the Professional plan.
-   **Channels:** Email, SMS, and WhatsApp. Mobile push is on Professional and Enterprise.
-   **Transactional:** A transactional email API in the same product.
-   **Pricing:** Free at 300 emails a day. Paid plans from $9 a month.

**Not ideal if:** you need US data hosting, Slack steps, or product analytics on the person.

**Alternatives to consider:** **Mailchimp** for consumer templates and integrations, **PostHog** for event-triggered campaigns on your own data.

### What's the best Customer.io alternative for consumer brands?

**Mailchimp** is the pick for consumer and ecommerce teams that want templates, a huge integration catalog, and SMS on the same plan.

**What makes Mailchimp good for consumer brands:**

-   **Triggers:** Customer Journeys start from an API event, a form, a purchase, or a date.
-   **Builder:** Conditional splits and 50/50 or percentage splits on the Standard plan and up.
-   **Channels:** Email, with SMS as an add-on. Transactional email is a separate product, Mailchimp Transactional, from $20 per 25,000 emails on Standard and up.
-   **Pricing:** Free for 250 contacts and 500 emails a month, with no flows or scheduling. Essentials is $13 a month for the first 12 months, with 4 flow steps. Standard is $20 a month for the first 12 months, with 200 flows.

**Not ideal if:** you are a product team triggering on in-app behavior, or you want conversion goals per campaign without wiring up Google Analytics.

**Alternatives to consider:** **Brevo** for a cheaper plan with transactional built in, **PostHog** for campaigns on product events.

## Other frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How is PostHog different from Customer.io?</summary>

Customer.io needs your product data copied in before a campaign can use it, through its SDK, its Data Pipelines product, or a CDP. [PostHog Email & Messaging](/messaging) runs on the events, cohorts, and person properties already in your PostHog project, so there is nothing to sync. And because every send is an event on the person, you can put a campaign in a funnel with what people did after it, or watch the replay.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I move my Customer.io opt-out lists?</summary>

Yes. PostHog has an [importer for Customer.io opt-outs](/docs/workflows/import-customerio-optouts) and keeps the lists in sync with a reporting webhook, so nobody hears from you twice after you switch.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which Customer.io alternatives have free tiers?</summary>

**PostHog** includes 10,000 emails a month. **Brevo** allows 300 emails a day. **Loops** covers your first 1,000 contacts with 4,000 sends per 30 days. **Mailchimp** allows 250 contacts and 500 emails a month, with no flows. **Customer.io** offers a 14-day trial, and **Braze** and **Iterable** have no free plan.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which Customer.io alternatives have public pricing?</summary>

**PostHog**, **Loops**, **Brevo**, and **Mailchimp** publish self-serve prices. **Customer.io** publishes its Essentials and Premium plans. **Braze** and **Iterable** are contract only.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which Customer.io alternatives can an AI agent set up?</summary>

**PostHog**, **Customer.io**, and **Loops** have official MCP servers that create campaigns. **Braze** and **Iterable** have MCP servers in beta. **Brevo** covers email campaigns over MCP. **Mailchimp** has a docs-search MCP only.

</details>
