  Every product has one action that is the whole point: checkout, publish, send, deploy. When it
  fails loudly, you hear about it. This watches for the failure you never hear about – the user
  clicks, something half-happens, and they leave without telling you.

  speaksUp: Completions fall below their recent norm while attempts hold steady.

    Steady demand with falling success is the shape of a silent failure. Separating the two is
    what keeps this from crying wolf every time traffic dips.

  schedule: Daily
  body: |
    # Silent failure in the core action

    Watch the product's core action for failures that never surface as errors.

    ## Discriminator

    Speak up when completions of the core action fall below their recent norm **while attempts
    hold steady**. Stay quiet when attempts and completions fall together – that is a traffic
    change, not breakage.

    ## Explore

    1. Compare the attempt and completion event counts for the core action against the trailing
       two-week norm, bucketed daily.
    2. If completions diverged, check for a deploy in the window.
    3. Look for new or spiking error-tracking issues on the same code path, including handled
       exceptions.
    4. Check session replays of failed attempts for stalls, rage clicks, or abandonment.

    ## Disqualifiers

    - Both attempts and completions moved together.
    - The change is within normal weekday/weekend variance.
    - A known, announced maintenance window covers the period.
