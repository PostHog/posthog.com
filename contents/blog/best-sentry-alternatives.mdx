[Sentry](/blog/posthog-vs-sentry) is one of the most widely-used error tracking tools on the market, and for good reason. It's mature, reliable, and most developers have used it at some point.

But Sentry isn't the only option. Whether you're looking for deeper product context, a more generous free tier, or a tool that combines error tracking with analytics and session replay, there are [strong alternatives worth considering](/compare/best-error-tracking-tools).

In this guide, we compare the best Sentry alternatives, including tools that go beyond traditional error monitoring to help you understand *who* is affected and *why* it matters.

Here's the short version:

## 1. PostHog

- **Founded:** 2020
- **Who it's for:** Developers, AI product builders, and startups or scaleups who want error tracking to be part of understanding their product rather than a separate tool. 
- **Who it isn't for:** SRE teams who need deep APM, profiling, and infrastructure monitoring; Sentry, Datadog, or New Relic will serve them better.

![PostHog](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/Clean_Shot_2025_02_24_at_11_05_43_2x_4bd442ec11.png)

### What is PostHog?

[PostHog](/) (that's us 👋) is an all-in-one developer platform combining [error tracking](/error-tracking), [session replays](/session-replay), [product analytics](/product-analytics), [web analytics](/web-analytics), [A/B testing](/experiments), [feature flags](/feature-flags), [logs](/logs), [AI observability](/ai-observability), and a lot more into one product. 

This means it's not only an alternative to [Sentry](/blog/posthog-vs-sentry) but also tools like [Amplitude](/blog/posthog-vs-amplitude), [LaunchDarkly](/compare/posthog-vs-launchdarkly), and [Langfuse](/compare/posthog-vs-langfuse).

PostHog acts as your product's context layer: an error shows up with the replay, flags, logs, and user behind it already attached, and the built-in [data warehouse](/context-warehouse) adds your Stripe, HubSpot, or Postgres data alongside it. You can query all of it with SQL or hand it to an agent from [Slack](/slack) or your editor via [the MCP](/mcp).

### Key features

- **Error tracking:** [Capture errors and exceptions](/docs/error-tracking/installation) with stack traces and source maps, group them into issues you can assign and resolve, tag them with the release that shipped them, and alert Slack, Discord, Teams, or a webhook. 

- **Session replays:** View exactly how users are using your site. Includes event timelines, console logs, network activity, and performance monitoring, with retention from 30 days on the free plan to a year on Boost and Scale. [Replay Vision](/replay-vision) runs AI scanners across recordings so you don't have to watch them one by one.

- **Product analytics:** Custom trends, funnels, user paths, retention analysis, and segment user cohorts. Also, direct [SQL querying](/docs/sql) for power users.

- **Feature flags:** Rollout features safely with [local evaluation](/docs/feature-flags/local-evaluation) (for faster performance), JSON payloads, and instant rollbacks.

- **AI observability:** Capture every LLM generation and trace with its cost, latency, tokens, and errors, tied to the user and session that triggered it. Run evals on production traffic and A/B test prompts or models behind a flag. 

- **Data warehouse and CDP:** Sync Stripe, HubSpot, Postgres, and other sources into the [data warehouse](/context-warehouse) and query them alongside your product data, then push events back out to your tools with [CDP destinations](/cdp) or batch exports.

## 2. Rollbar

- **Founded:** 2012
- **Who it's for:** Small teams that want focused error tracking with AI fixes and nothing else to configure
- **Who it isn't for:** Teams that need performance monitoring, log management, or a self-hosted option.

![Rollbar](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/rollbar_c04782ffbc.png)

### What is Rollbar?

[Rollbar](/compare/best-rollbar-alternatives) helps teams find and fix errors in their code fast through real-time error tracking, issue management, and AI root cause analysis. In 2025 it added session replay, and in 2026 it launched Resolve, an agent that writes a fix, runs the tests, and opens a PR. Their whole platform is designed to help teams quickly detect, prioritize, and resolve issues in their applications.

### Key features

- **Error tracking:** Real-time error detection with automatic grouping and issue tracking.

- **Alerting:** Customizable alerting with notifications where you need them.

- **Error context:** Detailed error context including stack traces, code, and telemetry.

- **Custom workflows**: Use Rollbar's rules engine to trigger API or webhook-based actions.

- **Session replay and AI fixes:** Replays attached to errors (1,000 a month free), plus AI root cause analysis and the Resolve fix agent, both billed through AI credits that paid plans include.

## 3. Raygun

- **Founded:** 2007
- **Who it's for:** .NET-heavy teams, and ecommerce or media sites, that want crash reporting, real user monitoring, and APM from one vendor with AI error resolution included.
- **Who it isn't for:** Small projects, since there's no free tier and Crash Reporting starts at $40 a month billed annually, or teams outside .NET, Node.js, and Ruby who want APM.

![Raygun](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/raygun2_1f01c82ebe.png)

### What is Raygun?

**Raygun** is a full-stack monitoring platform focused on ecommerce and media companies (if you got here searching for the Australian B-girl: wrong Raygun, but if your app ever does the kangaroo in production, this one will catch it).

It provides crash reporting, performance monitoring, and user experience insights. Its suite of tools enables teams to quickly detect, diagnose, and resolve issues across their entire tech stack. It's also built an AI error resolution system to help with this.

#### Key features

- **Error tracking:** Real-time error and crash reporting with detailed stack traces and code-level insights.

- **Performance monitoring:** Get insights into your app's performance for your users. Track metrics like Core Web Vitals and load times.

- **Application performance monitoring:** Uncover backend performance issues with tracing and visualizations.

- **AI error resolution:** Raygun's suite of tools has AI error resolution built in to make all of this easier.

## 4. Datadog

- **Founded:** 2010
- **Who it's for:** Enterprises already running Datadog for infrastructure, APM, and logs who want errors in the same place, with the backend trace a click away.
- **Who it isn't for:** Small teams; the free plan is 5 hosts with 1-day retention, Error Tracking is a separate line item, and there's no self-hosted option.

![Datadog](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/datadog_db7bb8e269.jpeg)

### What is Datadog?

[Datadog](/compare/best-datadog-alternatives) is a cloud observability and monitoring platform that provides real-time visibility into infrastructure, applications, logs, and network performance. It enables organizations to ensure performance, security, and reliability across their entire stack.

#### Key features

- **Performance monitoring:** Monitor your app (and infrastructure) performance across your stack, from frontend to workflows to databases. Troubleshoot issues with distributed tracing.

- **Log management:** Aggregate and analyze logs in real-time to correlate events, identify issues, and gain insight into system performance.

- **Infrastructure monitoring:** Get visibility into your infrastructure's performance, health, and security across your cloud or hybrid environment.

- **Security and compliance monitoring:** Ensure your data is secure and compliant with industry standards. Protect against reported vulnerabilities.

- **Integrations:** 1,000+ integrations covering nearly every cloud service, database, and framework you're likely to run.

## 7. AppSignal

- **Founded:** 2012

### What is AppSignal?

**AppSignal** is an application performance monitoring (APM) and error tracking tool designed for developers. It offers insight into errors, performance issues, host metrics, uptime monitoring, and logs in one unified platform. It's known for its ease of setup, developer-friendly experience, and real-time alerting.

#### Key features

- **Error tracking**: Capture exceptions, get alerts, and debug with full request details for your favorite languages like Ruby, Elixir, Python, and more.

- **Performance monitoring**: Track response times, slow queries, background jobs, and memory leaks. Find and fix bottlenecks.

- **Host monitoring**: Monitor CPU, disk, network, and memory usage across your infrastructure. Compare hosts to see which ones are underperforming.

- **Anomaly detection**: Get real-time alerts for any metric or unusual patterns in app behavior from any of the above sources.

- **Log management**: Store and search logs with a clean and intuitive UI. Ingest from any source.

## 8. GlitchTip
 
- **Founded:** 2020
- **Who it's for:** Teams leaving Sentry over cost or licensing who want to keep their SDKs and self-host, or pay $15 a month for the hosted version.
- **Who it isn't for:** Teams that want session replay, profiling, AI root cause analysis, or the user context around an error.

### What is GlitchTip?
 
**GlitchTip** is an open-source (MIT) reimplementation of Sentry's backend, built by Burke Software. It's compatible with Sentry's SDKs, so you point the Sentry client you already use at a GlitchTip DSN and keep going. You can self-host it with Docker Compose or Helm on as little as 512 MB of RAM, or use the hosted version, which has EU hosting on every plan.
 
It covers the basics well and skips the rest: there's no session replay, profiling, or AI root cause analysis, and alerts are email and webhooks only.
 
#### Key features
 
- **Error tracking:** Exceptions, log messages, and CSP violations, grouped into issues you can comment on, assign, and resolve. Source maps upload through its CLI.

- **Uptime monitoring:** Ping, GET, POST, and heartbeat checks with alerts when a service goes down.

- **Performance monitoring:** Transactions and spans with p50 and p95 timings, captured through the Sentry SDK's tracing options.

- **Logs:** Added in 2026, with native OpenTelemetry log ingest, alongside an MCP server for agents.
