'Do more weird' is an actual PostHog [company value](/handbook/values#do-more-weird), and we have a discretionary $45k/mo budget that anyone can use to bring weird ideas to life. 'Operationalizing weirdness' sounds like an oxymoron that some soul-sucking MBA came up with (hey), but there are a ton of ways you can practically inject more weirdness into your company. 

Let's get weird. 

## Why we do more weird in the first place

- Size vs. much larger companies with more money
- Competing for cat videos
- The average person is actually really weird

## How to do it wrong

- Make it the marketing team's responsibility
- Don't actually support it from founder down
- Corporate try hard
- In jokes

## How to do it right

- Channel
- Monthly meeting and decision makers
- Budget
- Remember weirdness is relative
- Remember it's a numbers game. 

## What to do first

Start a `#do-more-weird` Slack channel. Add a diverse group of people to it who are interested in this stuff. Then copy our [playbook](/handbook/company/do-more-weird). That's it.
