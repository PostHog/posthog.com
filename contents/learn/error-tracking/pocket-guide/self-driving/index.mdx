# Self-driving

Now that Twig's engineers see reliable, high-quality exceptions flow into Error Tracking, they're ready to take the next step to let PostHog self-drive fixes.

Twig's engineers turn on a troop of <Term name="scout">scouts</Term>. Scouts are agents that run on a schedule to look for specific issues. They stay silent unless they find something to act on.

They will automatically look for issues, assess their impact, create a <Term name="report" /> with the impact and the fix, and even open pull requests to help the engineers resolve them.

This chapter covers these examples of how Twig self-drives with Error Tracking:

- [Catch error spikes and stuck loops](/learn/error-tracking/pocket-guide/spikes-and-stuck-loops) ranks spikes by how many guests they affect.
- [Find the errors Twig's paying hosts hit](/learn/error-tracking/pocket-guide/errors-in-paying-hosts) reports issues concentrated in hosts who pay.
- [Connect a deploy to an error burst](/learn/error-tracking/pocket-guide/deploy-regressions) ties a new issue to the pull request that likely caused it.
- [Check whether the fix held](/learn/error-tracking/pocket-guide/verify-the-fix) reports fixes that didn't stop the error.
