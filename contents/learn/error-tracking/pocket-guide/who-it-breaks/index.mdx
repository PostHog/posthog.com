# Find the errors your paying accounts hit

This creates a <Term name="scout" />, which is a scheduled agent that queries your PostHog events and files a <Term name="report" /> in your <Term name="inbox" /> when it finds something.

This one reports <Term name="issue">issues</Term> concentrated in your paying accounts. The issue list sorts by occurrences, users, or sessions, so an issue four paying accounts keep hitting can rank below a free account's retry loop.

## What the scout looks at

`TypeError: Cannot read properties of null` fired 2,340 times this week across 31 accounts. Four of them pay you and send 9% of your traffic, but they carry 71% of the issue, so the scout reports it <SeeFig n={1} />. An issue spread evenly across your accounts gets no report.

The scout also skips issues driven by one client retrying. The [spikes and stuck loops](/learn/error-tracking/pocket-guide/errors-as-signals) scout covers those.

## The report in your inbox

The <Term name="report" /> names the issue and the accounts, with each account's share of the issue next to its share of your traffic <SeeFig n={2} />. Click **Ask AI** on the report to ask who else is affected, whether the problem is new, or what to do next. See [reports](/docs/self-driving/reports#ask-ai-about-a-report).

## The scout itself

This is a custom scout, and its file is in the [Self-driving pocket guide](/docs/self-driving/pocket-guide/errors-your-best-accounts-hit). With [group analytics](/docs/product-analytics/group-analytics), each `$exception` <Term name="event" /> carries the account that sent it. The scout compares each account's share of an issue with its share of your traffic over the last seven days. It checks which accounts pay you by their [Stripe](/docs/data-pipelines/sources/stripe) customer ID in Customer analytics. It only reports issues that fired in the last 24 hours. [Measure impact](/learn/error-tracking/pocket-guide/measure-impact#revenue-at-risk) shows the same join.

## The schedule it runs on

The scout runs every 24 hours by default, but stays quiet unless something needs your attention. You can change the schedule in its **Settings**.

## Add this to your scout troop

The scout needs exceptions reaching PostHog, [group analytics](/docs/product-analytics/group-analytics) set up, and your accounts in Customer analytics with a [Stripe](/docs/data-pipelines/sources/stripe) customer ID on each account that pays you. The **Add this scout** button in the Self-driving pocket guide opens PostHog with the scout prefilled for you to review.
