# Catch error spikes and stuck loops

This creates a <Term name="scout" />, which is a scheduled agent that queries your PostHog events and files a <Term name="report" /> in your <Term name="inbox" /> when it finds something.

This one reports error spikes and ranks them by how many people they affect. Sorted by occurrences alone, a few clients stuck in a retry loop can rank above a bug that breaks checkout for hundreds of people.

## What the scout looks at

The scout reports an issue when its exceptions jump well above the issue's own baseline. It sets the priority by comparing exceptions with distinct users:

- **Both climb.** Many people hit a regression. The report is P1, or P2 when the reach is moderate.
- **Exceptions climb, users stay flat.** A few clients are stuck in a loop <SeeFig n={1} />. The report is P3, or P2 when it's fresh and in the thousands.
- **A resolved issue fires again.** The report is P1.

The scout files new issues with overlapping stack traces as one report, and skips one-off exceptions and known upstream errors, like rate limits from your LLM provider.

## The report in your inbox

Each spike becomes a <Term name="report" /> in your <Term name="inbox" />, with exceptions, distinct users, and an hourly chart against the issue's baseline <SeeFig n={2} />. Click **Ask AI** on the report to ask who else is affected, whether the problem is new, or what to do next. See [reports](/docs/self-driving/reports#ask-ai-about-a-report).

## The scout itself

PostHog maintains this scout, listed as **Error tracking** in your scouts, and you can read [its skill file on GitHub](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog/blob/master/products/signals/skills/signals-scout-error-tracking/SKILL.md). Each run queries your issues and `$exception` events through the [PostHog MCP](/learn/error-tracking/pocket-guide/exceptions-as-events#posthog-mcp), and checks its notes and your inbox so it doesn't report the same spike twice.

## The schedule it runs on

The scout runs every 24 hours by default, but stays quiet unless something needs your attention. You can change the schedule in its **Settings**.

## Add this to your scout troop

You can turn this scout on or off for each project. It needs exceptions reaching PostHog.
