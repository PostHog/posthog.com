# Catch error spikes and stuck loops

This <Term name="scout" /> reports error spikes and ranks them by how many guests they affect. A bug that breaks booking for hundreds of guests ranks above a few browsers stuck in a retry loop.

## What the scout looks at

The scout reports an issue when its exceptions jump well above the issue's own baseline. It sets the priority by comparing exceptions with distinct users:

- **Both climb.** Many guests hit a regression. The report is P1, or P2 when the reach is moderate.
- **Exceptions climb, users stay flat.** A few browsers are stuck in a loop <SeeFig n={1} />. The report is P3, or P2 when it's fresh and in the thousands.
- **A resolved issue fires again.** The report is P1.

The scout files new issues with overlapping stack traces as one report, and skips one-off exceptions and known upstream errors.

## The report in your inbox

Each spike becomes a <Term name="report" /> in the team's <Term name="inbox" />, with exceptions, distinct users, and an hourly chart against the issue's baseline <SeeFig n={2} />. From the report, the engineers can [ask AI](/docs/self-driving/reports#ask-ai-about-a-report) who else is affected or what to do next.

## The scout itself

PostHog maintains this scout, listed as **Error tracking** in the project's scouts. Its [skill file](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog/blob/master/products/signals/skills/signals-scout-error-tracking/SKILL.md) is on GitHub. Each run queries Twig's issues and `$exception` events through the [PostHog MCP](/learn/error-tracking/agent-prompts-and-mcp), and checks its notes and the team's inbox so it doesn't report the same spike twice. It runs every 24 hours by default and stays quiet unless something needs the team's attention.

## Add this to your scout troop

The engineers turn it on for each project. It needs exceptions reaching PostHog.
