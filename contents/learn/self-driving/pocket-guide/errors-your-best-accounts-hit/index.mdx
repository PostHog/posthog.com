# Find the errors your paying accounts hit

This scout reports <Term name="issue">issues</Term> concentrated in your paying accounts. The issue list sorts by occurrences, users, or sessions, so an issue four paying accounts keep hitting can rank below a free account's retry loop.

## What the scout looks at

`TypeError: Cannot read properties of null` fired 2,340 times this week across 31 accounts. Four of them pay you and send 9% of your traffic, but they carry 71% of the issue, so the scout reports it. An issue spread evenly across your accounts gets no report, and neither does an issue driven by one client retrying.

## The report in your inbox

The <Term name="report" /> names the issue and the accounts, with each account's share of the issue next to its share of your traffic <SeeFig n={1} />. Click **Ask AI** on the report to ask who else is affected, whether the problem is new, or what to do next.

## The scout itself

With [group analytics](/docs/product-analytics/group-analytics), each `$exception` event carries the account that sent it. The scout compares each account's share of an issue with its share of your traffic over the last seven days <SeeFig n={2} />. It checks which accounts pay you by their [Stripe](/docs/data-pipelines/sources/stripe) customer ID in Customer analytics, and it only reports issues that fired in the last 24 hours.
