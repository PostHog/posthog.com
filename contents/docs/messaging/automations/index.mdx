An automation in Messaging is a **workflow**: a trigger, the steps that follow it, and an exit. A workflow reacts to your product data, messages the right people, and sends the results back to PostHog as events.

- **React to product data.** Start a workflow from any PostHog event, a batch audience, a webhook, a Slack message, or a schedule. Filter and split on the event, person, and group properties you already capture.
- **Message people.** Send email, push, Slack, or SMS, or call a webhook or any [real-time destination](/docs/data-pipelines/destinations). Messages use your [templates](/docs/messaging/library) and respect [opt-outs](/docs/messaging/opt-outs).
- **Send results back as events.** Sends, opens, clicks, and [conversions](/docs/messaging/engagement-events) come back as events on the person. A **Capture event** step records anything else, so you can measure a workflow in insights, funnels, and cohorts.

To send one email to many people with no other steps, a [broadcast](/docs/messaging/broadcasts) is quicker. For a full example, see [build an email drip campaign](/docs/messaging/email-drip-campaign).

## Launch your first workflow

This example sends an email to people who perform an event in your app or site.

<Steps>

<Step title="Set up a channel" badge="required">

To send email, [verify a sending domain](/docs/messaging/verify-domain) first. To send to Slack or SMS, [connect a channel](/docs/messaging/automations/workflow-builder#connect-slack-and-sms). Webhooks and other destinations need no channel.

</Step>

<Step title="Create a workflow" badge="required">

Go to [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows) and click **+ New workflow**. A new workflow has a trigger step and an exit step. The panel on the right lists the steps you can add.

<ProductScreenshot
  imageLight="https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/w_1600,c_limit,q_auto,f_auto/workflows_list_light_c6075ccaf8.png"
  imageDark="https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/w_1600,c_limit,q_auto,f_auto/workflows_list_dark_0d5c406369.png"
  classes="rounded"
  alt="Click the New Workflow button"
/>

</Step>

<Step title="Configure the trigger" badge="required">

Click the trigger step, then click **+ Add trigger event** and pick the event that starts the workflow, such as `user signed up`. Set **Frequency** to **One time** so a person enters the workflow only once. See [triggers](/docs/messaging/automations/workflow-builder#triggers) for the other trigger types.

  imageDark="https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/q_auto,f_auto/workflows_trigger_panel_dark_bf54d41ffd.png"
  classes="rounded"
  alt="Trigger warning that event is required"
/>

</Step>

<Step title="Add a message step" badge="required">

Drag an **Email** step below the trigger and click it. Pick a **From** address, and write the subject and body, or start from a [template](/docs/messaging/library). **To** defaults to the person who triggered the workflow. While you test, put your own address in **To**.

<ProductScreenshot
  imageLight="https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/w_1600,c_limit,q_auto,f_auto/workflows_email_step_light_ee7fc76477.png"

  classes="rounded"
  alt="Configure email template"
/>

To send to Slack, SMS, or a webhook instead, see [dispatches](/docs/messaging/automations/workflow-builder#dispatches).

</Step>

<Step checkpoint title="Test the workflow" subtitle="Confirm you can trigger the workflow">

Click **Test**. PostHog makes an example trigger event, or click **Load new event** to use a real one. With **Make a real request to PostHog** off, sends are mocked and logged. With it on, real messages go out. Click **Run test**, and the path the run takes lights up green.

<ProductScreenshot
  imageLight="https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/w_1600,c_limit,q_auto,f_auto/workflows_test_light_1d317768fd.png"
  imageDark="https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/w_1600,c_limit,q_auto,f_auto/workflows_test_dark_516c970a99.png"
  classes="rounded"
  alt="Test workflow"
/>

Test sends don't appear in the **Metrics** tab.

</Step>

<Step title="Enable the workflow" badge="required">

You can save a draft at any time, even when it is incomplete. PostHog checks the workflow only when you click **Enable**. Once enabled, the workflow runs each time the trigger fires.

</Step>

<Step checkpoint title="Monitor the workflow" subtitle="Confirm messages are being delivered">

The **Metrics** tab shows how many runs were triggered and succeeded, and conversions if you set a [conversion goal](/docs/messaging/automations/workflow-builder#conversion-goals). The **Logs** tab shows each run and any errors. If something is wrong, see [troubleshooting](/docs/messaging/automations/troubleshooting).

</Step>

</Steps>
