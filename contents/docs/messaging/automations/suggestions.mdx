Self-driving looks at how the emails in a live workflow perform, and suggests changes to the ones that underperform. For example, if a step has a low open rate and a long subject line, it suggests a shorter subject line. You review each suggestion before anything changes.

Self-driving is in beta.

## Turn it on

1. Open a workflow and go to its **Self-driving** tab.
2. Turn on **Suggest improvements**. The same switch is also in the workflow's menu.

To turn it on, you need:

- **Editor access** to the workflow.
- **A live workflow** with at least one published email step.
- **AI data processing approved** for your organization. If the **Self-driving** tab does not show, ask an organization admin to approve it in your organization settings.

The first workflow you turn on in a project also turns on the workflows [scout](/docs/self-driving/scouts) for that project. This needs editor access to skills and a free scout slot in the project.

## How it works

Once a day, self-driving reads the email metrics of each workflow that has it turned on. It files at most one suggestion per workflow each day.

It only looks at an email step when:

- The step has at least 20 tracked sends in the last 7 days.
- The current version of the workflow has been live for at least 2 days.
- The step does not have a high bounce or complaint rate. Fix deliverability first. See [sending reputation](/docs/messaging/sending-reputation).

Self-driving does not review transactional emails, and it only reviews email steps for now.

## Review a suggestion

When there is a suggestion, the workflow shows a "PostHog suggests a change to this workflow" banner, and the workflows list shows a count next to it.

On the **Self-driving** tab, each suggestion under **Waiting for you** shows:

- A before and after of each field it wants to change.
- The evidence that led to it, such as the open or click rate of the step.

You can:

- **Approve** a suggestion. This saves the change as a draft. Nothing goes out until you [publish the workflow](/docs/messaging/automations/editing-live-workflows).
- **Reject** a suggestion, with an optional reason. Self-driving does not suggest the same change again.

After you publish an approved change, the **Applied** section compares the results before and after the change.
