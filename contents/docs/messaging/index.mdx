Messaging sends email and push notifications to your users from the events and person data already in PostHog. It does three jobs: transactional email that your code sends to one person, broadcasts to an audience, and automations that react to what each person does, such as a drip campaign. Every send, open, and click comes back as an event on the person who got it.

Because it runs on the same events and person properties you already send to PostHog, you act on real product behavior instead of a separate marketing dataset. To automate more than messages, such as Slack alerts, webhooks, and AI tasks, use [automations](/docs/messaging/automations).

## Which one do you need?

| You want to | Use | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send one email to one person when your code says so, such as a password reset, a sign-in code, a receipt, or an invite | Transactional email | [Send transactional emails](/docs/messaging/transactional-emails) |
| Send one email to many people, once or on a schedule, such as a product update, a changelog, or an event invite | Broadcasts | [Send a broadcast](/docs/messaging/broadcasts) |
| Send a series of emails that react to what each person does, such as an onboarding sequence or a re-engagement email | Automations | [Build an email drip campaign](/docs/messaging/email-drip-campaign) |

You build [automations](/docs/messaging/automations) in the drag-and-drop [workflow builder](/docs/messaging/automations/workflow-builder). A drip campaign is one kind of automation. You decide when an automation is triggered, who it reaches, and what happens at each step: triggers, delays, audience splits, message sends, and PostHog actions.

## Where you can use it

Send transactional email from your own code. Build broadcasts and automations in the PostHog app, or have an agent build them for you. Script any of it over the API.

## Where its data comes from

A message is only as good as what it can trigger on and who it can reach. Three things feed it: the events and person data already in your project, the senders you set up, and the templates and opt-out rules that decide what gets sent to whom.
