The fastest way to start is to ask your coding agent. Install the [PostHog AI plugin](https://github.com/PostHog/ai-plugin), which connects the [PostHog MCP server](/docs/messaging/mcp) and adds the [Messaging skills](/docs/messaging/skills). Then ask:

```
Send me a test email from PostHog, then add a welcome email to our app that goes out when someone signs up.
```

Your agent sets up a sender, writes the email in your code, adds the send, and can build broadcasts and automations for you. It asks before it sends anything to real people.

<QuestLogItem
  title="Send your first email"
  subtitle="Recommended"
  icon="IconCode"
>

Transactional emails are the one-off emails your code sends to one person, such as password resets, sign-in codes, and receipts. You write each one as code, and your backend sends it with a [server SDK](/docs/messaging/installation#send-email-from-your-backend) or the API.

You don't need to set up DNS first. The [sandbox sender](/docs/messaging/sandbox-sender) sends from a PostHog address to members of your PostHog organization, so you can try it now.

To add your first send to your app, run the PostHog wizard in your project:

```bash
npx @posthog/wizard@latest email
```

It writes the email in your code, adds a project secret API key to your env file, and adds the send with the SDK for your backend. To do it by hand:

```js
await posthog.emails.send({
  to: "you@example.com",
  templateKey: "welcome",
  subject: "Welcome",
  html: "<p>Hi Ada</p>",
  idempotencyKey: "my-first-email",
})
```

<QuestLogItem
  title="Send a broadcast or build an automation"
  subtitle="Recommended"
  icon="IconDecisionTree"
>

A [broadcast](/docs/messaging/broadcasts) sends one email to many people, once or on a schedule, such as a product update. Choose the recipients with person properties and cohorts, write the email, and schedule it.

An [automation](/docs/messaging/automations) reacts to what each person does. For example, a drip campaign sends a welcome email at sign-up and a follow-up a few days later if the person has not finished onboarding. To trigger it from product events, [install a PostHog SDK](/docs/messaging/installation#trigger-broadcasts-and-automations-from-events) in your app.

<QuestLogItem

Each workflow, broadcast, and transactional email shows its sends, deliveries, opens, clicks, and bounces. Turn on engagement events, and PostHog also captures each send, open, and click as an event on the person who got it. Use them in insights, funnels, and dashboards, next to your product events.

<QuestLogItem
  title="Verify your domain"
  subtitle="Before you email your users"
  icon="IconLetter"
>

The sandbox sender only delivers to members of your PostHog organization. Before you email your users, add your own sender on the [**Channels** tab](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/channels) and verify its domain. You add a few DNS records, and verification usually takes a few minutes.
