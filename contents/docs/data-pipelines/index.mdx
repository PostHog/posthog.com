Data pipelines is how you move data in and out of PostHog. It works with both your PostHog data – events, persons, sessions, and groups – and data from external tools like Stripe, HubSpot, or your own Postgres database.

It's made up of three parts:

- **[Sources](/docs/data-pipelines/sources)** sync data from external tools and databases into PostHog, so you can query it alongside your product data.
- **[Transformations](/docs/data-pipelines/transformations)** add, edit, or drop event properties as events are ingested.
- **[Destinations](/docs/data-pipelines/destinations)** send data out of PostHog – events to tools like Slack or HubSpot in realtime, [batch exports](/docs/data-pipelines/batch-exports) of your PostHog data to a warehouse, or synced source data written to [your own database](/docs/data-pipelines/destinations/elt-destinations).

Realtime destinations and transformations are built from [Hog functions](/docs/hog), which you can configure from a template, write yourself, or have [PostHog AI](/docs/data-pipelines/hog-functions-ai) write for you.

## What's included

Data pipelines covers the data already in your PostHog project and anything you connect from outside it. Sources bring external data in, transformations reshape events during ingestion, and destinations send both PostHog and source data back out.

## Common use cases

Teams most often use data pipelines to bring revenue and CRM data from tools like [Stripe](/docs/data-pipelines/sources/stripe) and [HubSpot](/docs/data-pipelines/sources/hubspot) into PostHog, to keep a warehouse in sync with product data, to push events into tools like [Slack](/docs/data-pipelines/destinations/slack), [HubSpot](/docs/data-pipelines/destinations/hubspot), or [Intercom](/docs/data-pipelines/destinations/intercom) over [webhooks](/docs/data-pipelines/destinations/webhook), and to enforce a schema at ingestion so bad events never make it into analytics.

Transformations also help you label events on the way in – setting a property like `user_tier` or `page_category` – which makes downstream metrics and [SQL queries](/docs/data-warehouse/sql) much easier to write.
