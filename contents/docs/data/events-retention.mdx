---
title: Events data retention
---

PostHog Cloud keeps your events for a retention period, then deletes them. This page covers the default retention period for each plan and how to set a shorter custom retention period.

Some data follows different retention rules:

- [Session recordings](/docs/session-replay/recording-retention)  
- [AI events](/docs/ai-observability/data-retention)  
- [Logs](/docs/logs/pricing#retention)  
- [Data warehouse](/docs/data-warehouse) tables, which keep all of their rows  
- Person profiles and properties, which are stored indefinitely until you [delete them](/docs/privacy/data-storage#data-deletion)  
- Self-hosted instances, which keep events until you delete them

## Retention by plan

| Plan | Default retention period | Custom retention range |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Free | 1 year | Not available |
| Pay-as-you-go | 7 years | Up to 84 months (7 years) |

After your default retention period ends, events beyond this period may be deleted.

## Custom retention

On pay-as-you-go, you can set a custom retention period that's shorter than the 7-year default. Common reasons include:

- Meeting compliance requirements  
- Data minimization, so you keep only the data you need  
- Faster dashboards, since “all-time” queries scan less data

PostHog deletes events that are older than your custom retention period during a monthly deletion process.

### To set a custom retention policy for your organization

Go to [**Settings \> Organization \> General \> Events retention**](https://app.posthog.com/settings/organization-details#organization-events-retention). This policy applies to every project that doesn't have its own.

### To set a custom retention policy for one project

Go to [**Settings \> Project \> Product analytics \> Events retention**](https://app.posthog.com/settings/environment-product-analytics#project-events-retention). This policy overrides the organization's.

If you remove a project's policy, the project uses the organization's policy. If the organization doesn't have one, the project uses your plan's default retention policy.

### Who can change a retention policy

- Only organization admins can set or remove a custom retention policy. Project admins and members can view it but not change it.  
- Each setting shows the range of months your plan allows.  
- PostHog asks you to confirm before it saves or removes a custom retention policy.

## How retention works

Retention applies to every project on PostHog Cloud. Each project uses its own custom retention period if it has one. If not, it uses the organization's period, and if there isn't one, your plan's default.

- PostHog checks each event's `timestamp` against the retention period, not the time PostHog received the event. This way the event's real age decides when it expires.  
- You can only query events whose timestamp is inside your retention period.  
- Events that are older than the retention period may be deleted at any point. If you have a custom retention policy in place, they will be deleted during the next monhtly deletion process.

Retention uses each event's `timestamp`, so events you bring in through a [migration](/docs/migrate) are deleted at the next cleanup if they're older than your retention period, even though you only just migrated them. Check your retention period before you migrate.

## FAQ

#### Which events does retention apply to?

Every event in the events table, including error tracking exceptions, feature flag evaluations, survey responses and MCP analytics events.

#### Does the events data retention period apply to person profiles?

No. Person profiles and their properties are stored indefinitely. To remove them, use the [data deletion tools](/docs/privacy/data-storage#data-deletion).

#### What happens to my retention period if I move to the Free plan?

The Free plan doesn't offer custom retention, so any custom retention period is removed and your projects go back to the 1-year default.

#### When are expired events deleted?

On default retention, events may be deleted at any point after your retention period ends. On a custom retention policy, events older than the retention period are deleted during the next monthly deletion process. Deleted events can't be restored.

#### If I extend my retention period, will I get my events back?
Events that are older than your retention period may be deleted, so there is no guarantee that your data can be restored. Any events that have been hidden from queries, but have yet to be deleted from our database, will become available to you.

## Other ways to manage your data

The events retention process only deletes events once they expire. To delete data sooner, use the [data deletion tools](/docs/privacy/data-storage#data-deletion):

- Delete a person, along with their events and recordings, to handle a [right to be forgotten](/docs/privacy/data-storage#right-to-be-forgotten) request.  
- Delete a project or an organization to remove all of its events.

You may also send events to your own storage with a [batch export](/docs/data-pipelines/batch-exports). Batch exports can only export events PostHog still has, so set one up before your events expire.
