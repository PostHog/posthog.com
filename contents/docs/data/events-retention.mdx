---
title: Events data retention
---

PostHog keeps your events for a period that your plan sets. This page tells you what that period is and what it does not do.

This page describes general data retention rules, with the following exceptions:
- [Session recording retention](/docs/session-replay/recording-retention)
- [AI events](/docs/ai-observability/data-retention) for AI Observability
- [Data deletion tools](/docs/privacy/data-storage#data-deletion)

## Your retention period

Your retention period comes from your plan:

| Plan | Events data retention |
|-----------|---------------|
| Free | 1 year
| Paid plans | 7 years

Retention applies on PostHog Cloud only. A self-hosted instance keeps your events until you remove them.

## What retention does to your data

When retention applies to your project:

- A query on the events table reads only the events inside the window. PostHog does not include older events in the result.
- An insight whose date range goes past the window shows a banner, so that you do not read a smaller number as a drop in your product.
- Tables in the [Data Warehouse](/docs/data-warehouse) keep all of their rows, because retention applies to the events table only.

## Removing data sooner

Retention deletes events only after they pass the window. To remove data sooner, use the tools in [controlling data storage](/docs/privacy/data-storage#data-deletion):

- Delete a person, together with their events and recordings, to answer a [right to be forgotten](/docs/privacy/data-storage#right-to-be-forgotten) request.
- Delete a project or an organization to remove all of the events in it.

To keep events for longer than your retention period, send them to your own storage with a [batch export](/docs/data-pipelines/batch-exports).

## Custom retention

Organization admins can choose a shorter window than their plan's. PostHog deletes events older than the window at the next monthly cleanup. Deleted events can't be restored.

### Organization window

Set a window for every project in your organization under [**Settings** > **Organization** > **General** > **Events retention**](https://app.posthog.com/settings/organization-details#organization-events-retention). It applies to every project that doesn't have its own window.

### Project window

Set a window for one project under [**Settings** > **Project** > **Product analytics** > **Events retention**](https://app.posthog.com/settings/environment-product-analytics#project-events-retention). The project then uses this window instead of the organization's.

If you remove a project's window, the project uses the organization's window. If the organization has no window either, the project uses the [retention period](#your-retention-period) from your plan.

### Who can change a window

- Only organization admins can change a window. Project admins and members can see it but not change it.
- Each setting shows the range of months you can choose from. A window can't be longer than your plan's.
- PostHog asks you to confirm before it saves or removes a window.
