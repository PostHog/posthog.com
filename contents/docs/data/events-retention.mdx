---
title: Events data retention
---

PostHog keeps your events for a period that your plan sets. This page tells you what that period is, where to read it, and what it does not do.

This page describes general data retention rules, with the following exceptions:
- [Session recording retention](/docs/session-replay/recording-retention)
- [AI events](/docs/ai-observability/data-retention) for AI Observability
- [Data deletion tools](/docs/privacy/data-storage#data-deletion)

## Your retention period

Your retention period comes from your plan:

| Plan | Events data retention |
|-----------|---------------|
| Free | 1 year
| Paid plans | 2 years

Two read-only fields on the [projects API](/docs/api/projects) report it:

- `event_retention_months` – the length of the window in months. A value of `24` is 2 years, and a value of `84` is 7 years.
- `events_retention_enforced` – whether the window applies to your project today.

<CalloutBox type="fyi" title="Enforcement is a gradual rollout">

PostHog applies retention project by project. While `events_retention_enforced` is `false`, retention removes no events from your project, whatever `event_retention_months` shows.

</CalloutBox>

Retention applies on PostHog Cloud only. A self-hosted instance keeps your events until you remove them.

### Organizations created before October 1, 2026

On October 1, 2026, PostHog changed the retention period on paid plans from 7 years to 2 years.

- If you created your organization before October 1, 2026, it keeps the 7-year period. PostHog calls this a grandfathered period.
- If you create your organization on or after October 1, 2026, it gets the 2-year period.

The grandfathered period applies to the organization, not to the project. A new project in a grandfathered organization also gets the 7-year period. Read `event_retention_months` on the [projects API](/docs/api/projects) to see which period your project has.

## What retention does to your data

When retention applies to your project:

- A query on the events table reads only the events inside the window. PostHog does not include older events in the result.
- An insight whose date range goes past the window shows a banner, so that you do not read a smaller number as a drop in your product.
- Tables in the [Data Warehouse](/docs/data-warehouse) keep all of their rows, because retention applies to the events table only.

## Retention is not a deletion tool

Retention controls what your queries can read. It does not promise that PostHog deletes your data.

After your retention period ends:

- Your queries no longer return the events. You cannot get them back through the PostHog UI or the API.
- PostHog can delete the events, but PostHog does not guarantee that it deletes them at the end of the period, or on any schedule that you can rely on.
- A copy of the events can stay in cold storage, in a backup, or in another internal system.

<CalloutBox type="caution" title="Do not use retention as a compliance control">

If you must show that PostHog deleted specific data, do not use the end of your retention period as evidence. Use a deletion tool, which gives you an action that you can request, monitor, and record. Examples of such a requirement are a data subject request under the GDPR or the CCPA, an audit, and a contractual deletion term.

</CalloutBox>

You also cannot make your retention period shorter to remove data, and a shorter period is not available on request.

## How to delete your data

To remove data from PostHog, use the tools in [data management](/docs/data) and [controlling data storage](/docs/privacy/data-storage#data-deletion):

- [Delete a person](/docs/privacy/data-storage#right-to-be-forgotten), together with their events and recordings, to answer a right to be forgotten request.
- [Delete a project or an organization](/docs/privacy/data-storage#data-deletion) to remove all of the events in it.
- [Filter or drop events before ingestion](/docs/data/event-filtering) to keep unwanted data out of PostHog.

For more about what PostHog stores and where, see [controlling data storage](/docs/privacy/data-storage), [GDPR compliance](/docs/privacy/gdpr-compliance), and [CCPA compliance](/docs/privacy/ccpa-compliance).

To keep events for longer than your retention period, send them to your own storage with a [batch export](/docs/cdp/batch-exports).
