Logs is an OpenTelemetry-native log store, so there are no PostHog-specific SDKs to adopt – point any OTLP client at PostHog and your log records start arriving. Search them by service, severity, and attribute, group similar lines into [patterns](/docs/logs/patterns) to see what changed, and open any record to have [PostHog AI](/docs/logs/explain-logs-ai) explain it.

Because your logs live next to the rest of your data, a log line is one click from the [session replay](/docs/session-replay), [person](/docs/data/persons), and [error](/docs/error-tracking) it belongs to.

## Where its data comes from

Everything in Logs is derived from the log records you send over [OTLP](/docs/logs/installation). How useful they are depends on what you attach to them.
