The PostHog app for Slack helps you stay connected to your product data and receive timely updates directly in your workspace.

## Features

The PostHog app for Slack provides three key capabilities:

<!-- 1. **Ask PostHog AI product data questions** – Mention @PostHog in your workspace to get answers about your product data right where you are. -->

1. **Subscribe to product insights and dashboards** – Receive regular digests of your key metrics in chosen Slack channels.

1. **Set up Slack notifications via PostHog Data Pipelines** – Get timely notifications of any event or action in PostHog.

1. **Rich link previews** – When you paste PostHog insight or dashboard URLs in Slack, they automatically unfurl to show the title, type, and description.

### Inbox notifications

Self-driving reports can post to Slack too. In the [inbox](/docs/self-driving/inbox), open the **Agents** tab:

- **Notify the whole team** posts every actionable report to one channel. PostHog must be in the channel, so invite it with `/invite @PostHog`.
- **Notify me directly** pings you in a channel of your choice when you're a suggested reviewer, with a minimum priority so low-priority reports stay quiet.

### Disconnect

On the **Slack integration** card in **Settings** → **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** next to the workspace. Subscriptions and destinations that use the workspace stop posting until someone reconnects it.

### Connect a workspace

1. Open **Settings** → **Slack** in PostHog Desktop.
2. Under **Workspace connection**, click **Connect Slack workspace**. Your browser opens Slack's permission screen. Click **Allow**.
3. Back in PostHog Desktop, the workspace shows as connected. Use **Connect another workspace** to add more.

### Inbox notifications

Once a workspace is connected, the **Inbox notifications** section appears:

- **Default notification channel** is the team channel every actionable report is posted to, with the suggested reviewers @mentioned. Project admins set it. PostHog must be in the channel, so invite it with `/invite @PostHog`.
- **Notify me directly** pings you in a channel of your choice when you're a suggested reviewer, instead of the team's default channel.
- **Minimum priority** only pings you for reports at or above that priority.

<!-- TODO(screenshot): the Inbox notifications section in PostHog Desktop -->

This is the same configuration as the inbox in the web app, so a change in one place applies everywhere.

### Comment notifications

Under **Comment notifications**, click **Link** next to **Slack account** to link your Slack identity. Then turn on **Slack DMs for comments** to get a direct message when someone mentions you, replies to your comment, or comments on something you own.

### Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** on the workspace row. Reports and notifications from this project stop posting to Slack until someone reconnects it. **Advanced settings in PostHog** at the bottom of the page opens the same integration in the web app.

## Subscribing to insights and dashboards

You can receive regular updates of your [insights](/docs/product-analytics/insights) or [dashboards](/docs/product-analytics/dashboards) directly in Slack.

To set up a subscription:

1. Open the insight or dashboard you want to subscribe to in PostHog
2. Click **Subscribe** in the sidebar
3. Click "Add subscription" and select **Slack** as the destination
4. Choose the Slack channel where you want to receive updates
5. Set your desired frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, etc.)

<!-- TODO(screenshot): the subscription modal with Slack selected as the destination -->

For more details, see our [subscriptions documentation](/docs/product-analytics/subscriptions).

## Setting up event notifications

You can send custom messages to Slack based on PostHog events or actions using [Data Pipelines destinations](/docs/cdp/destinations/slack).

Common use cases include:
- Notifying your team when a user completes a key action
- Alerting on errors or issues detected in your product
- Sharing important user milestones with your team
- Sending survey responses to a dedicated channel

To set up event notifications:

1. Go to the [Data pipeline](https://app.posthog.com/data-management/destinations) tab in PostHog
2. Click the [Destinations](https://app.posthog.com/data-management/destinations?search=slack) tab
3. Click **+ New destination** and search for "Slack"
4. Configure which events or actions should trigger notifications
5. Select your Slack connection and channel
6. Template your message using event and person properties

For detailed setup instructions, see our [Slack destination documentation](/docs/cdp/destinations/slack).

## Link unfurling

When you paste a PostHog insight or dashboard URL in any Slack channel where the PostHog app is installed, Slack automatically shows a rich preview. The preview includes:

- The insight or dashboard title
- The type (e.g., "Trends insight", "Funnel insight", "Dashboard")
- The description, if available

<!-- TODO(screenshot): an unfurled insight link in Slack -->

Link unfurling respects PostHog access permissions. Only team members with at least viewer access to the insight or dashboard will see the unfurled preview.

## What can you do with this integration?

The PostHog Slack integration helps teams stay informed and make data-driven decisions without leaving Slack. You can:

- **Stay updated on key metrics** – Receive regular reports on user behavior, feature adoption, and conversion rates
<!-- - **Get instant insights** – Ask PostHog AI questions and get answers without switching context -->

- **React quickly to events** – Be notified immediately when important user actions or errors occur
- **Share insights with your team** – Make product data accessible to everyone in relevant channels
- **Preview links automatically** – Share PostHog URLs and see rich previews with titles and descriptions without leaving Slack
- **Monitor product health** – Keep an eye on critical metrics and get alerted to anomalies
