### Flushing before leaving your app

Because events are batched, an event captured immediately before your app moves to the background or is closed – for example when opening an external app, following a deep link, redirecting to a payment provider, or logging out – may not be sent until the next batch. Delivery is best-effort: events are persisted to the device's file storage on capture, so anything not sent before the operating system suspends the app isn't lost – it's retried automatically the next time the app opens.
