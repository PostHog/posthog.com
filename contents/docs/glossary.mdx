PostHog is an incredibly broad platform, with many distinct products and tools. As a result it's hugely powerful – but it can be overwhelming if you're new to it, or to product development in general. So we've put together a glossary explaining the most common terms and acronyms you'll come across. If a term is specific to PostHog, it's marked with a [hedgehog](#hedgehog) 🦔 to help reduce confusion.
