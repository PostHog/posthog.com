Join the [PostHog Discord](https://discord.gg/posthog) to get help and share what you build.

For bugs and feature requests, search [open desktop issues](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Adesktop) first. If nothing matches, [open an issue](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog/issues/new) with your app version from **Settings** > **About** and steps to reproduce the problem.
