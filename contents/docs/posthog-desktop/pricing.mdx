PostHog Desktop usage consumes **AI credits**. Credit use depends on the model, token use, and resources required to complete the task.

Each time you interact with PostHog Desktop, such as writing code, querying your PostHog data, or asking an ad-hoc question, credits are spent based on the resources required to complete your request.

This means you only pay for what you use. There's no fixed subscription, and every organization gets a **$20 monthly free tier** to explore PostHog Desktop's features.

## What consumes AI credits

Every interaction with PostHog Desktop consumes AI credits. This includes:

- Writing or editing code
- Querying your PostHog data
- Any ad-hoc question you ask

All usage is charged as credits, after your $20 monthly free tier.

## How AI credit usage works

AI credit use reflects the model and tokens required to complete your request.

- Simple, short tasks use very few tokens, and therefore very few credits.
- More complex tasks that span many files or multiple iterations use more tokens and consume more credits.
- You can pick [any model and any harness](/docs/posthog-desktop/use-any-model-and-harness), so you're free to choose a cheaper model for simpler tasks.

While exact usage varies, credit consumption usually scales with complexity. You'll always see **real-time cost information** while using PostHog Desktop.

## Track your AI credit usage

Stay on top of your AI credit spend in real time:

1. Open the **Plan & usage** tab in the PostHog Desktop app to see your real-time spend, broken down by day and by model
2. Check the [billing overview](https://app.posthog.com/organization/billing/overview) and [billing usage](https://app.posthog.com/organization/billing/usage) pages to view your total monthly consumption

## Control your AI credit spend

You're always in control of your AI credit usage:

- **Real-time tracking:** See costs as you go
- **Billing limit:** Set a limit to prevent additional use after your organization reaches the amount you choose. You can change it at any time.
- **Usage alerts:** Get notified when you hit key thresholds through automated billing emails

When you reach your billing limit, you won't be able to send messages until you raise it. Increase your billing limit to continue using PostHog Desktop.
