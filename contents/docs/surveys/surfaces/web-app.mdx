The [PostHog web app](https://app.posthog.com) is where surveys are built, targeted, launched, and read. You write the questions, decide who sees them and when, watch responses arrive in real time, and every answer carries the respondent's [session replay](/docs/session-replay), events, and person properties.

Open **Surveys** in [PostHog](https://app.posthog.com/surveys) to see every survey in your project with its status, response count, and schedule. Start from a template – NPS, product-market fit, churn, or open feedback – or write your own from scratch, and preview it before anyone sees it.

## What you can do here

- **[Create a survey](/docs/surveys/creating-surveys)** – pick a type (popover, widget, hosted form, or headless API), write your questions, style the appearance, and launch it.
- **Choose how it's delivered** – a popover inside your product, an always-available feedback widget, or a hosted form with a shareable public URL for email, social, or a QR code.
- **Target the right people** – limit a survey by URL, event trigger, device type, person properties, cohort, or a linked [feature flag](/docs/feature-flags), and set a wait period so you don't interrupt the same user twice.
- **[Branch on answers](/docs/surveys/conditional-questions)** – send detractors and promoters down different question paths so nobody answers something irrelevant.
- **[Read the results](/docs/surveys/viewing-results)** – the results tab, a dashboard widget, or your own insights and SQL, each response linked to its replay.
- **[Send responses onward](/docs/surveys/destinations)** – post each answer to Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, or a webhook as it arrives.
- **Run it on a schedule** – launch once or set a recurring schedule with a response limit, and stop it when you have enough.
- **[Create surveys with PostHog AI](/docs/surveys/create-surveys-ai)** – describe the survey you want in plain language and PostHog AI writes the questions, targeting, and display conditions. It's an interaction mode inside the web app, not a separate surface.
- **[Summarize responses with PostHog AI](/docs/surveys/summarize-responses-ai)** – pull themes, sentiment, and segment comparisons out of hundreds of open-text answers without reading them one by one.
