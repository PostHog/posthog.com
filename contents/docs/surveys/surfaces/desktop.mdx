[PostHog Desktop](/docs/posthog-desktop) is where you review the [Self-driving](/docs/self-driving) work your survey responses generate. A dedicated scout watches your active surveys, and when it finds something real – a score regression, an abandonment spike, or a theme several people keep raising – it files one report. When there's a code fix, Self-driving opens a draft pull request you review next to the agents working on your product, and nothing ships until you merge.

Surveys are direct user voice, so the bar for a report is deliberately high: a handful of converging open-text answers can outweigh a much larger analytics signal, but a wobble in a small NPS sample is not a report.

## What you can do here

- Review reports built from recurring survey themes, with the responses, replays, and events behind them attached.
- Review reports for NPS, CSAT, and rating regressions, response-volume drops, and abandonment spikes on active surveys.
- Inspect and edit the draft pull request that acts on a theme before merging.
- Hand a theme off to an agent to investigate further or draft a fix.
