Every email you send shows how it did: sent, delivered, bounced, marked as spam, opened, and clicked. Workflows uses the same events as the rest of PostHog, so you also see what people did next.

PostHog counts opens and clicks only for emails sent with [tracking](/docs/workflows/email/link-tracking) on.

## Transactional emails

In the **Transactional** app, the **Metrics** tab shows how many emails were sent, delivered, and bounced, and how many got spam complaints. Below that, it breaks the numbers down by email and by sending domain, with opens, clicks, and failures.

## Broadcasts

A sent broadcast shows its audience, and how many people it didn't send to and why, such as an opt-out or the frequency cap. It then shows sends, deliveries, bounces, spam complaints, unsubscribes, opens, clicks, and conversions, plus activity over time and the most clicked links. A recurring broadcast lists each send on the **Runs** tab.

## Automations

A live automation's **Overview** tab shows:

- **Conversions:** how many people reached your goal. Click **View converted people** to see who.
- **Step reach:** how many people got to each step, and where they dropped off.
- **Message leaderboard:** each email and push step, ranked by sends, opens, or clicks.
- **In the workflow now:** who is partway through.
- **Health:** failed steps, the hard bounce rate, and the spam complaint rate, with a link to the logs.

The **Metrics** tab charts these numbers over time, for the whole automation or for one step. Each email step also shows the clicks on each link.

## Sending health

Under **Messaging**, the **Reputation** tab shows your bounce rate and spam complaint rate, overall and by mailbox provider, and your daily sending allowance. It shows problems early, before they pause your sending. See [deliverability](/docs/workflows/email/deliverability).

## What each person did

## Use the events anywhere

Turn on [engagement events](/docs/workflows/email/engagement-events), and each send, delivery, bounce, open, click, and unsubscribe becomes a PostHog event, such as `$workflows_email_opened`. Use them in [insights](/docs/product-analytics/insights), [funnels](/docs/product-analytics/funnels), and [cohorts](/docs/data/cohorts), like any other event.

## Ask your agent

Over [MCP](/docs/workflows/mcp), ask "Which of our automations had the most unsubscribes last month, and why?" Your agent reads the same numbers.
