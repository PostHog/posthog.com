The suppression list holds addresses PostHog stops emailing, because email to them bounces or gets reported as spam. It protects your [deliverability](/docs/workflows/email/deliverability), so you don't have to clean your lists by hand.

An [opt-out](/docs/workflows/email/opt-outs) is a person's choice. A suppression is about the address: it can't, or shouldn't, get email.

## How addresses get on it

PostHog adds them for you:

- **Hard bounce:** the address doesn't exist, or refuses mail. Added after the first one.
- **Soft bounces:** the mailbox is full, or the server is down. Added after 5 in a row with no delivery between them.
- **Spam complaint:** the person marked an email as spam. Added after the first one.

You can also click **Add address**, for example for someone who asked you by email to stop.

Find the list in [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/suppression) under **Messaging** > **Suppression list**. Each entry shows the address, what added it, the reason, and when.

## What it blocks

- **Marketing email:** any entry blocks it.
- **Transactional email:** only a hard bounce blocks it, because only a hard bounce proves the address is dead. Someone who reported your newsletter still gets their password reset.

A transactional send to a hard-bounced address fails with `recipient_suppressed`. Don't retry it.

## Remove an address

Remove an entry if a bounce was a one-off, such as a full mailbox that's since been emptied. Don't remove a real hard bounce: it bounces again, and hurts your bounce rate twice. PostHog asks you to confirm before it removes a spam complaint.

## From your own systems

The [messaging suppressions API](/docs/api/messaging-suppressions) lists, adds, and removes entries. A bulk add takes up to 1,000 addresses, and a bulk remove up to 100. To remove a spam complaint, send `confirm_complaints: true`.

Each added address takes a `reason`:

| `reason` | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| `bounce` | All email, transactional too |
| `complaint` | Marketing email |
| `manual` | Marketing email. The default. |

Moving from another provider? Import its bounces as `bounce` before your first send. See [move from another provider](/docs/workflows/migrate).
