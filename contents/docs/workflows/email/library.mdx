The template library keeps the emails your team reuses, such as your welcome email, your monthly update, or a branded layout. Start any broadcast, automation email, or transactional email from one.

Find it in [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/library) under **Templates** > **Email templates**.

<!-- shot: email-template-library: the Templates tab with Email templates selected, showing the preview cards, search, and the Created by, Tags, and Transactional only filters, using demo data -->

## Save a template

- **From scratch:** click **New template**, and write it in the [email editor](/docs/workflows/email-editor).
- **From an email you wrote:** in the email editor, click **Save as new template**. Give it a **Template name** and an optional **Description**, then click **Save template**.

## Find a template

The library shows each template as a preview card. Use **Search templates**, or filter by **Created by**, **Tags**, or **Transactional only**.

## Use a template

In any email, click **Start from template**, and pick one. It replaces the email's content with a copy. Later edits to your email don't change the template, and edits to the template don't change your email.

## Automation templates

To reuse a whole automation, with its trigger and steps, save it as a workflow template under **Templates** > **Workflow templates**. See [templates and suggestions](/docs/workflows/automations/templates).

## From your coding agent

Your coding agent can list, write, and update email templates over [MCP](/docs/workflows/mcp), in the same format as the editor, so you can keep editing them by hand. For example:

- "Write a welcome email for new signups, with our logo and a big Get started button."
- "Change the button on our welcome email to say Get started."
