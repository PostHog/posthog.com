The **PostHog test sender** lets you try Workflows before you [set up your domain](/docs/workflows/domain). It sends from a PostHog address, and only to people in your PostHog organization.

## Who gets the email

Only active members of your PostHog organization with a verified email address. PostHog skips everyone else.

The from name is your organization's name, then "via PostHog", such as "Hedgebox via PostHog". PostHog adds a footer that says the email came from the test sender.

## Where you can use it

- **Automation email steps:** pick **PostHog test sender** in the step's **From** menu.
- **Emails your code sends:** when your project has no verified sender, PostHog sends them from the test sender.

## Limits

- It has daily caps per project and per recipient. Use it to test, not for real traffic.
- It ignores a custom `from` and `reply_to`.
- It refuses CC, BCC, attachments, reply headers, and marketing categories.
- It doesn't add UTM tags, and you can't turn off open and click tracking.

## Move to your own domain

1. [Set up your domain](/docs/workflows/domain).
2. Switch each email step that uses the test sender to your own sender.

Emails your code sends move to your verified sender on their own. You don't change your code.
