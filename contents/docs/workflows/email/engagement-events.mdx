With engagement events on, every send, open, click, bounce, and unsubscribe becomes a PostHog event on the person. You can use them like any other event: in funnels, cohorts, insights, and automation triggers.

## Turn them on

New projects start with engagement events on. To check, go to [**Settings** > **Workflows** > **Engagement events**](https://app.posthog.com/settings/environment-workflows#workflows-engagement-events), and turn on **Capture email engagement events**. It takes a couple of minutes, and doesn't fill in past sends.

The events count toward your event usage. If you turn them off, you still get [metrics](/docs/workflows/metrics).

## Events

| Event | When |
| --- | --- |
| `$workflows_email_sent` | PostHog handed the email to the email provider. |
| `$workflows_email_delivered` | The person's mail server accepted it. |
| `$workflows_email_opened` | The person opened it. Needs [open tracking](/docs/workflows/email/link-tracking). |
| `$workflows_email_link_clicked` | The person clicked a link. Needs click tracking. |
| `$workflows_email_bounced` | It bounced. |
| `$workflows_email_blocked` | The person marked it as spam. |
| `$workflows_email_failed` | It wasn't sent, or the email provider rejected it. |
| `$workflows_email_unsubscribed` | The person unsubscribed. |
| `$workflows_email_tracking_consent_updated` | The person changed their [tracking consent](/docs/workflows/email/link-tracking#tracking-consent). |
| `$workflows_conversion` | The person reached an automation's [conversion goal](/docs/workflows/automations/triggers#conversion-goal-and-exit). PostHog records it even with engagement events off. |

Test sends don't create events.

## Properties

| Property | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `$workflow_id` | The broadcast or automation that sent it |
| `$workflow_action_id` | The email step |
| `$email_to` | The recipient |
| `$email_subject` | The subject. Left out for [sensitive](/docs/workflows/transactional/sensitive-data) emails. |
| `$email_transactional` | Whether the email was transactional |
| `$email_template_id` | The transactional email's template ID, when the send used one |
| `$email_template_key` | The `template_key` of an email your code wrote |
| `$email_tracking_enabled` | Whether the send had tracking. On sent and failed events. |
| `$link_url` | The link, on clicks. Only the site address for sensitive emails. |
| `$bounce_type` | `hard`, `soft`, or `unknown`, on bounces |

Unsubscribe and consent events belong to the email address, not to one send. Their distinct ID is the email address, and they have `$email` and `source`. An unsubscribe also has `category`: the category's ID, or `$all` for all marketing email. When the person used the link in a workflow email, it also has that email's `$workflow_id`. A consent event has `status`: `OPTED_IN` or `OPTED_OUT`.

## Ideas

- **Did the email work?** A funnel from `$workflows_email_delivered` to your own event, such as `password_reset_completed`.
- **Who's ignoring you?** A cohort of people with no `$workflows_email_opened` in 90 days, to leave out of broadcasts.
- **Follow up on a click.** An automation triggered by `$workflows_email_link_clicked` on your pricing link.
