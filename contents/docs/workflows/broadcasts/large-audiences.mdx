Mailbox providers trust senders that grow steadily. So each project has a sending allowance that grows as you send clean email, and an email broadcast can only reach an audience that fits it. Push broadcasts don't use the allowance.

## Your sending allowance

Your project's tier sets how many emails it can send a day. One email broadcast can reach up to one day's allowance.

| Tier | Emails a day |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 100 |
| 1 | 1,000 |
| 2 | 3,000 |
| 3 | 10,000 |
| 4 | 30,000 |
| 5 | 100,000 |
| 6 | 300,000 |
| 7 | 1,000,000 |

A new project starts at tier 0. A project in an organization with an active paid subscription in good standing starts at tier 3.

Once a day, PostHog moves a project up one tier when all of these are true:

- It held its tier for at least 3 to 7 days. Higher tiers wait longer.
- It sent at least half its daily allowance on 2 separate days.
- Its bounce rate is under 2%, and its spam complaint rate is under 0.1%.

A full climb from tier 0 takes about five weeks. A project goes down one tier when its bounce or complaint rate goes over the limit, and one tier for every 30 days it sends nothing.

The allowance refills during the day, not at midnight. Email over the allowance waits in a queue and goes out as it refills. Nothing is dropped. Transactional email counts toward the allowance, but never waits for it, and goes out ahead of marketing email. So a big broadcast never holds up a password reset.

The **Reputation** tab shows your allowance, the largest audience you can send to, and how close you are to the next tier. See [sending health](/docs/workflows/metrics#sending-health).

## When the audience is too big

If an email audience is bigger than your allowance, the **Recipients** step shows your limit, and you can't launch the broadcast. You can:

- **Narrow it:** add [filters](/docs/workflows/broadcasts/audiences#filter-people).
- **Slow it down:** limit the sending rate, below.
- **Wait:** your tier grows as you send clean email.

## Limit the sending rate

On the **Schedule** step, turn on **Limit sending rate**, and set the most emails per minute or per hour. The broadcast spreads its sends out, and the rest wait their turn.

A slow rate also lets a broadcast reach more people than your daily allowance: as many as that rate sends in 7 days. The rate can use at most half your daily allowance, so the most a slow broadcast can reach is about 3.5 times your daily allowance. For example, at tier 3, 200 emails an hour for 7 days reaches 33,600 people.

When an audience is too big, PostHog suggests a rate that fits, such as **Use 200 per hour**. Click it to set the rate.

While a slowed broadcast that is over your allowance is still sending, you can't change its rate.
