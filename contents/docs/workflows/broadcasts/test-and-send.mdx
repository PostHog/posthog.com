Sent email can't be unsent, so check it in a real inbox first.

## Send a test

On the **Review** step, click **Send test email** and enter your address. You can also send one from the preview on the **Content** step. Nobody in the audience gets a test.

The test fills in variables with the values of the person in the preview. Read it in your inbox, on desktop and on your phone, and click its links.

For a push broadcast, click **Send test notification**. The test goes to the devices of the distinct ID you enter. See [push broadcasts](/docs/workflows/broadcasts/push).

## Launch

On the **Review** step, click **Send now**, or **Schedule broadcast** for a later or recurring send. If something needs fixing, such as an unverified sender or an audience over your [sending allowance](/docs/workflows/broadcasts/large-audiences), PostHog tells you what to fix and doesn't send.

## After you send

The broadcast's **Overview** shows who was in the audience and why some people didn't get it. Then it shows sends, deliveries, opens, clicks, and conversions. **Runs** lists each send of a recurring broadcast. See [metrics](/docs/workflows/metrics#broadcasts).

## Send it again

A sent broadcast can't change. Click **Actions**, then **Send again as a new broadcast**. PostHog makes a copy with the same audience and email, which you can change before you send it.

To change a broadcast that is scheduled and hasn't sent, use **Stop and edit**. See [change or stop a scheduled broadcast](/docs/workflows/broadcasts/scheduling#change-or-stop-a-scheduled-broadcast).

## Turn it into an automation

To add delays, branches, or a follow-up email, click **Actions**, then **Open in workflow editor**. You can also click **Continue in the workflow editor** on the **Review** step. After you add steps, the broadcast shows read-only on **Broadcasts**, and you keep editing it as an [automation](/docs/workflows/automations).

## Archive it

To archive a broadcast, click **Archive** in its **Actions** menu, or in its menu in the list. Any schedule stops sending. You can't archive a broadcast while it's sending.

An archived broadcast can go back to a draft with **Restore as draft**, or you can **Delete** it for good.
