A broadcast can send a push notification instead of an email. It shows on each person's phone, on every device they registered with your app.

## Before you start

Connect a push channel for your app (Firebase for Android, APNs for iOS), and register devices with the PostHog SDK. See [push notifications](/docs/workflows/push).

Until a push channel is connected, the **Push** option is turned off.

## Send one

1. On the **Recipients** step, under **Send as**, pick **Push**.
2. Pick the audience, the same as for email. See [audiences](/docs/workflows/broadcasts/audiences). The step shows how many people in it can receive push.
3. On the **Content** step, under **Send through**, pick the apps to send through. Write a **Title**, and an optional **Message**. Both can use variables such as `{{ person.properties.name }}`.
4. On the **Review** step, click **Send test notification**. Enter the distinct ID your app identifies you with, and check the notification on your phone. Nobody in the audience gets a test.
5. Click **Send now** or **Schedule broadcast**.

Only people who turned on push in your app get the notification. A [message category](/docs/workflows/broadcasts/audiences#pick-a-message-category) works the same as for email: people who unsubscribed from it are skipped.

## Results

A sent push broadcast shows how many notifications were sent and opened, how many people had no device, how many failed, and, with a goal, how many converted. See [metrics](/docs/workflows/metrics#broadcasts).
