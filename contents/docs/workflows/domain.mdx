---
title: Set up your domain
---

PostHog sends email to your users only from a domain you verified. Transactional email, broadcasts, and automations all use the same senders, so you set up your domain once per project.

Not ready to touch DNS? The [test sender](/docs/workflows/email/test-sender) lets you send to your own team first.

## Add your sender

You need to be a project admin.

1. In [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/channels), go to **Messaging** > **Channels**, click **New channel**, and select **Email**.
2. Enter your domain and pick a **Sending subdomain**. The default is `mail.`, such as `mail.example.com`, which keeps your main domain safe if a campaign lands in spam. You can also pick `send.`, **No subdomain**, or **Custom**.
3. Enter the **Name** and **Email address** that people see in their inbox, and click **Continue**.
4. Add the DNS records that PostHog shows. Pick one:
   - **Add them to Cloudflare for me** (or Vercel): approve the change once at your DNS host.
   - **I'll add them myself:** copy each record into your DNS host, such as Route 53 or Namecheap.
   - **Let an agent do it:** copy a prompt with the exact records, and the checks to run, into your coding agent.

Confirming the records can take up to 72 hours. If the email provider gives up waiting, you see **Verification stopped**. Check the records, then click **Restart verification**.

## The DNS records

Copy each value from PostHog, not from this page. The tokens are unique to your domain.

| Record | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DKIM (three records) | CNAME | Signs your email, so inboxes can check that it came from your domain. |
| SPF | TXT | Lists the servers that can send for your domain. If you already have an SPF record, add `include:amazonses.com` to it instead of adding a second one. |
| Custom MAIL FROM, such as `feedback.mail.example.com` | MX and TXT | Sends bounces back to PostHog. |
| DMARC | TXT | Tells inboxes what to do with email that fails the checks. PostHog needs one before it verifies the domain. An existing DMARC record counts. |

PostHog can also ask for a verification TXT record at `_amazonses`, or an ownership record when another organization already uses the domain.

## Already sending from this domain?

Add PostHog's records next to your old provider's. Both can send from the same domain, so you can move one email at a time. See [move from another provider](/docs/workflows/migrate#switch-over-safely).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Domain still pending | Some DNS hosts, such as Namecheap, add your domain to the end of the name, so `feedback.mail.example.com` becomes `feedback.mail.example.com.example.com`. Enter only the part before your domain. |
| Cloudflare records don't verify | Turn off the proxy (gray cloud) for the DKIM CNAME records. |
| No address in the **From** menu | Add a sender on the **Channels** tab first. |
| Emails go to spam | Check that DKIM, SPF, and DMARC pass, then see [deliverability](/docs/workflows/email/deliverability). |
