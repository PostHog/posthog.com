You don't have to start from a blank canvas.

## Start from a template

Click **New workflow**, and pick a template, such as **Welcome email sequence**, **Convert trials to paid**, or **Abandoned checkout recovery**. Filter the list by type or category. The template opens with its steps in place. Change the trigger, the emails, and the timing to fit your product, then enable it.

## Save your own

To let your team reuse an automation, open the **Edit** menu, and click **Save as template**. Under **Scope**, pick who can use it:

- **This project only:** the default.
- **All projects in organization:** every project in your organization.

## Let PostHog suggest one

PostHog reads your project's events and suggests automations to build, such as a welcome email for a signup event. Click **Create workflow** on a suggestion, and PostHog builds it as a draft for you to check before you enable it.

Your coding agent can do the same over [MCP](/docs/workflows/mcp): "Suggest an automation from our events, and build it as a draft."

## Ask PostHog AI

Describe the automation in plain English, such as "Email new signups who haven't created a dashboard after 2 days", and PostHog AI builds it as a draft. This is rolling out, so your project might not have it yet.
