Workflows is how PostHog sends email and messages. It covers three jobs:

- **[Transactional emails](/docs/workflows/transactional-emails):** password resets, receipts, and sign-in codes, sent from your code with one SDK call.
- **[Broadcasts](/docs/workflows/broadcasts) and [drip campaigns](/docs/workflows/email-drip-campaign):** one email to an audience, or a sequence that starts when a person does something and follows up based on what they do next.
- **Automations:** delays, branches, Slack posts, webhooks, and any [real-time destination](/docs/data-pipelines/destinations) as steps in the same [workflow builder](/docs/workflows/workflow-builder).

Everything runs on the events and person properties you already send to PostHog, so there is no separate marketing dataset to sync. Every send lands on the person who received it, next to their events, replays, and flags.

## Where you can use it

You build and launch workflows in the PostHog web app. MCP and the API are for scripting, reviewing, and automating that same work from outside the app.

## Where its data comes from

A workflow is only as good as what it can trigger on and who it can reach. Three things feed it: the events and person data already in your project, the channels you connect, and the templates and opt-out rules that decide what gets sent to whom.
