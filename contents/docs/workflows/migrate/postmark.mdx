PostHog imports your Postmark suppressions, message streams, and templates in one background import.

> The Postmark import is rolling out. If Postmark isn't in the list, your project doesn't have it yet.

## What moves

| From Postmark | In PostHog |
| --- | --- |
| Hard bounces | [Suppression list](/docs/workflows/email/suppression). Blocks all email. |
| Spam complaints and manual suppressions | Suppression list. Blocks marketing email. |
| Unsubscribes | Opted out of all marketing email |
| Custom broadcast streams | Marketing [categories](/docs/workflows/email/opt-outs) |
| Templates, with their layout | Templates, keyed by their Postmark alias. Pick a sender for each, because Postmark templates don't name one. |
| Servers, streams, domains, senders, and webhooks | A list, so you know what to set up |

Postmark has no automations or contact lists. Inbound streams, SMTP settings, send history, stats, and tokens stay in Postmark.

## Run the import

1. Copy a token from [Postmark](https://account.postmarkapp.com/api_tokens). A **server token** imports one server. The **account token** imports every live server, and lists your domains and senders. PostHog only reads, and deletes the token when the import ends.
2. In [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/opt-outs), go to **Messaging** > **Opt-outs** and click **Import from another tool**.
3. Pick **Postmark**, paste the token, pick what to import, and click **Start import**.

## Switch your sending

- **Transactional stream:** its mail becomes [transactional email](/docs/workflows/transactional) in PostHog, which only a hard bounce blocks. Postmark also blocks spam complaints and manual entries on this stream, so those addresses now get transactional email.
- **SMTP:** PostHog has no SMTP service. Change an app that sends over SMTP to the [server SDK](/docs/workflows/installation) or the [API](/docs/workflows/installation/api).
- **Inbound:** PostHog can't receive email, so inbound stays in Postmark.

See [switch over safely](/docs/workflows/migrate#switch-over-safely) for the full checklist.
