PostHog charges based on usage. You pay for what you use across each product, and generous free tiers mean many users pay nothing. This section explains how to estimate your costs, set spend controls, and understand your invoices.

## In this section

- [Estimating usage and costs](/docs/billing/estimating-usage-costs) – Learn why PostHog prices on usage, how to estimate your usage, and how to reduce your costs.
- [Billing limits and alerts](/docs/billing/limits-alerts) – Set spend limits per product and get alerts before you reach them.
- [Pre-paid plans](/docs/billing/pre-paid-plans) – Commit to usage in advance for a discount on high-volume products.
- [Spike detection](/docs/billing/spike-detection) – See how PostHog finds sudden jumps in usage and what to do about them.
- [Common questions about billing](/docs/billing/common-questions) – Read answers to frequent questions about invoices, payment, and plans.
