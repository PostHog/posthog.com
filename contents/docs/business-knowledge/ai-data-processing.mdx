<PrivateBeta />

PostHog AI, Support, and the Playground can't use a source until two checks pass. Until then, answers stay empty.

<Steps>

<Step title="Approve AI data processing" badge="required">

An organization owner or admin turns on **AI service providers** in organization settings. Until then, PostHog doesn't send source text to a model, so answers stay empty.

<OSButton variant="primary" external asLink to="https://app.posthog.com/settings/organization-details#setting=organization-ai-consent">Approve AI data processing</OSButton>

</Step>

<Step title="Wait for the content check" badge="required">

PostHog checks each document before search can use it. Search leaves a document out until the check marks it safe, and always leaves out a document marked unsafe. See [Content checks](/docs/business-knowledge/sources#content-checks).

</Step>

<Step title="Your sources are ready" checkpoint>

Keyword search works as soon as a document passes the content check. Semantic search builds in the background and can take up to an hour after that. The source shows **indexing** until it's done.

</Step>

</Steps>
