<QuestLogItem title="Add a source" subtitle="Text, a page, or a file" icon="IconUpload">

Open [**Business Knowledge**](https://app.posthog.com/business-knowledge) and choose **Add source**. You can also start from an empty project with **Add your first source**.

Paste a policy, add a public URL, or upload a file. The list shows how many sources are ready and how many chunks are indexed.

Sources belong to the project you add them in. [Learn from support](/docs/business-knowledge/learn-from-support) stores its setting and its learned sources on the parent project. [GitHub repositories](/docs/business-knowledge/github) stay on the environment where you connect them.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/sources#text">Add a source</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

<QuestLogItem title="Turn on learning" subtitle="Optional" icon="IconToggle">

Let PostHog add reusable answers from resolved Support tickets. Learning is off until you turn it on, and Support has to be on in that environment first.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/learn-from-support">Enable learning</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

<QuestLogItem title="Choose what every reply includes" subtitle="Tone and policies" icon="IconNotebook">

Turn on **Always include in replies** for guidance that should be in every answer: tone, policies, and company direction. PostHog still runs the content check.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/sources#always-include-in-replies">Set a source to always include</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

<QuestLogItem title="See what's covered and what's missing" subtitle="Playground" icon="IconSparkles">

Ask a question a customer or teammate would ask. If the answer cites a source, that fact is already in Business Knowledge. If it can't find the fact, add a source and ask again.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/playground">Open the Playground</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

<QuestLogItem title="Use the same sources in answers" subtitle="PostHog AI, Support, and MCP" icon="IconMagic">

PostHog AI searches these sources when a question is about your product, policies, or team. Support reads them when it drafts a reply. MCP clients can search and update them with the `business_knowledge:read` or `business_knowledge:write` scope.

Answers stay empty until [two checks](/docs/business-knowledge/requirements) pass.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge">Where you can use it</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

</QuestLog>
