<QuestLogItem title="Approve AI data processing" subtitle="Organization admins" icon="IconLock">

An organization owner or admin must approve **AI data processing** in organization settings, under **AI service providers**. Until it's approved, PostHog can't check your sources, so answers can't use them and the Playground refuses questions.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/requirements">Check the requirements</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

<QuestLogItem title="Add a source" subtitle="Text, a page, or a file" icon="IconUpload">

Open [**Business Knowledge**](https://app.posthog.com/business-knowledge) and choose **Add source**. Paste a policy, add a public URL such as your docs or pricing page, or upload a file.

PostHog checks the content of each new source before answers can use it. Keyword search works as soon as the check passes. Semantic search takes up to an hour more, and the source shows **indexing** until it's done.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/sources">Add a source</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

<QuestLogItem title="Turn on learning" subtitle="Optional" icon="IconToggle">

Let PostHog turn your team's replies on resolved Support tickets into reusable answers. Learning is off until you turn it on, and Support has to be on in that environment first.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/learn-from-support">Enable learning</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

<QuestLogItem title="Choose what every reply includes" subtitle="Tone and policies" icon="IconNotebook">

Turn on **Always include in replies** for guidance that every answer must follow, such as your tone of voice or a company policy.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/sources#include-a-source-in-every-reply">Always include a source</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

<QuestLogItem title="See what's covered and what's missing" subtitle="Playground" icon="IconSparkles">

Ask a question that a customer or teammate would ask, and that your new source answers. If the answer cites your source, the setup works. If it doesn't, add a source that holds the fact and ask again.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/playground">Open the Playground</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

<QuestLogItem title="Use the same sources everywhere" subtitle="PostHog AI, Support, and MCP" icon="IconMagic">

PostHog AI searches your sources when a question is about your product, policies, or team. Support searches them when it drafts a reply. Your own agent can search and update them through MCP, with the `business_knowledge:read` or `business_knowledge:write` scope.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge">Where you can use it</OSButton>

</QuestLogItem>

</QuestLog>
