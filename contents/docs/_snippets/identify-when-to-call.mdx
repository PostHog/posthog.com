In your frontend, you should call `identify` as soon as you're able to. 

Typically, this is every time your **app loads** for the first time, and directly after your **users log in**. 

This ensures that events sent during your users' sessions are correctly associated with them. 

You only need to call `identify` once per session, and you should avoid calling it multiple times unnecessarily.

If you call `identify` multiple times with the same data without reloading the page in between, PostHog will ignore the subsequent calls. After a page reload, a repeat call that passes person properties sends a `$set` event, which is billed as an identified event. On a multi-page site, pass person properties only when they change. See [cutting costs](/docs/product-analytics/cutting-costs#only-call-identify-once-per-session).
