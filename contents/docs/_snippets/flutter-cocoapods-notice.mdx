> **CocoaPods is becoming read-only.** [CocoaPods trunk becomes read-only on December 2, 2026](https://blog.cocoapods.org/CocoaPods-Specs-Repo/), and `posthog-ios` 4.0 and later is only published through Swift Package Manager (SPM).
>
> `posthog_flutter` 6.0 and later uses `posthog-ios` 4.0 and installs on iOS and macOS through SPM only. It has no podspec. It needs Flutter 3.41 or later, Xcode 26 or later, iOS 15, and macOS 11. Flutter 3.44 and later turn SPM on by default. See [Upgrading from version 5 to version 6](/docs/libraries/flutter#upgrading-from-version-5-to-version-6).
>
> `posthog_flutter` 5.x stays on `posthog-ios` 3.x through CocoaPods or SPM. If your app can't use SPM, such as a Flutter module embedded in an iOS or macOS app, stay on 5.x.
>
> This change doesn't affect `posthog_flutter` releases on pub.dev, Android, or web.
