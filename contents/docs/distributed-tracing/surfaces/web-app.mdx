Use [Tracing](https://app.posthog.com/tracing) in the PostHog web app to isolate slow or failing requests, read span waterfalls, compare deploys, and jump to logs, errors, or replays.

## Gather what you need

Send spans to your PostHog project over OTLP or a PostHog SDK. Follow [Install tracing](/docs/distributed-tracing/installation) if your app isn't exporting spans yet.

## Pick a workflow

| Workflow | Where it lives | Reach for it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Search and filter traces](/docs/distributed-tracing/search)** | **Tracing** (`Spans` and `Traces` modes) | You want to filter by service, status, duration, or attributes and zoom into latency spikes |
| **[Inspect traces and spans](/docs/distributed-tracing/inspect-traces)** | Trace drawer (`Waterfall` and `Flame graph`) | You spotted a slow or errored trace and want to see where wall-clock time went |
| **[Analyze operations and compare windows](/docs/distributed-tracing/operations-and-compare)** | **Tracing** (`Operations` mode and `Compare`) and `/tracing/operation` | You want per-endpoint p50/p95/p99/p99.9 rankings or a baseline diff after a deploy |
| **[Query traces with SQL](/docs/distributed-tracing/sql)** | [SQL editor](https://app.posthog.com/sql) (`posthog.trace_spans`) | You need custom percentiles, attribute breakdowns, or exports beyond viewer controls |
| **[Link persons, sessions, logs, and errors](/docs/distributed-tracing/correlate-data)** | **Settings > Tracing** and the trace drawer | You want person and session chips on traces or correlated Logs and Error Tracking records |
