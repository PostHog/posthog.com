A slow or failed row doesn't show which call broke. Open the trace drawer to see every span in the request, find where wall-clock time went, and jump to correlated logs or errors.

- **When you need it.** You spotted a slow or errored row in [Tracing](https://app.posthog.com/tracing) and need the root cause inside that request.
- **What you get.** The drawer opens a span waterfall or flame graph on the left and the span inspector on the right.
- **How you know it worked.** Selecting any bar on the left updates the summary header and attributes on the right without leaving the trace.

## Gather what you need

Send spans to your PostHog project over OTLP or a PostHog SDK. Follow [Install tracing](/docs/distributed-tracing/installation) if your project doesn't have spans yet.

## Open a trace

Open [Tracing](https://app.posthog.com/tracing) and click any row in **Spans** or **Traces** mode. You can also inspect sampled traces inline from an [operation detail page](/docs/distributed-tracing/operations-and-compare).

## Read the waterfall

The left pane shows the trace tree, and the right pane inspects the selected span.

Drag the divider to resize the inspector, or use the bottom-right drawer footer buttons to hide it or toggle fullscreen.

The left pane supports two views:

| View | What it shows | Reach for it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waterfall** | Every span laid out chronologically as nested bars, colored by service | You want to see sequence, concurrency, gaps, and which call waited on what |
| **Flame graph** | A call tree aggregating repeated operations by total duration | A trace has loops or N+1 queries and you want the heaviest call paths grouped together |

If your instrumentation missed a parent span, PostHog groups orphan spans under a top-level `<parent span missing>` row so you don't lose child timing. Select that placeholder row to see the missing-parent details in the inspector.

Use keyboard shortcuts in **Waterfall** mode to move through large traces faster:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `j` / `k` | Select the next or previous span |
| `Left` / `Right` | Collapse or expand the selected span's children |
| `Escape` | Close the trace drawer |

## Navigate large traces

The trace API returns up to `2,000` spans per page. To load the next page, scroll to the bottom of the waterfall or click the load-more footer in the flame graph.

Click the link icon in the drawer header to copy a direct URL to the trace. The URL includes the trace timestamp as a lookup hint, so teammates open it even past the default one-hour window.

## Use the span inspector

The summary header shows the operation name, status badge (`OK` or `Error`), service dot, duration, and timestamp range. Copy `Span ID`, `Trace ID`, or a child span's `Parent span ID` from the header.

If every span with an identity resolves to one person or session, the drawer header also shows person and session chips.

The inspector renders up to six tabs depending on what the span carries:

- **Attributes.** Lists span attributes, resource attributes, and OpenTelemetry span events. Use the inline buttons beside any attribute to include it in viewer filters, exclude it, or add it as a column in the span list.
- **Query.** Appears on database spans with query text and formats the SQL for copying.
- **Logs.** Shows correlated logs from [PostHog Logs](/docs/logs). Switch between **Whole trace** and **This span**, or click through to open the full Logs view with filters pre-applied.
- **Errors.** Lists correlated issues from [Error Tracking](/docs/error-tracking) when you have Error Tracking viewer access. Switch between **This span**, **This trace**, and **This session**.
- **Operations.** Summarizes every operation inside this trace by call count and duration. Click a row to highlight all matching spans in the waterfall or focus them in the flame graph.
- **Raw.** Displays the full span payload as an interactive JSON tree.

> **Tabs stay out of the way when empty.** If you view **Query** on a database span and click an HTTP span, the inspector falls back to **Attributes**. It restores **Query** when you select another database span.

## Check it worked

Check for these signals in the trace drawer:

- **Complete tree.** The root span and child spans render in the waterfall with no `<parent span missing>` row.
- **Span details.** Selecting a span shows its attributes and copyable `Trace ID` in the inspector header.
- **Correlated tabs.** Switching to **Logs** or **Errors** loads matching entries when your app sends correlated IDs.

## Fix what goes wrong

- **Missing parent span row.** No span matching `parent_span_id` arrived in PostHog, so an upstream service isn't instrumented, a sampler dropped the parent, or a short-lived process exited early. Call `flush()` before exiting in serverless or CLI workloads.
- **Logs or Errors tab is empty.** Check that your logger emits the active OpenTelemetry `trace_id` and `span_id`, and that `$exception` events carry `$trace_id`, `$span_id`, or `$session_id`. See [Link persons, sessions, logs, and errors](/docs/distributed-tracing/correlate-data).
