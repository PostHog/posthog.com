The sandbox sender is an email sender that PostHog manages. It sends from a PostHog address, so you can try Messaging before you [verify your sending domain](/docs/email/verify-domain). It only delivers to members of your PostHog organization.

## Who gets the email

The sandbox sender delivers only to active members of your PostHog organization whose PostHog email address is verified. Recipients see your organization's name followed by "via PostHog" as the from name.

To email anyone else, such as your users, add your own sender and verify its domain.

## Daily caps

The sandbox sender has daily caps for each project and for each recipient. When a project or a recipient reaches its cap, the sandbox sender refuses more emails until the cap frees up. Use the sandbox sender for tests, not for real traffic.

## Where you can use it

Once the sandbox sender is set up in your project, it shows as **PostHog test sender** on the [**Channels** tab](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/channels).

- **Workflow email steps** - Choose the sandbox sender as the step's sender. If it isn't set up yet, the step shows **Set up the sandbox sender**. The sandbox sender doesn't support a custom from name or address, several senders, a reply-to address, CC or BCC recipients, UTM tags on links, or turning off open and click tracking. When you switch a step to the sandbox sender, the step loses these settings.
- **[Transactional emails](/docs/email/transactional-emails) from your code** - When the project has no verified sender, a send with content from your code goes out from the sandbox sender. The send can't include `from`, `reply_to`, `cc`, `bcc`, or `attachments`. A send with one of these fields fails with an error that asks you to verify a domain.

## Leave the sandbox

1. [Verify your sending domain](/docs/email/verify-domain). On the **Channels** tab, click **Add your own sender**.
2. In each workflow email step that uses the sandbox sender, select your verified sender.

Sends from your code use a verified sender as soon as the project has one. You don't change your code.
