<QuestLogItem
  title="Set up a sender"
  subtitle="Required"
  icon="IconLetter"
>

Every email comes from a sender. To try Email & Messaging right away, use the [sandbox sender](/docs/email/sandbox-sender). It sends from a PostHog address before you verify a domain, but it only delivers to members of your PostHog organization.

To email your users, add your own sender on the [**Channels** tab](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/channels) and verify its domain. You add a few DNS records, and verification usually takes a few minutes.

<QuestLogItem
  title="Send a transactional email"
  subtitle="Recommended"
  icon="IconCode"
>

Transactional emails are the one-off emails your code sends to one person, such as password resets, sign-in codes, and receipts. Your backend sends each one with one call to a [server SDK](/docs/email/installation#send-email-from-your-backend) or the API.

```js
await posthog.emails.send({
  templateId: "<template_id>",
  to: "you@example.com",
  templateData: { first_name: "Ada" },
  idempotencyKey: "my-first-email",
})
```

The quick start creates the email and an API key, then sends a test email to you in a few minutes.

<QuestLogItem
  title="Send a broadcast"
  subtitle="Recommended"
  icon="IconSend"
>

A broadcast sends one email to many people, once or on a schedule, such as a product update or a weekly newsletter. Choose the recipients with person properties and cohorts, write the email, and schedule it. PostHog saves your progress as a draft until you send.

<QuestLogItem
  title="Build an onboarding sequence"
  subtitle="Recommended"
  icon="IconDecisionTree"
>

A drip campaign is a series of emails that reacts to what each person does, such as an onboarding sequence. Drip campaigns use the [workflow builder](/docs/workflows/workflow-builder), which starts a workflow from a trigger, such as a sign-up event, then waits, splits the audience, and sends messages at each step.

To trigger a workflow from what people do in your product, [install a PostHog SDK](/docs/email/installation#trigger-broadcasts-and-automations-from-events) in your app. Then follow [how to create an email drip campaign](/docs/email/email-drip-campaign) to send a welcome email at sign-up and a follow-up a few days later. For a tour of the builder, see [launch your first workflow](/docs/workflows/launch-workflow).

<QuestLogItem
  title="See the results"
  subtitle="Recommended"
  icon="IconGraph"
>

Each workflow, broadcast, and transactional email shows its sends, deliveries, opens, clicks, and bounces. Turn on engagement events, and PostHog also captures each send, open, and click as an event on the person who got it. Use them in insights, funnels, and dashboards, next to your product events.
