Broadcasts and workflows send to the people already in your PostHog project. You choose them with the same [person properties](/docs/data/persons) and [cohorts](/docs/data/cohorts) you use in insights, so there's no list to upload and no sync job to keep running.

## Who a broadcast reaches

In the **Recipients** step of a [broadcast](/docs/email/broadcasts), click **Add condition** and filter by person properties, cohorts, or distinct IDs. A person must match all of your conditions. With no conditions, the broadcast goes to everyone in your project.

The step shows an estimate of how many people the broadcast reaches before you send it.

## Send to a list

To send to a list from outside PostHog, upload it as a CSV. PostHog saves it as a static cohort, which you then pick as the audience.

- The CSV needs an `email` column.
- A `distinct_id` or `id` column matches rows to people already in PostHog.
- Other columns update properties on those people.

## When PostHog works out the audience

PostHog works out the audience when the broadcast or batch runs, not when you save it. A person who matches your conditions at send time gets the email. A [static cohort](/docs/data/cohorts) uses the people saved in it.

PostHog reads person properties again when each message goes out, so a `{{ person.properties.first_name }}` merge tag uses the current value.

## One email per address

When two or more people share an email address, PostHog sends that address one email. It compares addresses after it lowercases them and trims spaces.

This applies when every email step sends to the standard `person.properties.email` property. An email step with a custom recipient isn't deduplicated this way.

## Who an automation reaches

An automation reaches people through its trigger. For each trigger type, see [triggers in the workflow builder](/docs/workflows/workflow-builder#triggers).

| Trigger | Who it reaches |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Each person who does the event, the moment they do it. |
| Batch | Each person who matches an audience, once or on a schedule. This is how you send a recurring broadcast. |

Webhook, manual, schedule, tracking pixel, and Slack triggers start from something outside a person's activity in your product. A schedule trigger runs once per occurrence, without a person.

To keep a person from getting too many messages, set a [frequency cap](/docs/email/frequency-cap). To respect what people opted out of, see [opt-outs and suppression](/docs/email/opt-outs).
