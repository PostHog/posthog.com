PostHog AI is an AI product analyst that lives inside PostHog. Ask it a question in plain English and it queries your data, builds the insight, writes the SQL, or finds the session replay – then explains what it found. Because it runs on your project's own events, persons, and schema, its answers are about your product, not a generic dataset.

It also does the work, not just the analysis. PostHog AI creates dashboards, surveys, alerts, and feature flags, edits filters in the UI alongside you, and remembers what it learns about your product between conversations. It's the same agent that executes [Self-driving](/docs/self-driving) tasks, so the thing answering your questions is the thing opening your pull requests.

## Where you can use it

## How PostHog AI works with Self-driving

Most tools feed [Self-driving](/docs/self-driving) as a signal source. PostHog AI sits at the other end of the loop: it's the agent that picks the work up and does it. When a report from your signals needs a code fix, PostHog AI runs it in a sandbox against your connected repo and opens a draft pull request.
