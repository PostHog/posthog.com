The [PostHog web app](https://app.posthog.com) is home base for PostHog AI. It's available on every page, either as a full chat in the right-hand sidebar or as inline entry points built into the features you're already using, so you can hand off a question without losing your place.

Because it runs next to your data, PostHog AI can do more than answer. It reads your events, insights, and warehouse schema, runs queries against them, and then acts – creating dashboards, editing the filters on the page you're on, or writing the SQL you were about to write yourself.

## Before you start

1. **Enable PostHog AI** – [grant it access to your project](/docs/posthog-ai/allow-access), which an org admin does once.
2. **Open the web app** – no install needed, just sign in to [PostHog](https://app.posthog.com).

## In-app chat

The primary dedicated interface is an in-app <a href="https://app.posthog.com/#panel=max">AI chat</a> for conventional prompting and conversation-based flows.

When logged in, the PostHog AI chat is always available in the right-hand sidebar of the PostHog app. You can open it at any time by clicking the PostHog AI icon.

## Platform UI

PostHog AI shows up throughout the PostHog platform as inline or ambient UI elements, typically labelled with the <span className="inline-block"><IconSparkles className="h-4 w-4" /> </span> icon, and embedded within other features.

These entrypoints or shortcuts enable you to trigger PostHog AI directly from the PostHog UI.

## Website chat

PostHog AI is also accessible when logged out of the app and on the website, [posthog.com](/). In this mode it's limited to searching and answering questions about PostHog's documentation – it has no access to your project data.

## What you can do here

- **[Try example prompts](/docs/posthog-ai/example-prompts)** – a starting point for each PostHog product, from analytics to surveys to error tracking.
- **[Write and edit SQL](/docs/posthog-ai/write-and-edit-sql)** – explore your data and iterate on HogQL queries directly in the SQL editor.
- **[Investigate web traffic](/docs/posthog-ai/investigate-web-traffic)** – build insights and dashboards from your web analytics and session replay data.
- **[Switch modes](/docs/posthog-ai/modes)** – move between product analytics, SQL, session replay, error tracking, surveys, and feature flags without losing the thread.
- **[Plan before you act](/docs/posthog-ai/plan-mode)** and **[run deep research](/docs/posthog-ai/research-mode)** – for multi-step work you want to review first, or questions worth a long investigation.

## Approving sensitive actions

PostHog AI doesn't quietly run anything destructive. When a step could have real consequences, it pauses and shows you an approval card describing what it's about to do. The run only continues once you approve it, and you can reject a step and redirect PostHog AI instead.
