[PostHog Desktop](/docs/posthog-desktop) is where you review and merge the [Self-driving](/docs/self-driving) work that your product analytics data generates. Your insights don't just sit on a dashboard – a scout reads them, finds the ones that regressed, and hands concrete problems to a coding agent that opens a pull request.

## How a regression becomes a pull request

A [scout](/docs/self-driving/scouts) watches the funnel, retention, lifecycle, stickiness, and paths insights you've saved, and looks at the rates they produce rather than raw volume. Traffic being down is not a signal. A conversion step that used to convert at 40% and now converts at 22% is.

When it finds one, it files a [report](/docs/self-driving/reports) with the insight, the drop, and the events and [session replays](/docs/session-replay) behind it. If the cause looks like something in your code, the report carries a fix loop metric – the number the fix is supposed to move – and hands off to a coding agent that works in a sandbox against your connected repo, pushes a branch, and opens a draft pull request.

## What you can do here

- **Read the report** – the insight that regressed, when it started, and the evidence behind it.
- **Ask questions** – start a research task against the report to dig further before acting.
- **Create a PR** – auto-started when the fix is in a repo you've connected, or kick one off manually.
- **Watch the agent work** – follow the live transcript and review the files it changed.
- **Merge** – the pull request opens on GitHub, and PostHog reflects the merged state back.

Nothing ships without you. The merge happens on GitHub, so a human is always the last step.
