Product analytics helps you understand how people use your product. Use the events you capture to explore adoption, conversion, and retention with trends, funnels, paths, stickiness, and lifecycle insights. Save insights to dashboards, share them, and set alerts when metrics change.

Product analytics uses the same events, persons, and properties as the rest of PostHog. You can investigate a change in an insight with related [session replays](/docs/session-replay), see which [feature flags](/docs/feature-flags) users saw, or measure the results of an [experiment](/docs/experiments).
