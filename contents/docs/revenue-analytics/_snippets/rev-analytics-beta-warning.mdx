<CalloutBox icon="IconInfo" title="The Revenue analytics dashboard has been removed" type="info">

The dedicated Revenue analytics dashboard was deprecated and removed on **June 30th, 2026**. Revenue data is still available in PostHog — revenue properties (`$virt_revenue`, `$virt_mrr`) are now exposed directly on persons and groups, so you can use them in [product analytics insights](/docs/product-analytics), [SQL queries](/docs/hogql), and person/group profiles.

</CalloutBox>
