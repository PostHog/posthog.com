<CalloutBox icon="IconWarning" title="The revenue analytics dashboard has been removed" type="caution">

The revenue analytics dashboard was deprecated and removed on **June 30th, 2026**. Revenue data is now exposed directly on persons and groups via virtual properties, so you can use it everywhere: insights, SQL, and person/group profiles.

</CalloutBox>

## What replaced the dashboard?

Instead of a single, opinionated dashboard, PostHog now exposes revenue properties directly on persons and groups using the `$virt_revenue` and `$virt_mrr` virtual fields. This means you can:

- **Build custom insights** using [product analytics](/docs/product-analytics/insights) with revenue properties like `$virt_revenue` and `$virt_mrr` on persons or groups.
- **Query managed views directly** in the [SQL editor](https://app.posthog.com/sql) under the `views` tab for full flexibility.
- **View revenue on profiles** — person and group profile pages now show revenue data automatically.

## Using revenue data in product analytics

You can use revenue properties for deeper product analytics insights. This allows you to:

- Create custom revenue funnels
- Analyze user journeys that lead to purchases
- Segment users by revenue contribution

When working with revenue data in product analytics, you may want to use the [`convertCurrency` function](/docs/revenue-analytics/common-questions#can-i-query-converted-rates) for accurate reporting across different currencies.

We also expose streamlined views of your revenue data. You can find them under the `views` tab in our [SQL Editor](https://app.posthog.com/sql).
