<CalloutBox icon="IconWarning" title="Taggers are moving into evaluations" type="caution">

Taggers are being merged into [evaluations](/docs/ai-evals). New tagging should use an evaluation with a [categorical output](/docs/ai-evals#choosing-an-output-type). <!-- VERIFY with Bernat: timeline, and whether existing taggers migrate automatically -->

</CalloutBox>

Taggers label generations with categories you define, such as the product area or intent a conversation is about, so you can filter and group your LLM traffic by them.

Evaluations now do the same job. To label generations:

1. Go to **AI Evals** > **Evaluations** and click **Create evaluation**.
2. Choose **LLM as a judge**, or **Code-based (Hog)** if a deterministic rule can pick the label.
3. Set **Output type** to **Categorical**, and add a key and label for each category.
4. Choose **One** or **Multiple** categories per result.
