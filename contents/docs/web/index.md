The PostHog web app at [app.posthog.com](https://app.posthog.com) is the browser surface for your self-driving product, where you explore your data and steer the work.

It's one of [three surfaces](/docs/self-driving) where you interact with self-driving, alongside the [Slack app](/docs/slack) and [MCP](/docs/model-context-protocol).

## What you do here

- **Explore your product data** – dashboards, insights, funnels, session replays, and [SQL](/docs/sql).
- **Watch the signals that feed the loop** – errors, replays, experiments, and surveys become the [signals](/docs/self-driving#the-product-autonomy-loop) self-driving acts on.
- **Ask PostHog AI** – [analysis and answers on demand](/docs/posthog-ai), right inside the app.
- **Set the guardrails** – manage your data, access, and the project settings that decide what's automated and what needs a human.
