The inbox is where self-driving hands work back to you. [Reports](/docs/self-driving/reports) and the pull requests the loop opens both land here, ranked by priority, so the most important work rises to the top.

It's the human side of the [self-improving loop](/docs/self-driving/self-improving-loop): the loop watches, enriches, and proposes, and the inbox is where you review, steer, and decide.

## What lands in your inbox

- **Reports** – grouped [signals](/docs/self-driving/signals) framed as one problem, with the evidence behind them.
- **Pull requests** – the changes an agent opens for actionable reports.

Each item is ranked by priority, so you work top-down instead of triaging a flat list.

## What you do here

From the inbox you:

- **Review** a report and dig into the evidence.
- **[Ask AI](/docs/self-driving/reports#ask-ai-about-a-report)** a question about a report, with its evidence already in context.
- **Steer** the work: approve it, snooze it, or decline what self-driving proposes.
- **Merge** the pull requests you're happy with, the same as any other PR.

Nothing ships without you. The inbox is where you stay in control of what self-driving does.

Triage is also how you teach the loop. When you dismiss, snooze, or restore a report and say why, that note is [forwarded to the scout that filed it](/docs/self-driving/scouts#your-inbox-feedback-steers-scouts-too), so later runs read it before deciding what to surface. Explaining a bad report is the cheapest way to stop getting it – and restoring one you were wrong to suppress tells the scout that filing it was right.

## Where to find it

The inbox is available across surfaces – the [web app](/docs/self-driving/web), [PostHog Desktop](/docs/posthog-desktop/self-driving), and Slack – so you can review and act on work wherever you are. It's the same inbox everywhere; only the way you open it changes.

Self-driving is one shared setup, not a separate one per surface. Your [signal sources](/docs/self-driving/inbox/sources), [scouts](/docs/self-driving/scouts), [reports](/docs/self-driving/reports), and inbox state are the same whether you open PostHog Desktop or the web app – connect a source or tune a scout in one place and it takes effect everywhere.

To start filling it, connect your [signal sources](/docs/self-driving/inbox/sources).
