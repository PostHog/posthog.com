Web analytics gives you a simple dashboard of the numbers every website team looks at – visitors, pageviews, [sessions](/docs/data/sessions), bounce rate, top pages, and where your traffic came from. It's built for people who really liked GA3, without the setup tax.

Because it runs on the same events as the rest of PostHog, a traffic number is never a dead end. Click through from a landing page to the [session replays](/docs/session-replay) behind it, tie ad spend to conversions in [marketing analytics](/docs/web-analytics/marketing-analytics).

PostHog also sends traffic *out* on a schedule: a weekly digest of visitors, pageviews, sessions, bounce rate, top pages, and traffic sources arrives by email and as an in-app notification, so the numbers reach you without anyone opening the dashboard.

## Where its data comes from

Web analytics runs on the traffic your site already sends to PostHog. Pageviews and sessions come from the SDK, channels and campaigns are derived from each session's entry referrer and UTM tags, and marketing analytics adds cost data pulled from your ad platforms.
