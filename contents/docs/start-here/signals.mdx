A signal is a structured finding: something worth knowing, with the evidence behind it and a suggested action. Signals are the raw material of the [self-improving loop](/docs/start-here/self-improving-loop). Everything self-driving does starts from a signal.

## What's in a signal

A signal isn't only an alert. It carries:

- **The finding** – what's happening, stated concretely.
- **The evidence** – the data that backs it up.
- **A suggested action** – what could be done about it.

That structure is what lets the rest of the loop reason about a signal, group it with related ones, and act on it.

## Where signals come from

Signals come from two places:

- **[Scouts](/docs/start-here/scouts)** that watch your PostHog data on a schedule.
- **Connected sources**: error tracking, session replay, and health checks inside PostHog, plus external tools like Zendesk, GitHub Issues, and Linear.

A single problem usually shows up as several signals from several sources. The loop groups them so you act on the problem, not the noise.

## From signals to reports

On their own, signals are a stream. The loop deduplicates and enriches them, then groups related signals into a [report](/docs/start-here/reports) you can act on.
