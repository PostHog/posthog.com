Self-driving watches your product and proposes work without waiting to be asked. Guardrails are what keep that inside limits you set: what the loop can do on its own, what waits for your review, and what data it's allowed to touch.

The principle behind all of them is simple: self-driving is autonomy from having to ask, not autonomy from your judgment.

## Nothing ships without you

An agent drafts changes, but it never releases them. When a report clears the threshold you set, the agent opens a pull request in your [inbox](/docs/start-here/inbox), and you review it, request changes, and merge or decline it like any other PR. Nothing reaches production until a human merges it.

## What runs on its own, and what waits for you

The early stages of the [self-improving loop](/docs/start-here/self-improving-loop) run continuously, without asking. What the loop does once a report is actionable comes down to a threshold you set, and either way the change lands as a pull request you have to merge.

- **Automated by default:** watching your data, raising [signals](/docs/start-here/signals), grouping them into [reports](/docs/start-here/reports), opening a pull request for any report that clears your threshold, and re-checking whether a shipped change actually worked.
- **Up to you:** the threshold that decides what gets a pull request automatically, reviewing and merging those pull requests, acting on the reports that ask for your input, and any rollout that touches production.

You decide where that line sits for each project.

## Where the work happens

When a report clears your threshold, an agent plans and builds the change in a sandboxed environment, not against production. The change comes back as a pull request, so your existing branch protections, CI checks, and review rules apply to it exactly as they would to a teammate's work. Self-driving doesn't get a side door around the controls you already trust.

## What data self-driving can use

Because self-driving runs on your product data, it only operates once you've opted in to AI data processing. From there you control which sources feed the loop and, through [access controls](/docs/settings/access-control), who on your team can change how it's configured.
