<details>
  <summary>Unblock PostHog in your firewall or WAF (recommended)</summary>

  Your Web Application Firewall (WAF) can block the requests that PostHog makes to your site.

  For [heatmaps](/docs/toolbar/heatmaps), use the screenshot cookie. PostHog renders heatmap screenshots on a shared browser fleet with many rotating addresses, so an IP allowlist does not work for them. Instead, your project sends a secret `__ph_heatmap_render` cookie that your bot protection rule can match. See [bot protection and firewalls](/docs/toolbar/heatmaps#bot-protection-and-firewalls).

  An allowlist does not help when your app has a private address. For apps on an internal network, see [internal and intranet applications](/docs/session-replay/troubleshooting#internal-and-intranet-applications).
