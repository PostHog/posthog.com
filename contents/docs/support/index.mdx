Support gives you one inbox for every customer conversation, whether it arrives from your app, your email, or a GitHub issue. Because PostHog already knows what's happening in your product, each ticket comes with the sender's [session replay](/docs/session-replay), events, and errors attached, so you can see what went wrong instead of trying to reproduce it.

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/support/start-here">Get started</OSButton>

## Where you can use it

Read and reply to customers in the [Support inbox](/docs/support/inbox). You can also ask about support data in Slack, manage tickets over MCP, or build a custom experience with the API.

## Where tickets come from

Tickets reach your inbox through four [channels](/docs/support/concepts/channels). Each one syncs both ways, so you answer from a single place no matter where the conversation started.
