<CalloutBox icon="IconFlask" title="Private beta" type="action">

The AI agent is in beta, and it is not enabled for every project. If you do not see **AI agent** under **Support** > **Settings**, ask us to turn it on. It is free while it is in beta. We will set a price later, and we will tell you before any charge starts.

</CalloutBox>

The AI agent drafts a reply when a new ticket arrives. You decide whether that draft stays a private note for your team, or goes to the customer. It does not reply on its own until you choose **AI reply** for that channel and ticket type.

<Steps>

<Step title="Add Business Knowledge" badge="required">

The agent writes its replies from your [Business Knowledge](/docs/business-knowledge) sources. Most teams start with their docs and website. You can't turn the agent on until at least one source is ready, and a source is only ready after your organization approves [AI data processing](/docs/business-knowledge/ai-data-processing).

<OSButton variant="primary" asLink to="/docs/business-knowledge/start-here">Set up Business Knowledge</OSButton>

</Step>

<Step title="Turn on the agent" badge="required">

1. Go to [**Support** > **Settings** > **AI agent**](https://app.posthog.com/support/settings).
2. Turn on **Enable AI agent**.
3. Under **Allowed channels**, pick where the agent runs. A channel stays disabled until you connect it under **Channels**.

<Step title="Choose what the customer sees" badge="recommended">

Under **Reply behavior**, set a reply mode for each channel and ticket type. **Private note** is the default, and only your team sees it. Start there, and switch How-to tickets to **AI reply** once you trust the drafts. See [Reply modes](#reply-modes).

<Step title="Match your team's tone" badge="optional">

Add instructions to the **Support playbook**, such as tone, sign-offs, or topics to avoid. They sit on top of the default playbook. Safety rules and the agent's tools stay the same.

</Step>

<Step title="Let it investigate ticket data" badge="optional">

Turn on **Allow the agent to investigate ticket data**. When a ticket reports something broken, the agent checks your project's events, [error tracking](/docs/error-tracking) issues, [session replays](/docs/session-replay), and [logs](/docs/logs). It has read-only access, and only to that project. Leave it off to ground replies only in docs and Business Knowledge.

## Reply modes

| Ticket type | What you can choose | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How-to** | **Private note** or **AI reply** | The customer needs instructions. A reviewed reply can go out. |
| **Diagnostic** | Private note only | The draft can include data from your project. |
| **Account/Billing** | Private note only | The draft can include account data. |

**AI reply** sends a safety-reviewed message to the customer. On a channel set to **AI reply**, the agent may also ask the customer one clarifying question.

Diagnostic and Account/Billing stay private even if you would prefer a customer reply. The product enforces that. Do not paste a private note into the customer thread if it contains project data you would not send yourself.
