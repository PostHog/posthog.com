<CalloutBox icon="IconFlask" title="Open beta" type="action">

The AI agent is in beta, and it is not enabled for every project. If you do not see **AI agent** under **Support** > **Settings**, ask us to turn it on. It is free while it is in beta. We will set a price later, and we will tell you before any charge starts.

</CalloutBox>

The AI agent drafts a reply when a new ticket arrives. You decide whether that draft stays a private note for your team, or goes to the customer. It does not reply on its own until you choose **AI reply** for that channel and ticket type.

Replies are grounded in your [business knowledge](/docs/business-knowledge) sources: documents, links, and other context you add.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Support** > **Settings** > **AI agent**.
2. Turn on **Enable AI agent**.
3. Confirm AI data processing is allowed for your organization.
4. Add at least one business knowledge source and wait until it is ready.

<ProductScreenshot
    imageLight="https://res.cloudinary.com/dmukukwp6/image/upload/w_1600,c_limit,q_auto,f_auto/Screenshot_2026_10_20_at_17_28_16_52dfe7e34c.png"

Without consent and a ready source, the agent has nothing to ground a reply in.

## Let it investigate the ticket

Under **Investigate ticket data**, the **Allow the agent to investigate ticket data** switch is separate, and it starts off.

When it is on, a ticket that reports something broken can be checked against your project's events, [error tracking](/docs/error-tracking) issues, [session replays](/docs/session-replay), and [logs](/docs/logs). The agent has read-only access, and only to that project.

Leave it off if you want replies grounded only in documentation and business knowledge.

## Choose the channels

Under **Allowed channels**, pick where the agent runs. A channel that is not connected yet stays disabled until you enable it under **Channels**. The list is the widget (including the API), Slack, email, Microsoft Teams, and GitHub.

## Choose what the customer sees

For each channel you enabled, set a reply mode per ticket type.

| Ticket type | What you can choose | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How-to** | **Private note** or **AI reply** | The customer needs instructions. A reviewed reply can go out. |
| **Diagnostic** | Private note only | The draft can include data from your project. |
| **Account/Billing** | Private note only | The draft can include account data. |

**Private note** is the default. Only your team sees it. **AI reply** sends a safety-reviewed message to the customer. On a channel set to **AI reply**, the agent may also ask the customer one clarifying question.

Diagnostic and Account/Billing stay private even if you would prefer a customer reply. The product enforces that. Do not paste a private note into the customer thread if it contains project data you would not send yourself.
