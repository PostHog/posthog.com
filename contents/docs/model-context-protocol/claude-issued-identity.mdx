With Claude-issued identity, Claude tells PostHog which member of your Claude organization is signed in. An admin connects PostHog once, and members can use the PostHog MCP server in Claude without signing in to PostHog themselves. You don't need an identity provider or SSO.

This is one of the identity sources for [enterprise-managed authorization](/docs/model-context-protocol/enterprise-managed-authorization). If your organization signs in to Claude through Okta, use [Okta Cross App Access](/docs/model-context-protocol/okta-cross-app-access) instead.

<CalloutBox icon="IconStar" title="Scale and Enterprise add-ons" type="action">

Enterprise-managed authorization is available to organizations on the Scale or Enterprise [platform add-on](/platform-packages).

</CalloutBox>

## Before you start

- **In Claude:** a Team or Enterprise plan, and the **Owner** role in your Claude organization. Only owners can set up managed authorization for a connector.
- **In PostHog:** the Scale or Enterprise platform add-on, and admin access to your PostHog organization.
- **Matching email addresses.** PostHog connects each Claude member to the PostHog user with the same email address. A member works only if they already have a PostHog account in your organization under the email they use in Claude. PostHog does not create accounts, so [invite members](/docs/settings/organizations#adding-new-members) or provision them with SSO or [SCIM](/docs/settings/authentication/scim) first.

## 1. Copy the issuer URL from Claude

1. In Claude, go to **Organization settings → Connectors** and select the **PostHog** connector.
2. On the **Configuration** tab, click **Set up** next to **Managed authorization**.
3. Under **Identity source**, choose **Claude-issued**.
4. Copy the **Issuer URL**. It looks like `https://identity.anthropic.com/issuers/<id>` and is unique to your Claude organization.

Leave this screen open. You come back to it in step 3.

If the PostHog connector has no **Managed authorization** option, it isn't available for PostHog in your Claude organization yet. [Contact us](/talk-to-a-human).

## 2. Trust the issuer in PostHog

1. In PostHog, go to [**Organization settings → Authentication**](https://app.posthog.com/settings/organization-authentication).
2. Under **Authentication domains**, add and verify your email domain if it isn't verified yet. See the [SSO docs](/docs/settings/authentication/sso) for the steps.
3. Find **XAA authentication** and click **Configure**.
4. Paste the issuer URL from Claude into **Identity provider issuer URL**.
5. Leave **JWKS URL** and **Allowed client IDs** empty. PostHog finds Claude's signing keys from the issuer URL.
6. Under **Domains**, choose the domain your Claude members use.
7. Click **Save configuration**.

## 3. Test and enable in Claude

1. Back in Claude, leave **Tenant ID (optional)** empty. PostHog picks your organization from the issuer URL and the member's email domain.
2. Click **Test connection**. Claude shows which step failed, if any, and what PostHog returned.
3. Click **Enable and continue**.
4. Choose which roles get the PostHog connector. All roles get it by default.

If Claude lets you choose scopes for the connection, start with the [recommended scopes](/docs/settings/authentication/id-jag#which-scopes-should-i-grant). They're the ones the PostHog MCP server grants by default when someone connects with OAuth. [API scopes](/docs/api/scopes) explains what each one covers.

## What members see

The next time a member uses PostHog in Claude, Claude connects for them. They don't see a PostHog sign-in prompt. Each connection acts as that member in PostHog, with their own permissions, limited to the granted scopes.

## Revoking access

- **For one member in Claude:** removing them from your Claude organization stops new tokens. A token already issued stays valid for up to 2 hours.
- **For one member in PostHog:** removing them from your PostHog organization, or deactivating them, stops their access on the next request.
- **For everyone:** in Claude, choose **Disable** under the connector's **Managed authorization**, or delete the XAA configuration in PostHog.

## Troubleshooting

| What Claude's test shows | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| `invalid_grant` (`ID-JAG could not be verified`) | The issuer URL in PostHog doesn't match the one Claude shows, or the member's email domain isn't selected under **Domains**. |
| `invalid_grant` (`ID-JAG sub is not an active member…`) | The member's Claude email doesn't match a member of your PostHog organization. |
| `access_denied` | Your PostHog organization doesn't have the Scale or Enterprise add-on. |
| `invalid_client` | Claude couldn't authenticate to PostHog. [Contact us](/talk-to-a-human). |
| `insufficient_scope` (from the MCP server) | The connection is missing `user:read`, `organization:read`, `project:read`, or a scope a tool needs. |

For every error code and what PostHog checks, see the [ID-JAG reference](/docs/settings/authentication/id-jag).
