Workflows comes with a free tier every month, then you pay for what you send. No credit card to start.

| | Free each month | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Emails** | 10,000 | From $0.003 per email |
| **Push and other destinations** | 10,000 dispatches | From $0.00075 per dispatch |

Prices drop as you send more. See [Workflows pricing](/workflows/pricing) for the tiers and a calculator.

## What counts

- **Every email:** transactional, broadcast, and automation emails.
- **Every dispatch:** push notifications, webhooks, Slack messages, SMS, and other destinations. A push that reaches no device isn't billed.

## Things billed elsewhere

- **Engagement and conversion events** count toward your [product analytics](/docs/product-analytics/pricing) event usage. Turn engagement events off any time.
- **AI steps,** such as Replay Vision, are billed by their own product. See [AI steps](/docs/workflows-next/automations/ai-steps).

## Stay in control

Set a [billing limit](/docs/billing/limits-alerts) so you never get a surprise. Over it, sends return `quota_limited`, and nothing goes out until the next period or until you raise the limit.
