The test sender lets you try Workflows before you set up your domain. It sends from a PostHog address, and only to people in your PostHog organization.

## Who gets the email

Only active members of your PostHog organization with a verified email address. The from name is your organization's name, then "via PostHog".

To email anyone else, such as your users, [set up your domain](/docs/workflows-next/domain).

## Where you can use it

It shows as **PostHog test sender** on the **Channels** tab.

- **Automation email steps:** pick it as the step's sender.
- **Transactional emails from your code:** when your project has no verified sender, emails your code writes go out from the test sender.

## What it can't do

- A custom from name or address, a reply-to, CC, or BCC.
- Attachments.
- UTM tags on links, or turning off open and click tracking.
- Real traffic. It has small daily caps per project and per recipient.

## Move to your own domain

1. [Set up your domain](/docs/workflows-next/domain), and click **Add your own sender** on the **Channels** tab.
2. Switch each email step that uses the test sender to your sender.

Emails from your code switch on their own as soon as you have a verified sender. You don't change your code.
