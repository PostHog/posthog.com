Automations can do more than email. Post to your team's Slack, text your users, or call any system. Set each one up once on the [**Channels** tab](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/channels). Only project admins can add a channel.

## Slack

Post a message to a Slack channel from an automation, such as when a big customer signs up. A Slack channel can also [start an automation](/docs/workflows-next/automations/triggers).

## SMS

Send text messages through Twilio. Click **New channel**, pick **Twilio**, and enter:

| Field | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| **Account SID** | [Twilio Console](https://help.twilio.com/articles/14726256820123-What-is-a-Twilio-Account-SID-and-where-can-I-find-it-) |
| **Auth Token** | [Twilio Console](https://help.twilio.com/articles/223136027-Auth-Tokens-and-How-to-Change-Them) |

Then add an **SMS** step to an automation.

## Webhooks and destinations

A **Webhook** step calls any URL, such as your own API, with the person's and event's data. Save what it returns as a variable for later steps. See [steps](/docs/workflows-next/automations/steps#use-results-in-later-steps).

You can also send to any of PostHog's [destinations](/docs/data-pipelines/destinations), such as a CRM or an ad platform, right from an automation step.

**WhatsApp** works this way: add the **WhatsApp** destination as a step, and it sends an approved message template through Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. You need your own WhatsApp Business account.
