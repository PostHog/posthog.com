Every marketing email needs a way out. It's the law in most places, and someone who can't find the link clicks "Report spam" instead, which hurts your [deliverability](/docs/workflows-next/channels/deliverability).

## Add the link

In the [email editor](/docs/workflows-next/email-editor), add the **Unsubscribe link** block, usually in the footer. Or use the variable anywhere:

```liquid
<a href="{{ unsubscribe_url }}">Unsubscribe or change what you get</a>
```

`{{ unsubscribe_url }}` opens your preferences page, where the person picks which kinds of email to stop, or stops them all.

## One-click unsubscribe

Gmail, Yahoo, and other mailbox providers show their own **Unsubscribe** button next to your sender name. `{{ unsubscribe_url_one_click }}` powers it: one click, and the person is unsubscribed from all marketing email, with no page to load. Large senders need it to reach Gmail and Yahoo inboxes.

## What happens next

- The person is opted out of that [message category](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules#opt-outs), or of everything, and PostHog skips them from then on.
- PostHog records a `$workflows_email_unsubscribed` event, with the `category` they left and the `source`: `one_click` or `preferences_page`.
- The unsubscribe shows on the broadcast or automation's metrics.

## Transactional emails

Transactional emails, such as receipts and password resets, don't need an unsubscribe link, because people need them. They skip opt-outs, so never use them for marketing.
