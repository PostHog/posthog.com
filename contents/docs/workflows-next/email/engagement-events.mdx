Turn on engagement events, and every send, open, click, bounce, and unsubscribe becomes a PostHog event on the person. Then you can use them like any other event: in funnels, cohorts, insights, and automation triggers.

## Turn them on

Go to [**Settings** > **Workflows** > **Engagement events**](https://app.posthog.com/settings/environment-workflows#workflows-engagement-events), and turn on **Capture email engagement events**. It takes a couple of minutes, and doesn't fill in the past.

The events count toward your event usage. Turn them off any time, and you still get [metrics](/docs/workflows-next/metrics).

## Events

| Event | When |
| --- | --- |
| `$workflows_email_sent` | PostHog handed the email to the email provider. |
| `$workflows_email_delivered` | The person's mail server accepted it. |
| `$workflows_email_opened` | The person opened it. Needs open tracking. |
| `$workflows_email_link_clicked` | The person clicked a link. Needs click tracking. |
| `$workflows_email_bounced` | It bounced. |
| `$workflows_email_blocked` | The person marked it as spam. |
| `$workflows_email_failed` | It wasn't sent. |
| `$workflows_email_unsubscribed` | The person unsubscribed. |
| `$workflows_email_tracking_consent_updated` | The person changed their tracking consent. |
| `$workflows_conversion` | The person reached an automation's [conversion goal](/docs/workflows-next/automations/triggers#conversion-goal-and-exit). Recorded even with engagement events off. |

## Properties

| Property | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `$workflow_id` | The broadcast or automation that sent it |
| `$workflow_action_id` | The email step |
| `$email_to` | The recipient |
| `$email_subject` | The subject, left out for [sensitive](/docs/workflows-next/transactional/sensitive-data) emails |
| `$email_tracking_enabled` | Whether the send had tracking, on sent and failed events |
| `$link_url` | The link, on clicks |

Unsubscribe and consent events are recorded on the email address, not one send. Their distinct ID is the email address, and they have `category` or `status`, and `source`, but no workflow ID.

## Ideas

- **Did the email work?** A funnel from `$workflows_email_delivered` to your own event, such as `password_reset_completed`.
- **Who's ignoring you?** A cohort of people with no `$workflows_email_opened` in 90 days, to leave out of broadcasts.
- **Follow up on a click.** An automation triggered by `$workflows_email_link_clicked` on your pricing link.
