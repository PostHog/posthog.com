People don't want all your email, or none of it. Message categories let them pick: leave your newsletter, and still get product updates and receipts.

## Message categories

On the **Opt-outs** tab, click **New category**. Each one has:

- **A name**, such as "Newsletter".
- **A public description**, which people see on the [preferences page](/docs/workflows-next/email/unsubscribes), such as "Tips and stories, once a month".
- **A type:** **marketing** or **transactional**.

Make the categories you actually send, then pick one on each broadcast and automation email. PostHog skips anyone who opted out of it.

## Marketing or transactional

| | Marketing | Transactional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **For** | Newsletters, product updates, offers | Receipts, password resets, security alerts |
| **Opt-outs** | Respected | Skipped, because people need these |
| **Frequency cap** | Counts | Doesn't count |
| **Suppression list** | Any entry blocks it | Only a hard bounce blocks it |

Use transactional only for emails a person needs. Sending marketing as transactional breaks the law in most places, and gets you reported as spam.

## The opt-out list

Under each category, and under **Marketing opt-out list** for people who left all marketing email, you see who opted out and when. People get there by:

- Clicking unsubscribe, in an email or in their email app. See [unsubscribes](/docs/workflows-next/email/unsubscribes).
- Changing their choices on the preferences page.
- You adding them, with **Add opt-out** or **Import CSV**.
- An import from another email provider. See [move from another provider](/docs/workflows-next/migrate).

**Export CSV** downloads the list.

## From your own systems

If your product has its own email settings, keep them in sync with the [messaging preferences API](/docs/api/messaging-preferences): list a category's opt-outs, add them one at a time or 1,000 at once, and remove one when someone opts back in. Leave out the category to work with the opt-out from all marketing email.

Opting someone back in to one marketing category also lifts their opt-out from all marketing, but their other categories stay off.
