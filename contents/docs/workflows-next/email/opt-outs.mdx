People don't want all of your email, or none of it. Message categories let them choose: they can leave your newsletter, and still get product updates and receipts.

## Message categories

In [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/opt-outs), go to **Messaging** > **Opt-outs**, and click **New category**. Fill in:

- **Name**, such as "Newsletter".
- **Key**, such as `newsletter`. The API uses it. You can't change it later.
- **Message type:** **Marketing** or **Transactional**. See below.
- **Public description**, which people see on the [preferences page](/docs/workflows-next/email/unsubscribes#the-preferences-page), such as "Tips and stories, once a month".

Then pick a **Message category** on each broadcast and automation email step. PostHog skips everyone who opted out of that category, or out of all marketing email.

## Marketing or transactional

| | Marketing | Transactional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **For** | Newsletters, product updates, offers | Receipts, password resets, security alerts |
| **Opt-outs** | Respected | Ignored, because people need these |
| **[Frequency cap](/docs/workflows-next/email/frequency-cap)** | Counts | Doesn't count |
| **[Suppression list](/docs/workflows-next/email/suppression)** | Any entry blocks it | Only a hard bounce blocks it |

Use transactional only for emails a person needs. Sending marketing as transactional breaks the law in most places, and gets you reported as spam.

## See who opted out

The **Marketing opt-out list** shows who left all marketing email, and when. Expand a marketing category to see who left only that one. Transactional categories have no opt-outs.

People get on a list when they:

- Click unsubscribe, in an email or in their email app. See [unsubscribes](/docs/workflows-next/email/unsubscribes).
- Change their choices on the preferences page.

You can also add people yourself:

- **Add opt-out** adds one address.
- **Import CSV** adds many. The file needs an `email` column, and can have a `category_key` column.
- **Import from another tool** brings opt-outs over from your old email provider. See [move from another provider](/docs/workflows-next/migrate).

**Export CSV** downloads the list.

## From your own systems

If your product has its own email settings, keep them in sync with the [messaging preferences API](/docs/api/messaging-preferences). You can list a category's opt-outs, add them one at a time or up to 1,000 at once, and remove one when someone opts back in. Leave out the category to work with the opt-out from all marketing email.

When you opt someone back in to one marketing category, PostHog also lifts their opt-out from all marketing email. Their other marketing categories stay off.
