Landing in the inbox comes down to three things: a domain that's set up correctly, a clean sending record, and email people want. PostHog runs the sending infrastructure. You bring the domain and the list.

## What PostHog handles

- **No IP to warm up.** Email goes out through Amazon SES on shared IP addresses that already carry steady traffic.
- **Transactional email goes first.** It never waits for your allowance, and goes out ahead of marketing email.
- **Marketing grows with your record.** Your [sending allowance](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts/large-audiences#your-sending-allowance) starts small and grows as you send clean email.

All projects in a region share one Amazon SES account, so one bad list can hurt everyone. That's why PostHog judges and limits each project on its own record.

## Watch your sending health

The **Reputation** tab, under **Messaging**, shows your bounce and spam complaint rates over the last 30 days: for the project, by mailbox provider, and by workflow. See [sending health](/docs/workflows-next/metrics#sending-health).

| Rate | Keep it under |
| --- | --- |
| Bounce rate (hard bounces) | 2% |
| Spam complaint rate | 0.1% |

A few sends don't decide a rate. The bounce rate counts from 200 emails, and the complaint rate from 1,000 emails or 3 complaints.

## When something goes wrong

- **Your tier drops.** If your rates over the last 7 days are over a line, your project drops one tier, at most once a week. It climbs back the same way it [climbed the first time](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts/large-audiences#your-sending-allowance).
- **One workflow can pause.** PostHog can pause email from one workflow whose rates spike. Sends from it return `sending_paused`.
- **Sending can be suspended.** PostHog or Amazon can stop all email from a project. Sends return `sending_suspended`, and a banner asks you to contact support. When sending starts again, the project starts again at tier 0.

PostHog also puts hard-bounced addresses and spam complaints on your [suppression list](/docs/workflows-next/email/suppression), so you don't email them again.

## Keep your rates low

- **Send only to people who asked:** signups, customers, and opt-ins. Never a bought or scraped list.
- **Leave the suppression list alone.** Retrying a bounced address only bounces again.
- **Make unsubscribing easy.** Add an [unsubscribe link](/docs/workflows-next/email/unsubscribes) to every marketing email. Someone who can't find it clicks "Report spam" instead.
- **Use a sender people know.** The same name and from address every time.
- **Write a subject that matches the email.** No all caps, and no "RE:" on emails that aren't replies.
- **Send steadily.** A weekly email to active people builds your record. A blast to a list that hasn't heard from you in months damages it.
- **Drop people who never engage.** Send to a [cohort](/docs/data/cohorts) of active people, not everyone.

Before a big send, send a test to your own address and to a personal Gmail or Outlook account. If it lands in spam for you, it will for them. Check **Reputation** the next day, because complaints arrive hours later.

## Keep marketing away from transactional

1. **Pick a transactional [category](/docs/workflows-next/email/opt-outs#marketing-or-transactional)** for emails people need. They go out first and never wait.
2. **Use a subdomain per stream,** such as `news.example.com` for marketing and `account.example.com` for transactional. A complaint spike on marketing then stays on the marketing subdomain.
3. **Use a project per stream** for full separation. Each project has its own tier, allowance, and suspension.

## Questions

**Can I get a dedicated IP?** No. A dedicated IP only builds a reputation with large, regular volume, and [Amazon recommends shared IPs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/dedicated-ip.html) for everyone else.

**Can I use my own SMTP server or email provider?** No. You bring the domain, and PostHog sends through Amazon SES.

**Can I get a higher allowance sooner?** If the **Sending allowance** card on the **Reputation** tab shows **Request a higher allowance**, click it. It opens a support request with your sending record filled in.

**Does this apply to push, SMS, or webhooks?** No. Only email has a sending reputation and allowance.

**Moving from another provider?** See [move from another provider](/docs/workflows-next/migrate).
