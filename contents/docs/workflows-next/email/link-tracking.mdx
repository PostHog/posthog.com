PostHog counts opens and clicks by adding a tiny image to each email and sending its links through a redirect. You choose where that happens, and you can tag links so your web analytics know where visitors came from.

## Opens and clicks

Every email step and broadcast has **Track opens and link clicks**, on by default. Turn it off for emails that should never be tracked. With it off, PostHog still records deliveries, bounces, and unsubscribes, but not opens or clicks.

Opens and clicks only become events when [engagement events](/docs/workflows-next/email/engagement-events) are turned on.

## Leave one link alone

Some links break when they go through a redirect, such as app deep links and universal links. Add `data-ph-no-track` to the link in a **Custom HTML** block:

```html
<a href="https://example.com/open-in-app" data-ph-no-track>Open the app</a>
```

The link keeps its address, and its clicks aren't counted.

## Sensitive emails

Emails with sign-in codes or reset links shouldn't send their links through a redirect at all. Mark them as sensitive. See [sensitive data and tracking](/docs/workflows-next/transactional/sensitive-data).

## Tracking consent

Some countries need a person's consent before you track their opens and clicks. In [**Settings** > **Workflows** > **Email tracking consent**](https://app.posthog.com/settings/environment-workflows#workflows-email-tracking-consent), pick:

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **No consent enforcement** | Track by each email's setting. The default. |
| **Opt-out** | Track, except people who said no. |
| **Opt-in** | Track only people who said yes. |

People change their choice on the preferences page. It only applies to marketing email, and an email with tracking off is never tracked.

## UTM tags

Turn on **Add UTM tags to links**, and PostHog adds `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` to every link, so [web analytics](/docs/web-analytics) shows which email sent each visitor. Leave a field empty to use the value shown, such as the email's name.

Click **Save as team default**, and new emails start with the same values.
