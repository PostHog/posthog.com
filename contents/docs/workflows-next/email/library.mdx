The **Library** keeps the emails your team reuses: your welcome email, your monthly update, your brand's header and footer. Start any broadcast, automation email, or transactional email from one.

## Save a template

On the **Library** tab, click **New template**, name it, and write it in the [email editor](/docs/workflows-next/email-editor). Or save an email you already wrote as a template from its menu.

The Library shows each template as a preview, so you can find it by looking. Search by name, or filter by who made it.

## Use a template

In any email, click **Start from template**, and pick one. You get a copy, so editing it doesn't change the template, or anything else that uses it.

## Templates for automations

You can also save a whole automation as a template, with its trigger and steps. Pick who sees it:

- **Project:** just this project. The default.
- **Organization:** every project in your organization.

See [templates and suggestions](/docs/workflows-next/automations/templates).

## From your editor

Your coding agent can write templates for you over [MCP](/docs/workflows-next/mcp), in the same format as the editor, so you can keep editing them by hand:

- "Write a welcome email for new signups, with our logo and a big Get started button."
- "Change the button on our welcome email to say Get started."
