PostHog imports your Mailchimp audiences, groups, cleaned addresses, abuse reports, templates, and campaigns in one background import.

> The Mailchimp import is rolling out. If Mailchimp isn't in the list, your project doesn't have it yet.

## What moves

| From Mailchimp | In PostHog |
| --- | --- |
| Audiences | Marketing [categories](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules#opt-outs). People who unsubscribed, never subscribed, or were archived are opted out. |
| Groups | Categories. Hidden groups stay behind. |
| Cleaned addresses | [Suppression list](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules#suppression-list). Blocks all email. |
| Abuse reports | Suppression list. Blocks marketing email. |
| Saved templates | Templates, with merge tags converted to Liquid |
| Campaigns | Draft templates. None of them send. |

Customer journeys, classic automations, signup forms, reports, and Mailchimp Transactional (Mandrill) stay in Mailchimp. Rebuild journeys as [automations](/docs/workflows-next/automations).

## Run the import

1. Create an API key in [Mailchimp](https://us1.admin.mailchimp.com/account/api/). It ends with your data center, such as `-us21`. Mailchimp keys can change everything in your account, so PostHog only reads, and deletes the key when the import ends.
2. In [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows), go to **Opt-outs** and click **Import from another tool**.
3. Pick **Mailchimp** and paste the key. Under **Campaigns**, pick drafts, paused, and scheduled campaigns, add recently sent ones, or import every campaign.
4. Pick what to import, and click **Start import**.

## Switch your sending

- **Merge tags:** `*|FNAME|*` becomes the `first_name` person property, and other tags become the property with the same name. Make sure your people have them.
- **Images:** Mailchimp keeps hosting the images in your emails. Upload them to PostHog before you close your account.
- **Before you delete an audience:** import once more. After that, PostHog can't see who unsubscribed.

See [switch over safely](/docs/workflows-next/migrate#switch-over-safely) for the full checklist.
