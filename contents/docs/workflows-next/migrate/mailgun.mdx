PostHog imports your Mailgun bounces, complaints, unsubscribes, mailing lists, and templates across all your domains, in one background import.

> The Mailgun import is rolling out. If Mailgun isn't in the list, your project doesn't have it yet.

## What moves

| From Mailgun | In PostHog |
| --- | --- |
| Bounces | [Suppression list](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules#suppression-list). Blocks all email. |
| Complaints | Suppression list. Blocks marketing email. |
| Unsubscribes | Opt-outs. An unsubscribe from a tag becomes its own category. |
| Mailing lists, and who left each | Marketing [categories](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules#opt-outs) and opt-outs |
| Templates, Handlebars or Go | Templates, converted to Liquid |
| Domains and allowlists | A list, so you know what to set up |

Mailgun has no automations. Routes, webhooks, tracking settings, send history, and stats stay in Mailgun.

## Run the import

1. Create an account API key in [Mailgun](https://app.mailgun.com/settings/api_security), with the **Support** role if you have it. A domain sending key can't read your data, and an Analyst key can't read suppressions. PostHog deletes the key when the import ends.
2. In [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows), go to **Opt-outs** and click **Import from another tool**.
3. Pick **Mailgun** and paste the key. Pick your **Mailgun region**: US, EU, or both. PostHog only sends your key to the region you pick.
4. Pick **Template versions**: the active version only, or every version as its own template. Then pick what to import, and click **Start import**.

## Switch your sending

- **Unsubscribe footer:** Mailgun adds one to some domains automatically. PostHog doesn't, so add an unsubscribe link to your marketing emails.
- **Variables:** pass what you sent as `t:variables` or `recipient-variables` as `variables`.
- **Preferences page:** people see your imported list and tag names, so rename any that read badly.

See [switch over safely](/docs/workflows-next/migrate#switch-over-safely) for the full checklist.
