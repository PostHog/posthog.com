Sent email can't be unsent, so check it in a real inbox first.

## Send a test

On the **Review** step, send a test to your own address. Read it in your inbox, on desktop and on your phone, and click its links. Nobody in the audience gets a test.

For a push broadcast, the test goes to your phone.

## Launch

Click **Send now**, or **Schedule broadcast**. The broadcast checks its sender, content, and audience size first, and tells you what to fix.

## After you send

The broadcast shows who was in the audience and why some didn't get it, then sends, deliveries, opens, clicks, and conversions. See [metrics](/docs/workflows-next/metrics#broadcasts).

## Fix or resend it

A sent broadcast can't change. **Send again as a new broadcast** copies it, so you can fix it and send it to a new audience.

## Turn it into an automation

To add waits, branches, or a follow-up email, click **Open in workflow editor**. Once you add steps, the broadcast is read-only here, and you keep editing it as an [automation](/docs/workflows-next/automations).

## Archive it

Archive a broadcast you don't need from its menu in the list. You can restore an archived broadcast as a draft, or delete it. You can't archive one while it's sending.
