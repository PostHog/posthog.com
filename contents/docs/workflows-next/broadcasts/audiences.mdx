A broadcast's audience comes from the same people as the rest of PostHog, so you can target by anything you track: plan, signup date, what they did, or a cohort.

## Filter people

On the **Recipients** step, click **Add condition** and filter by:

- **[Person properties](/docs/data/persons):** such as `plan` equals `pro`.
- **[Cohorts](/docs/data/cohorts):** such as people who used a feature in the last 30 days.

A person must match every condition. With no conditions, **Send to everyone** sends to your whole project.

The step shows **This broadcast will reach** about how many people, before you send. The list updates at send time, so people who match by then get it too.

## Upload a list

Click **Upload a list** and drop in a CSV of people. PostHog saves it as a static cohort, so you can reuse it in other broadcasts.

## Let PostHog suggest one

On the **Broadcasts** tab, **Audiences you could send to** suggests audiences from your events, such as people who signed up but never uploaded a file. Each card shows the rule, the reason, and about how many people it reaches. **Draft the email** creates the broadcast with that audience, and PostHog AI writes the first draft.

Suggestions are in beta, and need a [sender on your own domain](/docs/workflows-next/domain).

## Who doesn't get it

PostHog skips people before it sends:

- **One email per address.** People who share an email address get one email.
- **No email address.** People without one are skipped.
- **Opted out.** People who opted out of the broadcast's **Message category**, or of all marketing email. See [opt-outs](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules#opt-outs).
- **Suppressed.** Addresses that bounced or complained. See the [suppression list](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules#suppression-list).
- **Over the frequency cap.** People who already got their share of marketing email. See the [frequency cap](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules#frequency-cap).

After the send, the broadcast's results count each group, so you can see why someone didn't get it.

## Audiences that are too big

Each project can send to an audience up to a size set by its [sending allowance](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts/large-audiences). Over it, PostHog won't send. Add filters, or limit the sending rate.
