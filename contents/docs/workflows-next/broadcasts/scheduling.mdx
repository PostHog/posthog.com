Send a broadcast now, at a time you pick, or on a repeating schedule. The **Calendar** tab shows it next to your other planned sends.

## Pick when it sends

On the **Schedule** step, pick one:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Send now** | Sends as soon as you launch it. |
| **Send later** | Sends once, at a date, time, and time zone you pick. |
| **Recurring** | Sends on a repeating schedule, such as every Monday at 9am. |

## Pick a day that isn't crowded

With **Send later**, a month view shows your other marketing sends. Days that would give some people in this audience too many marketing emails show as **Too close to other sends**. PostHog suggests the first day that fits. Click **Use this day** to take it.

The rule comes from your [frequency cap](/docs/workflows-next/email/frequency-cap). Without a cap, PostHog suggests one marketing email per person every 2 days.

## Set a recurring schedule

Type the schedule in plain English, such as "every week on Monday and Wednesday", or set it with the controls:

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Every** | A number of days, weeks, months, or years |
| **On** | For weekly: the days of the week. For monthly: a day of the month, a weekday such as the 3rd Wednesday, or the last day. |
| **Ends** | **Never**, **On date**, or **After** a number of sends |

Check the next send times before you continue. Each send goes to whoever matches the audience at that time. The broadcast's **Runs** tab lists every send, with its results.

## Plan on the calendar

The **Calendar** tab on **Broadcasts** shows scheduled broadcasts and scheduled workflows that send marketing email, plus past sends. Drafts, transactional emails, and event-triggered workflows don't show. Switch between **Month** and **Week**, and filter to **Broadcasts** or **Workflows**.

- **Start from a day:** hover a day, click **+**, and pick **New broadcast** or **New workflow**.
- **Move a send:** drag a scheduled send to another day. For a recurring send, this moves every future send by the same number of days. Click **Undo** on the message to put it back.

## Change or stop a scheduled broadcast

Open the broadcast, click **Actions**, then **Stop and edit**. It goes back to a draft, and nothing sends until you launch it again. This works for a one-time broadcast that hasn't sent, and for a recurring broadcast that has sends to come. Sends that already went out stay as they are.

You can't stop a broadcast while it's sending.
