Send a broadcast now, at a time you pick, or on a repeating schedule. The send calendar shows it next to everything else going out.

## When it sends

On the **Schedule** step, pick one:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Send now** | Sends as soon as you launch it. |
| **Send later** | Sends once, at a date and time you pick. |
| **Recurring** | Sends on a schedule, such as every Monday at 9am. |

Each one with a time has a time zone.

## Recurring broadcasts

Type the schedule in plain English, such as "every week on Monday and Wednesday", and check the next send times before you launch. Or set it with the picker:

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Frequency** | Daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly, with an interval such as every 2 weeks |
| **Weekly** | The days of the week |
| **Monthly** | A day of the month, a weekday such as the 3rd Wednesday, or the last day |
| **Ends** | Never, on a date, or after a number of sends |

Each send picks up whoever matches the audience at that moment. **Runs** lists every send, with its results.

## The send calendar

The calendar shows every broadcast and automated email side by side, so the same people don't get two marketing emails in two days.

- **Start from a day:** click a day, and pick **New broadcast** or **New workflow**.
- **Move a send:** drag a scheduled broadcast to another day.
- **Avoid doubling up:** when you schedule, PostHog warns you if a broadcast on the same thing is already scheduled or sending.

## Change or stop it

- **Scheduled:** click **Stop and edit**. It goes back to a draft, and you launch it again when you're done.
- **Recurring:** change the schedule the same way. Sends that already went out stay as they are.
