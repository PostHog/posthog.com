title: Set up your domain
---

PostHog sends to your users only from a domain that you verified. Transactional email, broadcasts, and automations all use the same senders, so you set up your domain once.

## Try it first with the test sender

You can send before you touch DNS. The **PostHog test sender** sends from a PostHog address, as your organization's name followed by "via PostHog". It delivers only to members of your PostHog organization, and it has a daily cap. Use it to test, not for real traffic.

Pick it as the sender in an email step, or send from your code before your domain is verified. When your domain is verified, sends from your code move to it at once. You don't change your code.

## Add your sender

You need to be a project admin to add a sender.

1. On the [**Channels**](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/channels) tab, click **New channel** and select **Email**.
2. Enter your domain, then the sender name and address that people see in their inbox.
3. Add the DNS records that PostHog shows:
   - **Cloudflare or Vercel:** click the button, and approve the change at your host.
   - **Another host:** copy each record into your host, such as Route 53 or Namecheap.
   - **Your coding agent:** hand it the setup. It adds the records and checks PostHog until the domain is ready.

DNS changes can take a few minutes. You see a green check mark when the domain is verified. Every other sender on the same domain is then verified too.

## The DNS records

Copy each value from PostHog, not from this page. The tokens are unique to your domain.

| Record | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DKIM | CNAME | Signs your email, so inboxes can check that it came from your domain. |
| SPF | TXT | Lists the servers that can send for your domain. |
| Custom MAIL FROM, usually `feedback.yourdomain.com` | MX and TXT | Sends bounces back to PostHog. |
| DMARC | TXT | Tells inboxes what to do with email that fails the checks. |

## Already sending from this domain?

Add PostHog's records next to your old provider's. Both can send from the same domain, so you can move one email at a time. See [move from another provider](/docs/workflows-next/migrate#switch-over-safely).

A new project starts with a small daily allowance for marketing email, which grows as you send with low bounce and spam rates. Transactional email never waits for it. See [sending reputation and allowance](/docs/workflows-next/channels/deliverability).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Domain still pending | Some DNS hosts add your domain to the end of the name, so `feedback.yourdomain.com` becomes `feedback.yourdomain.com.yourdomain.com`. Enter only the part before your domain. |
| No address in the **From** menu | Add a sender on the [**Channels**](https://app.posthog.com/workflows/channels) tab first. |
| Emails go to spam | Check that DKIM and SPF pass, and add the DMARC record. |
