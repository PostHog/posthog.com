A broadcast sends one email or push notification to a group of people, once or on a schedule. Use it for announcements, product updates, and newsletters.

Open **Broadcasts** in [PostHog](https://app.posthog.com/broadcasts) and click **New broadcast**.

## Start from a suggested audience

The **Broadcasts** page can suggest audiences from your events, such as people who signed up but never created a project. Each suggestion shows about how many people it reaches. Click **Draft the email**, and PostHog AI writes it in the style of your last broadcast or a saved template. You check it before it sends.

Suggestions are in beta, and need an email sender on your own [domain](/docs/workflows-next/domain). See [let PostHog suggest an audience](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts/audiences#let-posthog-suggest-one).

## Create a broadcast

A new broadcast has five steps. PostHog saves it as a draft as you go.

### 1. Recipients

Pick **Email** or **Push notification**. Then filter by [person properties](/docs/data/persons) or [cohorts](/docs/data/cohorts), or click **Upload a list** to upload a CSV. PostHog saves an uploaded list as a static cohort that you can use again. With no filters, the broadcast goes to everyone.

The step shows about how many people the broadcast reaches. The list updates at send time, and people who share an email address get one email.

Pick a **Message category**, so people can [opt out](/docs/workflows-next/email/opt-outs) of this kind of email. See [audiences](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts/audiences).

### 2. Goal

This step is optional. Click **Set a conversion goal** and pick what counts as a conversion, such as the event `subscription_started`. PostHog counts the recipients who do it after the email.

### 3. Content

Pick a sender, write a subject, and write the email in the [email editor](/docs/workflows-next/email-editor), or click **Start from template**. **Track opens and link clicks** and **Add UTM tags to links** are below the email.

For a push broadcast, see [push broadcasts](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts/push).

### 4. Schedule

Pick **Send now**, **Send later**, or **Recurring**. For a recurring broadcast, type the schedule in plain English, such as "every Monday at 9am", and check the next send times.

Broadcasts follow your [frequency cap](/docs/workflows-next/email/frequency-cap). For an email that must reach everyone on time, such as an event invite, turn on **Send even if people reached your frequency cap**. For a large audience, turn on **Limit sending rate** to spread the send over time. See [scheduling](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts/scheduling) and [large audiences](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts/large-audiences).

### 5. Review

Check each step, and send a test to yourself. Nobody in the audience gets the test. Then click **Send now** or **Schedule broadcast**. You can't unsend an email after it goes out. See [test and send](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts/test-and-send).

## Plan on the calendar

The **Calendar** tab shows your scheduled broadcasts and scheduled automations side by side, so the same people don't get two marketing emails too close together. Click a day to start a broadcast or an automation on it, or drag a scheduled send to a new day.

## After you send

The broadcast's **Overview** shows how many emails were sent, delivered, opened, and clicked, and how many people converted. The **Runs** tab lists each send of a recurring broadcast. See [broadcast metrics](/docs/workflows-next/metrics#broadcasts).

To change a scheduled broadcast, click **Stop and edit**. It goes back to a draft.

## When you need more than one email

A broadcast is an automation with one email step. To add waits, branches, or a second email, click **Open in workflow editor** and build it out as an [automation](/docs/workflows-next/automations).

To create and send broadcasts from your own code, see the [broadcasts API reference](/docs/api/hog-flows).
