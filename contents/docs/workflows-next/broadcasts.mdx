A broadcast sends one email or push notification to a group of people, once or on a schedule. Use it for announcements, product updates, and newsletters.

Open **Broadcasts** in [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows) and click **New broadcast**.

## Let PostHog pick the audience

PostHog looks at your events and suggests audiences you could send to, such as people who signed up but never created a project. Each suggestion shows its rule and about how many people it reaches. Click **Draft the email**, and PostHog AI writes it in the style of your last broadcast or a saved template. You check it before it sends.

Suggestions are in beta, and need a [sender on your own domain](/docs/workflows-next/domain).

## Create a broadcast

A new broadcast has five steps. PostHog saves it as a draft as you go.

### 1. Recipients

Pick **Email** or **Push notification**. Then filter by [person properties](/docs/data/persons) or [cohorts](/docs/data/cohorts), or **Upload a list** as a CSV. An uploaded list saves as a static cohort that you can reuse. With no filters, the broadcast goes to everyone.

The step shows how many people the broadcast reaches. The list updates at send time, and people who share an email address get one email.

Pick a **Message category** so people can [opt out](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules) of this kind of email.

### 2. Goal

Optional. Pick the event that counts as a conversion, such as `subscription_started`. PostHog counts the recipients who do it after the email.

### 3. Content

Pick a sender, write a subject, and build the email in the [email editor](/docs/workflows-next/email-editor), or start from a template.

### 4. Schedule

Pick **Send now**, **Send later**, or **Recurring**, such as every Monday at 9am. Type a recurring schedule in plain English and check the next send times.

For a large audience, turn on **Limit sending rate** to spread the send over time. Broadcasts follow your [frequency cap](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules) unless you turn on **Send even if people reached your frequency cap**.

### 5. Review

Check everything, and send a test to yourself. Nobody in the audience gets the test. Then click **Send now** or **Schedule broadcast**. Sent emails can't be unsent.

## After you send

The broadcast shows how many emails were sent, delivered, opened, and clicked, and how many people converted. **Runs** lists each send of a recurring broadcast.

To change a scheduled broadcast, click **Stop and edit**. It goes back to a draft.

## Broadcasts and automations

A broadcast is an automation with one email step. To add waits, branches, or a second email, click **Open in workflow editor** and build it out as an [automation](/docs/workflows-next/automations).
