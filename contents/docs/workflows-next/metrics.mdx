Every email you send shows how it did: sent, delivered, opened, clicked, bounced, and marked as spam. Because it runs on the same events as the rest of PostHog, you also see what people did next.

## Transactional emails

The **Metrics** tab counts sent, delivered, opened, and clicked emails: for all transactional emails, by email, or by sending domain.

## Broadcasts

A sent broadcast shows who was in the audience and why some didn't get it: opted out, suppressed, over the frequency cap, or no email address. Then it shows sends, deliveries, bounces, unsubscribes, opens, clicks, and conversions, activity over time, and the top clicked links. A recurring broadcast lists each send under **Runs**. See [broadcasts](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts#after-you-send).

## Automations

An automation's **Overview** tab shows:

- **Conversions:** how many people reached your goal. Click **View converted people** to see who.
- **Step reach:** how many people got to each step, and where they dropped off.
- **Message leaderboard:** each email and push step, ranked by opens and clicks.
- **In the workflow now:** who is partway through, and which step they wait on.
- **Health:** failed steps, hard bounces, and spam complaints, with a link to the logs.

The **Metrics** tab charts the same numbers over time, for the whole automation or one step. Each email also lists clicks for each link.

## Sending health

The **Reputation** tab shows your bounce rate and spam complaint rate, overall and by mailbox provider, and your sending allowance. It flags problems early, before they can pause your sending. See [sending reputation](/docs/workflows/sending-reputation).

## Use the events anywhere

Each open, click, bounce, and unsubscribe is a PostHog event, such as `$workflows_email_opened`. Use them in [insights](/docs/product-analytics/insights), [funnels](/docs/product-analytics/funnels), and [cohorts](/docs/data/cohorts), like any other event.

Opens and clicks need [engagement events](/docs/workflows/engagement-events) turned on.

Over [MCP](/docs/workflows-next/mcp), ask "Which of our automations had the most unsubscribes last month, and why?" Your agent reads the same numbers.
