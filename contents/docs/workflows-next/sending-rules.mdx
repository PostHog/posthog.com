Before PostHog sends a message, it checks three rules: what the person opted out of, whether their address is on the suppression list, and how many marketing messages they got recently. The rules apply to every broadcast and automation.

Find the rules on the **Messaging** tab of the Transactional, Broadcasts, or Workflows app in [PostHog](https://app.posthog.com/workflows).

## Opt-outs

Opt-outs are grouped into **message categories**, so someone can leave your newsletter and still get product updates. Each category is **marketing** or **transactional**, and people can opt out of marketing categories only. Pick a category on each broadcast and email step, and PostHog skips the people who opted out of it.

People opt out with one click in their email app, or on the preferences page that `{{ unsubscribe_url }}` links to. The **Opt-outs** tab lists the categories and who opted out. You can add opt-outs by hand, import them from a CSV, or click **Import from another tool**.

## Suppression list

An address goes on the **Suppression list** when an email to it hard bounces, it soft bounces again and again, the person marks an email as spam, or you add it by hand. PostHog stops emailing it, which protects your [sending reputation](/docs/workflows-next/metrics#sending-health).

Only a hard bounce blocks transactional email, so a password reset still reaches someone who marked a newsletter as spam. If a bounce was a one-off, remove the address from the list.

## Frequency cap

The frequency cap limits how many marketing messages one person gets across every broadcast and automation, such as 2 in 7 days. Set it on the **Settings** tab:

- **Messages per person** and **Within this many days** set the cap.
- **Hold capped messages for up to (hours)** keeps a message that is over the cap waiting for a free slot, instead of skipping it.

Transactional emails never count toward the cap. For a message that people need on time, turn on **Skip the frequency cap** on its automation step, or **Send even if people reached your frequency cap** on a broadcast. Opt-outs still apply. See [frequency cap](/docs/workflows-next/email/frequency-cap).

## From your own systems

The [API](/docs/workflows-next/api) and [MCP](/docs/workflows-next/mcp#sending-rules-tools) manage opt-outs, categories, and the suppression list, so your own preferences page or support tools can update them.
