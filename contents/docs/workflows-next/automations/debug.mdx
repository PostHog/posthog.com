Test each step before anyone gets an email, then follow every run once it's live.

## Test a step

Open **Test**, pick a sample person or event, and run a step. PostHog shows what it would do, such as the email it would send, without sending it to anyone. Send a test email to your own address to check it in a real inbox.

<!-- shot: automation-test -->

## Follow every run

**Invocations** lists every run: who it was for, its status, and when it started. Filter by status, such as **Running** or failed. Open a run to see each step it took, and its logs, such as why a step was skipped.

<!-- shot: automation-invocations -->

Each email a run sent also shows on its own, with its timeline and what the person did around it. See [metrics](/docs/workflows-next/metrics#what-each-person-did).

## Stop runs

On **Invocations**:

- **Cancel** stops one run before its next step.
- **Cancel in-flight runs** stops every run at once.
- **Stop** on a batch run stops it entering more people, and cancels the runs it started.

A run that's mid-step finishes that step first. Emails already sent can't be recalled. A cancelled run keeps its logs, and you can **Rerun** it later.

## Common problems

- **Nobody entered.** Check the trigger's filters, and that the event arrives: the trigger shows how many matched in the last 7 days.
- **An email didn't send.** Open the run's logs. It might be the person's opt-out, a suppressed address, the [frequency cap](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules#frequency-cap), or a missing email address.
- **A variable is empty.** The person doesn't have the property, or the step that sets it ran after they passed. Add a default, such as `{{ person.properties.first_name | default: 'there' }}`.
- **It won't turn on.** PostHog checks every step first, and shows which one needs fixing.
- **No sender in the email step.** Set up your domain first. See [set up your domain](/docs/workflows-next/domain).
