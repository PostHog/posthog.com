You don't have to start from a blank canvas.

## Start from a template

Click **New workflow**, and pick a template, such as a welcome series or a trial reminder. It opens as a draft with its steps in place. Change the trigger, emails, and timing to fit, then turn it on.

<!-- shot: automation-templates -->

## Save your own

Save any automation as a template, so your team can reuse it. Pick who sees it:

- **Project:** just this project. The default.
- **Organization:** every project in your organization.

## Let PostHog suggest one

PostHog looks at your events and suggests automations worth building, such as a welcome email for a signup event. Build one, and it opens as a draft for you to check.

Your coding agent can do the same over [MCP](/docs/workflows-next/mcp): "Suggest an automation from our events, and build it as a draft."

## Ask PostHog AI

Describe the automation in plain English, such as "Email new signups who haven't created a dashboard after 2 days", and PostHog AI builds it as a draft. This is rolling out, so your project might not have it yet.
