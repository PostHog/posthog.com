In about 10 minutes, you build an onboarding automation. It welcomes new signups, waits up to 2 days for them to upload their first file, and nudges the people who don't.

## Before you start

- **Set up your domain**, or use the [test sender](/docs/workflows-next/email/test-sender) for now. See [set up your domain](/docs/workflows-next/domain).
- **Capture events** for what people do, such as `signed_up` and `uploaded_file`, and identify people with their email. See [installation](/docs/workflows-next/installation#capture-events-from-your-app).

## 1. Pick the trigger

Open [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows), click **New workflow**, and pick **Blank workflow**. Click the trigger step, set **Trigger type** to **Event**, and add the `signed_up` event.

Under **Frequency**, pick **One time per period**, then change the period to **ever**. Each person gets the welcome series only once.

## 2. Set the goal

On the same trigger step, go to **Conversion goal**. Under **Detect conversion from events**, add `uploaded_file`. PostHog now counts how many people convert.

To take people out of the automation when they convert, set **Exit condition** to **Exit when conversion goal is met**.

## 3. Send the welcome email

Click **+** below the trigger, and pick **Email**. Pick a sender, write the subject, and write the email in the [email editor](/docs/workflows-next/email-editor). To greet people by name, use `{{ person.properties.first_name | default: 'there' }}`.

## 4. Wait for the first upload

Add a **Wait until** step after the email. Under **Events to wait for**, add `uploaded_file`. Set **Max time to wait** to 2 days.

The step has two paths: one for people who upload, and one for people who reach the time limit.

## 5. Nudge the people who didn't upload

On the time-limit path, add a second **Email** with a short tip about how to upload a file. Leave the other path empty, so it goes to the exit.

## 6. Test it

Open the **Test** tab, and click **Start testing**. Pick a sample person, click **Run test**, then **Go to next step** to move through the automation. By default, nothing sends: PostHog logs what each step would do.

To see an email in a real inbox, select the email step, turn on **Make real HTTP requests**, and enter your own address. PostHog asks you to confirm before it sends.

## 7. Turn it on

Save the automation, then click **Enable**. Nothing sends until you enable it. After that, everyone who signs up goes through it.

## What's next

- See how it performs on the **Overview** and **Metrics** tabs. See [metrics](/docs/workflows-next/metrics#automations).
- Let PostHog suggest improvements with [self-driving](/docs/workflows-next/self-driving).
- Learn every option for [triggers](/docs/workflows-next/automations/triggers) and [steps](/docs/workflows-next/automations/steps).
