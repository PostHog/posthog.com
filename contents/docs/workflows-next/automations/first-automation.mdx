In about 10 minutes, build an onboarding automation. It welcomes new signups, waits up to 2 days for them to upload their first file, and nudges the ones who don't.

## Before you start

- **Set up your domain**, or use the test sender. See [set up your domain](/docs/workflows-next/domain).
- **Capture events** for what people do, such as `signed_up` and `uploaded_file`, and identify people with their email. See [installation](/docs/workflows-next/installation#capture-events-from-your-app).

<!-- shot: automation-canvas -->

## 1. Pick the trigger

Open [Workflows](https://app.posthog.com/workflows) and click **New workflow**. Click the trigger step, pick **Event**, and choose `signed_up`.

Under **Frequency**, pick **One time per period**, then **ever**, so each person only gets the welcome series once.

<!-- shot: automation-trigger-event -->

## 2. Send the welcome email

Click **+** under the trigger, and pick **Email**. Pick a sender, write the subject, and write the email in the [email editor](/docs/workflows-next/email-editor). Use `{{ person.properties.first_name | default: 'there' }}` to greet them by name.

## 3. Wait for the first upload

Add a **Wait until** step. Wait for the `uploaded_file` event, for at most 2 days.

The step has two exits: one for people who uploaded, and one for people who hit the time limit.

<!-- shot: automation-wait-until -->

## 4. Nudge the people who didn't

On the time-limit exit, add a second **Email** with a short tip on how to upload a file. Leave the other exit going straight to the end. They've already done it.

## 5. Set the goal

Click the trigger step again. Under **Conversion goal**, pick the `uploaded_file` event. PostHog now counts how many people convert, and you can take people out of the automation once they do.

## 6. Test it

Click **Test**, and run the email step on a sample person. PostHog shows the email it would send, without sending it to anyone. Send a test to your own address to see it in a real inbox.

<!-- shot: automation-test -->

## 7. Turn it on

Click **Save**, then turn the automation on. Nothing sends until you do. From now on, everyone who signs up goes through it.

## What's next

- Watch how it does on the **Overview** tab. See [metrics](/docs/workflows-next/metrics#automations).
- Let PostHog suggest improvements with [self-driving](/docs/workflows-next/self-driving).
- Add more steps. See [triggers](/docs/workflows-next/automations/triggers) and [steps](/docs/workflows-next/automations/steps).
