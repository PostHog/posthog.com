The [PostHog web app](https://app.posthog.com/workflows) is where you manage your transactional emails, send broadcasts, plan them on a calendar, write emails, and build automations. Every email sits on the same events and people as the rest of PostHog, so you can go from a send to the [session replay](/docs/session-replay) of what the person did next.

Open **Workflows** in [PostHog](https://app.posthog.com/workflows) to start a broadcast or an automation.

## Before you start

1. **Set up your domain:** [verify the domain](/docs/workflows-next/domain) that you send from, or try it first with the test sender.
2. **Capture your users:** [install PostHog in your app](/docs/workflows-next/installation#capture-events-from-your-app), so broadcasts and automations know who to email and when.

## What you can do here

- **[Manage transactional emails](/docs/workflows-next/transactional):** your code sends them, and the **Transactional** tab manages them. Create templates, read the log of every send with what the person did next, watch delivery metrics, and forward email events to a webhook.
- **[Send broadcasts](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts):** email many people at once. Pick an audience, or one that PostHog suggests, and PostHog AI drafts the email.
- **[Plan on the send calendar](/docs/workflows-next/broadcasts#plan-it-on-the-calendar):** see every broadcast and automated email side by side, and stop the same people getting two marketing emails in two days.
- **[Write emails](/docs/workflows-next/email-editor):** use the block editor, save templates to the library, or ask PostHog AI for the first draft.
- **[Build automations](/docs/workflows-next/automations):** start on something a person does, then wait, branch, and send email, push, or a Slack message to your team.
- **[Let automations improve themselves](/docs/workflows-next/self-driving):** PostHog watches how each step performs and suggests changes. You approve them.
- **[See how every send did](/docs/workflows-next/metrics):** sends, opens, clicks, bounces, and conversions for each broadcast and automation.
- **[Set your sending rules](/docs/workflows-next/sending-rules):** opt-out categories, the suppression list, and a frequency cap that every send follows.
