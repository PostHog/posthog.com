### Q4 2026 objectives

<details>
<summary>Give paid ads and creative teams faster access to performance data (Brian)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Build reporting by platform, campaign, and creative into PostHog so we can see what is working throughout the month. Give paid ads and creative teams a shared view of results so we can adjust spending, improve creative, and plan future tests without waiting for the growth review. Build it so we can also add in data from our non-paid marketing efforts too.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Use fewer ad formats and build a stronger pipeline of brand and product ads (Brian)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Focus on reusable formats that create and test more creative assets for the brand and products. Rank ads against their intended objectives, increase investment in strong performers, and use what we learn to guide the next round of creative. Product ads should explain the problems users face and how PostHog solves it. Brand ads should connect those products to PostHog's role as the context layer for your product.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Turn customer and creator content into effective paid ads (Brian)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Help more people hear why PostHog is useful from people who use PostHog. Find and turn specific posts, content, and recommendations into ads, then use targeting and measurement to find the audiences and messages that work best and double down.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Build the affiliate program (Joe)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Incentivize creators to feature PostHog and send good users to us by making it clear we want to be featured, and that we're willing to pay for good content. We'll incentivise content that shows how PostHog makes developers' lives better.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Decide whether a team sponsorship is worth pursuing (Charles)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Everybody at PostHog has been asking for it, especially F1. What would it take to end up on Lando Norris' helmet? Is it possible for us?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Roll out a new suite of product ads with structured testing (Jonah)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Develop eight variations per product across different customer problems, copy, and art directions. Use content and ads reporting to learn which content and messaging resonate with our target audience. Show what each product helps customers do and how its data gives developers and their agents more context.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Launch the Q4 context-layer brand campaign (Jonah)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Create a video and static campaign showing how bringing analytics, errors, replays, and business data together helps people and their agents understand what is happening, find problems, and decide what to change. Use concrete examples to make PostHog's role as the context layer for your product clear, and test which messages communicate that value best.

</details>
