### Q4 2026 objectives

1. **Make PostHog web simple again**
    - Interview users in our ideal customer profile (ICP).
    - Define what "simple" means for these users. Do not optimize for every user.
2. **Understand and improve PostHog Desktop**
    - Interview PostHog Desktop users.
    - Learn whether PostHog Desktop is confusing. Learn why it is confusing. Learn what users want to see.
    - Build it. Fix it. Do it. Fax it. Rename it.
3. **Create a design system**
    - Define where buttons go, what remains hidden, and what happens when a user selects the close button.
