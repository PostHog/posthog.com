<summary>Automations be automating</summary>

**Driver:**  <TeamMember name="Carol Donnelly" photo /> <TeamMember name="Milagros Pedriel" photo /> <TeamMember name="Tara Howard" photo />

**Motivation:** We've spent a lot of time on data and capturing what workflows should look like but we still spend time in external tooling to trigger workflows or check items have been completed. Bringing this inside the Ops Platform will significantly reduce context switching and manual effort

**What we'll ship:**
- Onboarding automation completeness; Provisioning, Contracts, checklist auto-completing
- Birthday Blobby, New Hire Kits, Employment Checks, Laptops
- Offboarding worklow in Ops platform
- Org changes are self-serve; team, team lead and title changes, approvals, updates flowing to Deel, Slack, GitHub and Google

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- All integrated tool workflows execute from inside the Ops platform & success/failures are reported back and checklists are updated
- No more manual work for items like Birthday Blobby, Employment Checks and Issuing laptops
- The Small Team Offsite Planner supports more of the offsite lifecycle with less manual intervention.
- Offboardings are logged in Ops platform and workflows are visible
