# Q4 Quarterly Goals

<details>

<summary><strong>Goal: Hire approximately 35 people (focus on high and medium priority roles)</strong></summary>

**Drivers:** Coua, Ellie, Julia, Mark, Rune, Zbynek

**Motivation:** Meeting our hiring goals will enable our small teams to get one step closer to achieving their objectives, helping the company continue to grow.

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- We have achieved our hiring goal of approximately **35 hires**, with High and Medium Priority roles ranking highest. See the [proposed hires page](https://ops.posthog.dev/proposed-hires) for the current hiring plan.
- We have successfully managed to close the high priority technical customer success manager roles.
- We continue to keep our time-to-offer under 30 days.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Goal: Maximize sourcing efforts for technical customer success managers by increasing top of funnel and leveraging Ashby + agents</strong></summary>

**Drivers:** Julia, Eliana, Coua, and Mark

**Motivation:** We want to be able to source, resurface and revisit candidates over time, without sacrificing finding new talent. This also helps us build better habits of nurturing candidates across all roles.

**We'll know we're successful when:**

- We have successfully built in an Ashby workflow to tag, alert, and sequence candidates in the proper project folders.
- We begin generating qualified candidates through these automations, and those candidates convert into the interview process.
- We have been able to keep the number of hires for technical customer success managers consistent MoM through this effort.
- The process becomes repeatable and scalable for future roles.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Goal: Increase referrals quality and quantity by educating the PostHog team and enabling them to be confident referrers</strong></summary>

**Driver:** Julia, Rune, and Eliana

**Motivation:** Increase the amount of high-dense talented referrals from our internal team's network. This will strengthen the top of the funnel, resulting in a higher probability of close more roles this quarter.

**We'll know we're successful when:**

- Educate the wider team on what good looks like by continuing on the scorer tool that has already been built by Eliana.
- When #project-lets-fucking-hire-them has served its purpose and we're able to place 1-2 of those talented individuals somewhere at PostHog.
- Successfully collaborate with hiring teams for ongoing content and blog to encourage referrals and non-referrals to apply.
- Content results in qualified applicants for roles across GTM and engineering; with an emphasis to focus on technical customer success managers.

</details>
