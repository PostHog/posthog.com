## Q4 2026 objectives

**North star (kept from Q3): make shipping 10,000 PRs a month easy.** Q3 made CI faster and cheaper, made the merge queue load-bearing, and shipped StampHog v2. In Q4 the bottleneck is getting work reviewed and merged, and keeping a fast-growing codebase manageable.

### CI optimization (<TeamMember name="Georges-Antoine Assi" photo />, everyone contributes)

Most of the repo-wide levers are pulled. Next is moving the p90, not just the p50: team- and author-specific fixes where CI got worse, all required checks on Depot CI, and more dynamic CI.

### Merge queue stability (<TeamMember name="Georges-Antoine Assi" photo />)

Less CI rework from flaky tests. Own lanes for teams doing isolated work.

### Product foundation system (<TeamMember name="Julian Bez" photo />)

Build your product in the standard shape and you get the fast lane: scoped CI, its own queue lane, StampHog approval with less human review. One golden path that bundles product isolation, MCP, code generation, ingress/egress, and guardrails.

### StampHog + ReviewHog (<TeamMember name="Julian Bez" photo />)

ReviewHog provides the PR review experience across the repo, replacing paid review bots. StampHog stays a separate service and gets stricter about what it approves. To explore: cheaper local reviews on developer subscriptions.

### Test growth (<TeamMember name="Raúl Negrón-Otero" photo />)

Tests grow faster than anything else in the repo. Measure which tests earn their keep, starting with the teams that own the most, and stop adding tests that add no coverage.

### DevEx simplicity (<TeamMember name="Raúl Negrón-Otero" photo />, <TeamMember name="Julian Bez" photo />)

Fewer tools and fewer bots on PRs, so developers' attention goes to the PR itself. Smaller, readable CI workflows that other engineers can safely extend.

### AgentEx (<TeamMember name="Georges-Antoine Assi" photo />)

You don't think about it, your agent does it. Skills, tools, and visibility so agents handle reviews, queue ejections, and flakes on their own.

### Code quality and PR experience (<TeamMember name="Julian Bez" photo />)

How agents keep code quality up locally and on PRs, and a light helper that tells people why a PR is stuck, without taking over from their own agent.

**How we'll hear from teams:** team-specific numbers (merge times, flakes) posted in each team's channel, with a poll or a 15-minute slot to talk.

**Not this quarter:** in-house flaky test quarantining (Trunk keeps it, revisit in Q1), and new local dev environment work.
