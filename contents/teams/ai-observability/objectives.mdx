### Q4 2026 objectives

#### Objective 1: Finish the UI redesign (Carlos Marchal)

**Motivation:** Every tab in the core tracing product should help users find issues with their AI agents and features. The redesign will make these tabs more useful and provide a consistent experience across the product.

**What we'll ship:**

- **UI redesign** – redesign all tabs in the core tracing product around finding issues with AI agents and features, with consistent navigation and interactions

#### Objective 2: Roll out offline evals (Radu Raicea)

**Motivation:** Offline evals shipped internally in Q3. We now need to roll them out to external users, iterate on their feedback, and reach general availability.

**What we'll ship:**

- **External rollout and iteration** – roll out offline evals to external users and improve the experience based on their feedback
- **Billing and data retention** – add billing support and apply data retention policies to offline evaluation data
- **General availability** – bring offline evals to general availability after the external rollout and iteration

#### Objective 3: Multimodal general availability (Carlos Marchal)

**Motivation:** Multimodal ingestion and trace views are available. We need to make them generally available with clear limits and pricing, and support large text content too.

**What we'll ship:**

- **General availability** – finalize the cap and pricing for multimodal content
- **Large text content** – extend multimodal storage and ingestion support to large text inputs and outputs

#### Objective 4: Query and loading optimization

**Motivation:** Slow queries and loading times make investigations frustrating. We need improvements in both the UI and the underlying queries.

**What we'll ship:**

- **UI optimizations** – improve loading performance in the UI (Carlos Marchal)
- **Query performance investigation** – investigate lower-level speedups for slow queries (Radu Raicea)

#### Objective 5: Useful clusters (Radu Raicea)

**Motivation:** Clusters need to surface useful, actionable findings, and their cost needs to make sense for the product.

**What we'll ship:**

- **Opt-in clusters** – make clustering opt-in
- **Customizable summarization prompts** – give teams control over how their traces are summarized for clustering
- **Richer cluster centroids** – enrich centroids with evaluation results and show how they evolve over time
- **Pricing exploration** – explore pricing that makes clustering sustainable

#### Objective 6: Online evals improvements (Bernat Torres)

**Motivation:** Teams need evaluations that go beyond boolean results, work with multimodal content, and make the results easy to understand.

**What we'll ship:**

- **More output types** – support categorical, multi-categorical, and numerical evaluation results
- **Multimodal evaluations** – evaluate multimodal content
- **Results reporting** – improve the UI for reporting evaluation results

#### Objective 7: Use case / agent primitive (Bernat Torres)

**Motivation:** Teams need to distinguish between the different use cases and agents in their product to understand their behavior.

**What we'll ship:**

- **Use case / agent primitive** – introduce a way to organize AI Observability data by use case or agent
