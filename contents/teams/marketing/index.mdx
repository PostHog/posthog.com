You can find out more about what the marketing team does in the handbook. On Slack we use [the #team-marketing channel](https://posthog.slack.com/archives/C08CG24E3SR) for most things.

If something needs marketing attention, there are several group handles you can use on Slack.

- `@all-marketers` notifies this team, the Wizard & Docs team, the Editorial team, the YouTube team, and James
- `@marketing-team` notifies the team members on this page specifically
- `@product-marketers` notifies the product marketers on this page specifically

The marketing team isn't responsible for maintaining the website. For that, please refer to [the website team](/teams/website).
