### Q4 2026 objectives

### **Commercial universe dataset** – (Driver: <TeamMember name="Abhischek Thottakara" />)

Build one materialized table of all active and churned customers, so that lead triggers use the same, complete account context.

**What we'll ship:**

* `org_universe` table that joins billing (invoices, usage momentum, credits, and startup plan eligibility), account ownership and segments, Salesforce and third-party enrichment, and PostHog usage (events and enabled features)
* Daily CRON in lead-gateway that queries `org_universe` and creates lead tasks
* TAM lead triggers that fire only for TAM-owned accounts
* Migrate existing lead triggers to query `org_universe` instead of separate HogQL queries

### **Trusted lead score** – (Driver: <TeamMember name="Abhischek Thottakara" />)

Make lead scores meaningful, so that GTM Humans trust them and get fewer noisy tasks.

**What we'll ship:**

* Compare Q3 lead scores with qualification and revenue outcomes
* Skip tasks that GTM Humans consistently disqualify, and weight scores by task outcomes
* Account potential score v0: estimate ACV from the median ACV of accounts with similar usage and company data
* Improve the account potential score: compare predicted ACV with 30, 60, and 90-day growth
