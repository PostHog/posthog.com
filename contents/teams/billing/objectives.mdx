### Make billing the source of truth

_Theme: Own credits, products, prices, and subscription behavior so billing can support PostHog's needs with fewer constraints from external providers._

**2. Migrate all credits to the in-house ledger (lead: <TeamMember name="Pawel Cebula" />)**

-   Seed credit pools from the new revenue model and reconcile them with customer balances. Fix or explicitly accept differences before cutover.
-   Move all credits onto live ledger, applying promotional credits through discounts and prepurchased credits through customer balance, with the correct tax treatment.
-   Rebuild refunds around ledger transactions and ship the operational tooling needed to manage live credits.
-   Integrate credit ledger transactions into the new revenue model so credit usage and refunds are reflected correctly in revenue reporting.

**3. Make the new product catalog the source of truth for products and prices (lead: <TeamMember name="Roy Cohen" />)**

-   Build a catalog with at least parity with today's product and pricing setup, with requirements checked with the teams that need it.
-   Sync the catalog to Stripe and integrate it wherever `billing` currently uses the old catalog.
-   Run existing pricing configurations through it in production without changing customers' billing unexpectedly. Integration with existing subscriptions can ship before the new subscription engine is ready.

**4. Run our own subscriptions in shadow mode for all Stripe subscriptions (lead: <TeamMember name="Daniel Snitkovskiy" />)**

-   Compare recurring billing, prorations, and invoice outcomes against Stripe for all subscriptions.
-   Define a migration plan, whether and how old and new subscriptions can coexist, and the cutover criteria.

Rollout needs v2 or equivalent usage data to avoid a latency regression. Alex's reporting work supplies and improves that foundation. Real-time enforcement via counters is a separate objective under objective 6.

**6. Ship real-time usage reporting and limiting (lead: <TeamMember name="Alexander Mays" />)**

-   Real-time limiting: Implement limit enforcement using Valkey counters and a new real-time usage service, and integrate it with the products that need it.
-   Disambiguate caps, limits, allowances, and allocations.

### Control abuse and unpaid usage
