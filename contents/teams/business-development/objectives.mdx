### Q4 2026 objectives

<details>
<summary>Every BDR hits their SQO target (Lorena, Andreas, Melad)</summary>

- **Rationale:** 3 SQOs per BDR per month is the floor.
- **We'll know we're successful when:** every BDR consistently books a minimum of 2 meetings a week.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Deliver scored accounts to BDRs instead of having them build lists (Mitch, with Abhischek)</summary>

- **Rationale:** BDRs spend too much time deciding who to reach out to. Accounts should be scored on fit and timing, delivered through Lead Gateway into the tool reps work from, and every accept, reject and outcome measured so the score gets better over time.
- **What we'll ship:** a v0 account score built on Org Universe, scored accounts delivered to each BDR, and a way to see which accounts went to which rep and what happened next.
- **We'll know we're successful when:** BDRs work accounts they're given rather than lists they build, and we can show which kinds of accounts turn into meetings.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Write down how we work, from first touch to handoff (Mitch, with Alex and Lorena)</summary>

- **Rationale:** We don't have one documented way of doing the job. Lorena's workflow works, the handoff to TAEs was agreed in Austin, and both need to live somewhere everyone can follow and link to.
- **What we'll ship:** a BDR playbook and the BDR to TAE handoff process in the handbook, plus the Salesforce changes the handoff needs.
- **We'll know we're successful when:** a new BDR can read it on day one and know how to do the job, and every meeting is handed off the same way.

</details>
