## What we do

* Start conversations with teams that are a great fit for PostHog, whether they're already using it or haven't found it yet
* Get people to value faster by connecting them with the right products, docs, and people at PostHog
* Speed up growth by creating pipeline for our account executives and handing it over with the full picture
* Try new ways to reach people, and share what works with the rest of the company

## Who we work with

* New Business and Product-led Sales, who run the deals we create
* GTM Engineering, who build the data and tools we prospect with
* Marketing, so what we say matches what we ship

## Principles

**Lead with value hypothesis** – every conversation starts with a specific reason PostHog can make someone's product better.

**Speed matters** – the sooner someone sees what PostHog does for them, the sooner they grow with us.

**Experiment in the open** – we run new approaches as short tests with a clear metric, share the results in our team channel, and keep what works.
