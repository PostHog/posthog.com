## What we do

We're product evangelists. We start conversations with teams we think could get value from PostHog, and help them find it faster and with less effort, in a way that's useful and interesting.

The teams we talk to range from AI-native startups shipping every day to large, traditional companies running a digital product across lots of teams and tools, and everything in between. What they share is a problem we think we can help with.

* Start conversations, always with a specific reason we think we can help
* Help people get value faster by connecting them with the right products, docs, and people at PostHog
* Create opportunities for our account executives and hand them over with the full picture

## Who we work with

* New Business and Product-led Sales, who take the conversations we start and keep adding value
* GTM Engineering, who build the tools we use to do our job
* Marketing, so we stay on brand and help share the great content they create

## Principles

**Unreasonable hospitality.** We strive to approach the market with unreasonable hospitality. That means being thoughtful about what we learn about a person or an account before we reach out, and always asking ourselves whether what we're doing is helpful and adds value.

**Process driven.** We're intentional about what we do and know what outcome we expect before we start. We measure twice and cut once, spend the time sharpening the axe before we chop, and stick with the process.

**Speed as a lever.** AI is compressing how fast teams build and change. We try to do everything a little bit faster, whether that's account research, writing a personal note or following up with someone, and that compounds for the people we work with.

**Experiment in the open.** We run new approaches as short tests with a clear metric, share the results in our team channel, and keep what works.

## How we work

* We participate in the New Business team's weekly sprint on GitHub
* A weekly outbound checkpoint with the BDR team
* A weekly 1:1 between each BDR and the BDR team lead
