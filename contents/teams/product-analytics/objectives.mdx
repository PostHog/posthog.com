### Q4 2026 objectives

This quarter, our goals are split. We don't have enough certainty to predict a whole three months of direction. We could carry on as the money-engine. Improving charts, visualisations, and feature parity. But we want to take a moment and learn more about how people's behaviour is changing. We want to take a moment to look up and around and orient ourselves better.

We will focus on what we'll explore rather than what we'll ship. For a long time, our challenge was building everything customers asked for. Now it's building the right things. We think what our team needs most is to step back and understand our users' broader goals and pain points, so we can refocus on what matters most.

For each area below, we'll interview users, dig into our metrics, and share findings with the team at our offsite. Meanwhile, we'll keep shipping based on what we learn and maintaining the features we own.

**Focus areas:**
1. Large customer needs: What are our large customers struggling with? Where are the limits getting pushed?
2. Startup needs: What needs are we missing with small organizations and startups trying to build and scale their business?
3. User retention: Why are individual users stopping using PostHog?
4. MCP-first users: Where are we not meeting the needs of MCP-first or MCP-only users?

### What we’ll explore

#### Large customer needs: <TeamMember name="Mike Warren" />

- **Who we'll speak to:** Large customer users; our sales team
- **Questions to explore:**
  - Why are larger, managed accounts churning?
  - Why are we losing competitive deals?
  - Why aren't large orgs switching to PostHog?

#### Startup needs: <TeamMember name="Matt Pua" />

- **Who we'll speak to:** Small orgs that churned early; small orgs that signed up but didn't finish setup or invite teammates
- **Questions to explore:**
  - Why do early-stage orgs churn?
  - Why don't teams invite their teammates?
  - Where do orgs get stuck during setup?
  - What are people saying about PostHog on Reddit, G2, X, etc.?
  - What does marketing see as the reasons we win or lose?
  -  Are smaller/younger orgs more AI-pilled, more MCP first, less likely to use web? How is web use changing?
  

#### User retention: <TeamMember name="Daniel Parker" />

- **Who we'll speak to:** Users invited to an org who stopped using PostHog after setup (in both existing and new orgs); people new to PostHog (spilt by new to analytics vs used to a different analytics solution),
- **Questions to explore:**
  - Why do new orgs stop using PostHog after setup?
  - Why do existing org users stop using PostHog?
  - What trips up someone seeing PostHog with fresh eyes? (Existing and new orgs)

#### MCP-first users: <TeamMember name="Sam Pennington" />

- **Who we'll speak to** (across Codex, Claude, Slack, etc.): Hybrid users (MCP + Web); MCP-only users; users who stopped using our MCP
- **Questions to explore:**
  - Does the experience differ across harnesses?
  - What challenges are they running into?
  - How well do they understand PostHog?
  - What leads users to stop using our MCP?
