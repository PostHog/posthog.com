## What the RevOps team does

* Own the definitions behind PostHog's revenue metrics (MRR, retention, expansion, contraction, churn) so every team measures the same thing the same way
* Revenue and usage analysis for exec, board, and investor reporting
* Design and run sales compensation: quota setting, comp plans, attainment and commission calculation
* Data quality across the revenue stack: billing, Stripe, Salesforce, and the semantic layer
* Sales ops: defining how the revenue process works and building the automations behind it. [CRM process](/handbook/growth/sales/crm), [contract rules](/handbook/growth/sales/contract-rules), credits, and refunds

Our values, what we own, and what we deliberately don't do are written up in the [RevOps handbook](/handbook/growth/revops/overview).

## Who are we building for?

* Exec and finance, who need revenue numbers that reconcile and hold up under scrutiny
* Sales and CS, who need quotas, comp, and pipeline data they can trust and act on without waiting for us
* Product and engineering teams, who need to know how their work shows up in revenue

## Principles

* **Make data simple.** Turn scattered data into a small number of clear, reliable definitions people can reuse.
* **Build for self service.** If someone needs a number regularly, they should be able to get it themselves.
* **Automate relentlessly.** If it has to be done twice by hand, it gets automated. Constant clean-up means we built the wrong system.
* **Show your work.** Every number we publish should be reproducible and auditable back to its source.
