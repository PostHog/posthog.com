## Q3 2026 objectives

This quarter, we want to finish the move to the new revenue model and make the numbers behind it something people can get themselves.

### Tier 1: Finish the revenue model

_Theme: One set of revenue numbers that reconcile and that Finance, exec, and product teams all trust._

1. **Close out the new revenue model (lead: <TeamMember name="Mine Kansu" />, eng lead: <TeamMember name="Marce Coll" />)**
    - Close out the remaining material discrepancies against the old model: fix them or explicitly accept them.
    - Rebuild internal ARR/MRR dashboards on the new model and run them next to the old ones until we trust them.
    - Migrate investor and board reporting to the new model.
    - Get Finance sign-off on the model docs and the close process.
    - Get revenue model data flowing into Campfire.

2. **Make revenue definitions the shared source of truth (lead: <TeamMember name="Mine Kansu" />)**
    - Publish the metric conventions for MRR, retention, expansion, contraction, and churn, and make sure billing, Salesforce, and the semantic layer use the same definitions.
    - Make routine ARR/MRR questions self-serve for Finance, Sales, leadership, and product teams, so the answer comes from a dashboard or PostHog AI rather than from us.

### Tier 2: Sales ops and compensation

_Theme: The revenue process runs on rules and automations, not on manual clean-up._

3. **Sales compensation**
    - Set quotas and comp plans for the quarter, and make attainment and commission calculation reproducible from source data.

4. **Revenue process automation**
    - Encode the CRM process and contract rules into automations, so discounts, credits, and refunds follow the same path every time.
    - Track where we still do manual clean-up and treat each recurring case as a system to fix.
