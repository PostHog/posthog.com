### Q4 2026 objectives

#### Objective 1: Reduce the complexity of building AI products <TeamMember name="Charles Vien" photo />

**Motivation:** We should be the core that powers every agentic product at PostHog, but some teams have built their own systems instead of using ours. Two gateways are still in production and product-specific logic in our task models makes every new integration slow.

**Key results:**

- Move all products fully onto the new gateway and switch off the legacy one, so there is one gateway in production <TeamMember name="Brandon Leung" photo />
- Ship one task interface any team can build on, with a written contract for what a task is, what state it's in and who can access it <TeamMember name="Charles Vien" photo />
- Ship a smart router with provider policy per product or team, using the same mechanism as failover, so we can route for margin and HIPAA <TeamMember name="Brandon Leung" photo />

#### Objective 2: Every AI unit of work is costed and attributed <TeamMember name="Brandon Leung" photo />

**Motivation:** Product teams top ask is accurate task costs and spending controls. Today the gateway ledger and Django credits are separate systems and we only find abuse after the bill arrives.

**Key results:**

- Know and cap what every run and generation costs: exact LLM and compute cost per run, caps at mint, product-level rate limits and live budgets <TeamMember name="Brandon Leung" photo />
- Agree a default billing model for the whole runtime that connects the gateway ledger to Django credits, written up as a pricing review RFC <TeamMember name="Ben White" photo />

#### Objective 3: Safe model and sandbox access <TeamMember name="Alessandro Pogliaghi" photo />

**Motivation:** Security has been neglected and we aren't talking enough about abuse, data exfiltration and prompt injection.

**Key results:**

- Ship security at the compute and inference layers: an egress proxy for deterministic rules and trace investigation for the fuzzy cases <TeamMember name="Alessandro Pogliaghi" photo />
- Investigate running the harness outside the sandbox and decide whether it's worth the move <TeamMember name="Jonathan Mieloo" photo />

#### Objective 4: A reliable agent runtime teams can build on <TeamMember name="Jonathan Mieloo" photo />

**Motivation:** People see sandboxes and tasks as slow and unreliable and building on or debugging them is painful.

**Key results:**

- Harden and simplify the agent runtime package, deciding which harnesses product teams need and whether we keep investing in the Pi harness <TeamMember name="Jonathan Mieloo" photo />
- Give people and agents a dependable local setup and an easy way to debug sandboxes and the task runtime <TeamMember name="Jonathan Mieloo" photo />

### Stretch

#### Internal evals

**Motivation:** Teams have no shared way to tell whether a model or agent change made things better or cheaper.

**Key results:**

- Give teams a shared way to test models and agent changes against real use cases, measuring quality and cost per successful task and catching regressions before they ship

### Ongoing work

- Document the gateway, tasks and sandbox interfaces.
