## Q4 2026 objectives

Our focus this quarter is UI: make PostHog a compelling home for business intelligence (BI) and notebook analysis.

### Objective 1: Build a BI interface that rivals Tableau

Owner: Marius

Motivation: Teams should be able to move their BI work to PostHog without compromising on the interface. "We use Tableau" should no longer be an excuse not to move to PostHog.

What we'll ship:

- A BI interface that rivals Tableau for exploring data, building visualizations, and working with dashboards.

### Objective 2: Build notebooks that rival Hex and Jupyter

Owner: Sinan

Motivation: Teams should have a notebook experience in PostHog that can stand alongside Hex and Jupyter. The UI should make PostHog a compelling place to do their analysis.

What we'll ship:

- A notebook interface that rivals Hex and Jupyter for writing, running, and presenting analyses.

We'll know we're successful when nobody can say "we use Tableau" as an excuse not to move to PostHog.
