## Q4 2026 objectives

Our focus this quarter is UI: make PostHog a compelling home for business intelligence (BI) and notebook analysis.

Team: Marius, with Anna as PM.

### Objective 1: Build an industry-leading BI interface

Owner: Marius

Motivation: Teams should be able to move their BI work to PostHog without compromising on the interface. Their existing BI tool should no longer be a reason not to move to PostHog.

What we'll ship:

- An industry-leading BI interface for exploring data, building visualizations, and working with dashboards.

### Objective 2: Build an industry-leading notebook experience

Owner: Marius

Motivation: Teams should have a notebook experience in PostHog that matches the best dedicated analysis tools. The UI should make PostHog a compelling place to do their analysis.

What we'll ship:

- An industry-leading notebook interface for writing, running, and presenting analyses.

We'll know we're successful when teams no longer see their existing BI or notebook tools as a reason not to move to PostHog.
