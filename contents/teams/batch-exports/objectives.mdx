# Q4 2026

## Objective 1: 10% usage comes from HogQL-modeled batch exports

### Motivation
* Understand how far can we get with HogQL as an interface for batch exports.
* Take ownership of parts of PostHog that could be better served by batch exports.

### What we’ll ship
* Complete support for HogQL in scheduled and on demand batch exports.
* Skill/MCP improvements to better direct agents to use HogQL batch exports.

## Objective 2: Understand and optimize our product's growth

### Motivation
* Without knowing what's costing us:
  * We can't optimize effectively.
  * We can't know how to improve our pricing strategy.

### What we’ll ship
* Understand which areas of batch exports cost us the most.
  * Ideally: Cost breakdown per team, batch export, and even individual runs.
* Act on the findings to deliver cost savings.

## Objective 3: Scaling batch exports to many more customers

### Motivation
* Increase confidence that our pipeline can handle 10x our current volume.

### What we'll ship
* Experiment and apply new architectures.
* Test different file formats and/or compression algorithms.
