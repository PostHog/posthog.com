### Who we're building for
**Support**, **Customer Success**, and **Product Engineers** at product-led B2B or B2C tech companies, where:
- The support team is small and can escalate to engineers
- Most tickets are product questions or bugs, and the fastest way to an answer is seeing what the user did and having good docs to point to

**Conversations is a good fit if they:**
- Already use PostHog (or should) and want [session replay](/session-replay), [product analytics](/product-analytics), and other product context right next to each ticket
- Want AI to investigate and answer tickets, with a human stepping in when it's unsure
- Need a knowledge layer to power their support answers or their own app
- Want to track support quality and speed
- Want to build workflows around their support process
- Want value right away, without a long setup

**Who else at their company might use it:**
- **Product Managers** to spot where users get stuck or the product falls short
- **Marketing** to see common questions and points of confusion

### Who we're NOT building for (right now)
**Large support orgs and contact centers** that need:
- Big teams of dedicated agents with shift scheduling, SLAs, and complex routing
- Phone and voice support
- Support across many languages

**Teams that want AI to fully replace their support team** and need:
- An agent that handles everything on its own, with no human in the loop
- AI that takes actions in other tools, like issuing refunds or changing orders
- Heavy testing and simulations before anything goes live

Later-stage competitors are a better fit for these teams today. If they try us and churn, that's fine. We haven't built what they need (yet).
