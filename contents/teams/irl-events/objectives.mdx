### Q3 2026 objectives

These are the primary goals the team is prioritizing this quarter.

<details>
<summary>Launch a signature self-driving event series</summary>

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Daniel Zaltsman" photo />

**Rationale:** Conferences are (still) wasteful; the weird, owned, curated format is the way we win (with weird).

**Things we could do:**
- Test 3 formats
- Pitch our format to partners
- Curate ICP guest lists

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- One format clearly wins because of glowing response and partner interest

</details>

<details>
<summary>Build the event-to-content flywheel</summary>

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Daniel Zaltsman" photo /> <TeamMember name="Kliment Minchev" photo />

**Rationale:** Our biggest unused leverage currently. We mostly lean on partners for video and social.

**Things we could do:**
- Creator network
- Handhelds at hacker houses
- Footage-to-social pipeline
- Feed s*itposts
- Feed/feature builders and event guests in brand channels
- Higher visibility partnerships and co-marketing

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- Events ship content we drove and buzz from events climbs
- Deepen our partnerships via IRL events co-hosting

</details>

<details>
<summary>Turn builder groups into curated collectives</summary>

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Kliment Minchev" photo />

**Rationale:** Builder groups are unfocused and passive; collectives and hacker houses are rising.

**Things we could do:**
- Rebrand to collectives
- Reach a higher profile organizer
- Embed in hacker houses
- Target by ICP data [dashboard here](https://us.posthog.com/project/2/dashboard/1329163)

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- More collectives, more self-organization, and motion without our input

</details>

<details>
<summary>Reposition the forum from company inbox to community space (/questions → /forum)</summary>

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Brittany Joiner" photo />

**Rationale:** Today the forum works like a company inbox, and 85% of questions are self-servable. It should be a space where the community talks to each other.

**Things we could do (small-ships sequence):**
- Remove docs questions
- AI-nudge in the ask flow (and on docs)
- New conversation-driving sections and topics
- Reframe/rename, make the sidebar make sense for "what to do"
- Seed initial community members and incentivize discussions

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- Repeat posters increase   
- Replies-per-post up 
- Total new posts down (*yes, this is counterintuitive*)

</details>

<details>
<summary>Grow quality engagement on Discord beyond Desktop product</summary>

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Brittany Joiner" photo />

**Rationale:** Activity is concentrated in one channel (#posthog-desktop). Quality engagement means conversations happening across more of the server and more topics.

**Things we could do:**
- Weekly conversation prompts (*but don't make it forced or cheesy*)
- Expand active spaces past the #posthog-chat channel
- Bring users into discord for other products

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- Repeat posters go from 17% → 20%+
- Increase in posts in non-#posthog-chat channels
- Total members past ~1,150 (continued growth even past Desktop launch)

</details>

<details>
<summary>Build the community response system</summary>

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Brittany Joiner" photo />

**Rationale:** Community responses need triage tooling plus the people who run it, so posts don't get skipped and don't depend on one person.

**Things we could do:**
- Triage v1: bug → support, feature request → roadmap, feedback → owning team
- Contributor rewards on [existing points infra](https://posthog.com/handbook/community/points)
- Internal folks active on the Support Hero model (*maybe Community Heroes?*)

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- ≥90% of incoming triaged/responded within 24h
- ≥3 active moderators/responders who aren't Brittany (mix of internal + external champions)
- Replies on 50%+ of posts across channels

</details>
