### Q4 2026 objectives

#### Goal 1: Finish and evaluate the Replay encoder

*Description*: Finish the current encoder training run and learn what the model does well and badly before we scale it.

*What we will ship*:
- **Finish training** – complete the current run (30/40 epochs)
- **Benchmarks** – compare the encoder against SOTA
- **Visualizations** – PCA and RCDM
- **Probes** – MLP probes on the embeddings
- **Weights analysis** – SwiGLU activations, dead paths, and the attention softmax distribution
- **Kernels**
- **Labeling suite evals** – evaluate with the labels from the data labeling suite
- **Checkpoint evals** – evaluate at different checkpoints
- **Anisotropy** – check if the embeddings sit in a narrow cone, and test centering as a fix
- **Multi-head ablation**

#### Goal 2: Train the v3 encoder

*Description*: Apply what we learn in Goal 1 and train the next encoder on the cluster in approximately 2 weeks, then evaluate it in 1 week.

*What we will ship*:
- **Train on v3 data** – train on the cluster with the findings from Goal 1 applied
- **Higher-resolution cooldown** – train the cooldown at a higher resolution
- **10k labels** – 10k training QA pairs and 1k validation QA pairs
- **Evaluate the v3 encoder** – the self-supervised loss is very different from the supervised loss, so we must compare them carefully

#### Goal 3: Scale the data for the end-to-end agent

*Description*: Make the training data large enough to train the end-to-end agent.

*What we will ship*:
- **50k text renderer samples** – generate them with the session replay text renderer
- **LLM pipeline** – build the pipeline that makes QA labels from the samples
- **Path to 100k labels** – scale the labels from the text renderer to 100k
