### Q4 2026 objectives

These are the primary goals the team is prioritizing this quarter. Goals for PMMs can provide an extra level of focus or to define a specific initiative beyond the business-as-usual work that comes from covering a specific product. Supporting [the product they are assigned to](/handbook/marketing/ownership) is always the default quarterly goal. 

<details>
<summary> Create a referral platform (Joe)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Word of mouth is how we grow. Let's make that explicit and measurable. 
- **Things we could do:** Finish previous hackathons, or find a partner.
- **We'll know we're successful when:** We've discovered incentives that work and have a new growth channel. 

</details>

<details>
<summary>Grow Replay Vision adoption (Sara)</summary>

- **Rationale:** This is potentially one of our biggest revenue generators. 
- **Things we could do:** Use-case marketing. Field guide distribution. In-app cross-selling. New scanner launches. 
- **We'll know we're successful when:** More Session Replay users adopt Replay Vision.


</details>

<details>
<summary>Unify PostHog's AI/Agent story (Cleo)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Our best users use PostHog outside the PostHog UI.

- **We'll know we're successful when:** PostHog AI adoption and engagement increases across multiple surfaces. 

</details>

<details>
<summary>Make users data literate (Lizzie)</summary>

- **Rationale:** Product engineers need data tools, but don't know it yet.

- **We'll know we're successful when:** Warehouse sources are our fastest growing product.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Fold the context warehouse into PostHog's data-first positioning (Lizzie)</summary>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Take MCP analytics to market (Juliana)</summary>

- **Rationale:** MCP analytics is our best kept secret, but shouldn't be.

</details>
