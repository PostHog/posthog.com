### <TeamMember name="Jack Tomei" photo />
**Build a setup wizard that tells customers what to cut, keep, and add**

*A guided wizard, shipped through Christophe's concierge mode, that helps customers fit their PostHog setup to what they're actually trying to do. The customer picks their role (founder, product engineer, product manager, data engineer, or CTO to start, with more to follow) and states their goals. The wizard then asks a short set of questions: are they trying to cut spend, replace other tools in their stack with PostHog, or get more out of what they already pay for? It finishes with recommendations based on their own project: savings where they're overpaying, and expansion where PostHog can replace something they pay for elsewhere. Think of it as an upgrade to (or replacement for) the current wizard audit: the same idea, but tailored to the customer's role and goals instead of one review for everyone.*

**We'll know we're successful when**:
- The wizard is live in concierge mode with all 5 role paths, and adding a new role is a config change, not a rebuild
- Customers can find the wizard in the PostHog UI and run it themselves, without a CSM having to point them to it
