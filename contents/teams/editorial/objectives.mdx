## AEO maintenance mode

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Natalia Amorim" photo />

**Motivation:** After a quarter of headwinds, the highest-leverage thing we can do is making sure everything we have in place is working and that we can actually see whether it's working.

**What we'll ship:**

- Optimized product pages and key website pages (about, FAQ, pricing, etc.)
- A refresh pass on our highest-value articles that have declined, prioritized by past traffic, citation loss and business relevance
- A technical SEO/AEO bug sweep

**We'll know we're successful when:**

- Refreshed articles win back rankings and citations, and SEO/AEO traffic and signups recover
