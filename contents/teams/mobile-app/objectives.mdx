## Q4 2026 goals

Build a focused PostHog mobile app so you can check your data and act on reports when you're away from your computer.

### Launch the mobile app on iOS and Android

-   Release the app through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
-   Start with feedback from people who already use PostHog on their phones.
-   Use early testing and app store feedback to improve the experience.

### Make key metrics easy to check on the go

-   View dashboards, insights, and session replays.
-   Pin frequently used dashboards and insights, and carry pinned insights over from the web app.
-   Let people choose the view the app opens to, while respecting their organization's AI consent settings.

### Help people act on self-driving reports

-   Send notifications when new reports are ready to review.
-   Make it easy to review a report and hand it off to PostHog, Claude, or Codex.
-   Let people ask questions about their data through PostHog AI chat.

### Learn where mobile adds value

-   Find out whether the app helps people check their data and act on reports when they otherwise wouldn't.
-   Measure repeat visits, report actions, and the time from a report being created to someone acting on it.
-   Improve onboarding so new PostHog customers can discover the mobile app.
