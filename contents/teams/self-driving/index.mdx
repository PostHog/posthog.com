## What this team does

We build PostHog's self-driving platform, allowing users to fix bugs and ship improvements in their apps before anyone gets around to reporting them.
We help teams catch problems early, turn them into working pull requests, and merge them quickly and confidently.

Two paths run from your data (Session Replay, Product Analytics, Error Tracking, AI Observability etc.) to Reports, and from there to PRs:

- Data → Signals → Signal groups → Reports → PRs
- Data → Scouts → Reports → PRs

## ICP

Self-driving is built for small, AI-pilled startups: YC-like companies of roughly 2 to 20 people who already ship with coding agents.

## User personas

### Assessing customer fit
Use this guide to determine if a prospect's use case is a good fit for Self-driving now, in the future, or at all.

### Product engineers (product, growth, full-stack)
**✅ Fully supported now**

They get the most value since they own a slice of the product end to end and can merge a PR themselves, which turns a report into shipped code with no handoff and no review queue.
