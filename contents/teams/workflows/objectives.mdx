### Q4 2026 Objectives

- **Run all of PostHog's messaging on Workflows** (Champion: Harley)
  - Motivation: We use Workflows for much of our own messaging, but there are still gaps. Closing them makes the product better for everyone.
  - What we'll ship:
    - Move the rest of PostHog's messaging onto Workflows
    - Rebuild the remaining campaigns by hand first
    - Bring over everything else with the importer
- **Grow Workflows** (Champions: Mike & Michael)
  - Motivation: The product is in a solid state. Now we want more teams to start using it and to get value from it quickly.
  - What we'll ship:
    - Onboarding flow for Workflows (UI and MCP)
    - Workflows suggested from your own product data when you start
    - Deeper integration with the rest of PostHog
    - Route existing marketing and messaging destinations into Workflows
    - Behavioural cohorts
    - Importers so teams can bring their existing campaigns
    - Hands-on help for teams moving to Workflows
- **Self-driving v1 in GA** (Champion: Daniel)
  - Motivation: Customers get good suggestions to improve each campaign and their whole set of campaigns. This is the first step towards messaging that runs itself.
  - What we'll ship:
    - Suggest changes to a single workflow or broadcast based on customer data
    - Suggest changes across workflows and broadcasts based on customer data, e.g. make sure the same cohort doesn't get messages from several campaigns at once
    - Suggest new workflows and broadcasts based on customer data
