## Mission

We help builders level up by learning from each other, online and IRL.

## What we do 

Builder Relations is the team that brings PostHog users together to learn from each other, both in real life and online.

We do this through our in-person events, meetups, and community incubators, as well as the online channels we own: the [forum](/questions) and our [Discord](https://discord.gg/posthog) community.

Each channel has a different job, and we're deliberate about which. Read [community channels](/handbook/community/channels) for how we think about Discord vs. the forum, why neither is a support channel, and how to join Discord as a PostHog team member.

## Feature ownership

- The [community forum](/questions), including moderation and triage
- The [Discord server](https://discord.gg/posthog)
- Community response and triage across channels, including GitHub and Reddit
- [Points, achievements, and rewards](/handbook/community/points)
- In-person events, meetups, and the [community incubator](/community-incubator)
