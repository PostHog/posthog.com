## Mission

We help builders level up by learning from each other, online and IRL.

## What we do 

Builder Relations is the team that organizes and runs PostHog's in-person events and community gatherings.

We bring PostHog users together in real life through events, meetups, and community incubators, and we own the [forum](/questions) and our [Discord](https://discord.gg/posthog) community.
