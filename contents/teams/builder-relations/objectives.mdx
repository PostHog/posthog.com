<details>

<summary>Establish the Student program and host events on several university campuses</summary>

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Kliment Minchev" photo />

**Rationale:** Pilot campus ambassador recruitment and slowly increase footprint on campuses through events.

**Things we could do:**
- Talk to students to come up with a compelling format to engage
- Ship a [/students](/students) community page with a signup form for ambassadors
- Connect with student orgs on multiple campuses in the Bay Area/California
- Test a 2 events per semester cadence (mixer and check-in)
- Collect signals and metrics from ambassador applications and hosting events

**We'll know we're successful when we see:**
- Our presence is more recognizable at target schools (see signs in the wild)
- We see a lot of ambassador applications in subsequent semesters (interest grows)
- Keep our events format engaging and attended

</details>

<details>
<summary>Shift and expand conversations in community channels</summary>

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Brittany Joiner" photo />

**Rationale:** Today the forum works like a company inbox, and 85% of questions are self-servable. It should be a space where the community talks to each other. Discord has this model, so we need to nurture and expand conversations past PostHog Desktop.

**Things we could do (small-ships sequence):**
- Remove docs questions
- AI-nudge in the ask flow (and on docs)
- New conversation-driving sections and topics
- Reframe/rename, make the sidebar make sense for "what to do"
- Seed initial community members and incentivize discussions
- Weekly conversation prompts (*but don't make it forced or cheesy*)
- Expand active spaces past the #posthog-chat channel
- Bring users into discord for other products
- Use Onboarding questions in Discord to nurture more conversations

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- Repeat posters increase (from 17% to 20%++ in Discord)
- Replies-per-post up in forum
- How to/troubleshooting questions breakout decreases from 75%.
- Total new posts down (*yes, this is counterintuitive, but we want quality conversations not a full inbox*)
- Increase in posts in non-#posthog-chat channels
- Total Discord members past ~1,150 (continued growth even past Desktop launch)

</details>

<details>
<summary>Build the community response system</summary>

**Owner:** <TeamMember name="Brittany Joiner" photo />

**Rationale:** Community responses need triage tooling plus the people who run it, so posts don't get skipped and don't depend on one person.

**Things we could do:**
- Triage v1: bug → support, feature request → roadmap, feedback → owning team
- Contributor rewards on [existing points infra](/handbook/community/points)
- Internal folks active on the Support Hero model (*maybe Community Heroes?*)

**We'll know we're successful when:**
- ≥90% of incoming triaged/responded within 24h
- ≥3 active moderators/responders who aren't Brittany (mix of internal + external champions)
- Replies on 75%+ of posts across channels ([90%+ in Forum, up from 17% baseline measured](https://us.posthog.com/project/2/insights/jwuvycT3))

</details>
