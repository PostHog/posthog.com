# From flag to experiment

A rollout asks whether a change is safe. An <Term name="experiment">experiment</Term> asks whether it's better, and in PostHog it runs on a flag with variants <SeeFig n={1} />.

## You already have the pieces

An experiment needs a multivariate flag with at least two variants, one of them named `control`. Use an existing flag that has those, with `control` as its first variant, or let the experiment create one. A person counts as exposed when a `$feature_flag_called` event comes in, and your checks already send it. Only exposed people count in the results, and anything they did before exposure is ignored.

## The 101 rules matter more here

A person who switches variants mid-experiment counts in both groups and muddies both results. Switching happens when the distinct ID changes between checks, so call `identify()` before the first check, check once, and keep the variant.

## Check the flag as late as you can

Put the check where the experience splits, after every other condition. An earlier check counts people who never see the change as exposed, and they dilute your results.

## When it ends

Ship the winning variant, then delete the flag. When you end an experiment, PostHog can open the cleanup PR for you.
