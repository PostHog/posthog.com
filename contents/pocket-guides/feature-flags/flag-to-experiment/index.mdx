# From flag to experiment

A rollout asks "is it safe?" An <Term name="experiment">experiment</Term> asks "is it better?" In PostHog, an experiment runs on a flag with variants <SeeFig n={1} />.

## You already have the pieces

An experiment needs a multivariate flag with at least two variants, one named `control`. Use an existing flag that has those (with `control` as its first variant), or let the experiment make one. Someone counts as exposed when a `$feature_flag_called` event comes in, and your checks already send that.

## The 101 rules matter more here

If someone switches variants mid-experiment, their results count in both groups. That happens when the distinct ID changes between checks. Call `identify()` before the first check, check once, and stick with the variant.

## Check the flag as late as you can

Put the check right where the experience actually splits, after every other condition. Otherwise people who never see the change still count as exposed, and they water down your results.

## When it ends

Ship the winning variant, then delete the flag. When you end an experiment, PostHog can even open the cleanup PR for you.
