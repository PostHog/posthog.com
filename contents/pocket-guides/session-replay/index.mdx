# Session replay

Watch how people actually use your product with Session Replay – or let <Term name="replay vision">Replay Vision</Term> watch it for you.

## Where to start

Session Replay is one of the tools that makes your product <Term name="self-driving" />: play back sessions to see exactly why something happened so the fix is obvious. The context agents use to debug UI issues and nuanced user behavior in your product, website, or mobile app.

Session Replay records what happens in a user's session – clicks, scrolls, form inputs, page views, network requests, console logs – and plays it back like video.

You can watch replays yourself, with coworkers, on a loop on your office TV, or have Replay Vision watch it for you.
