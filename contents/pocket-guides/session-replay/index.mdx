# Session replay

Watch how people actually use your product with Session Replay – or let <Term name="replay vision">Replay Vision</Term> watch it for you.

Session Replay records what happens in a user's session – clicks, scrolls, form inputs, page views, network requests, console logs – and plays it back like video.

You can watch replays yourself, with coworkers, on a loop on your office TV, or let your product self-drive and have
[Replay Vision](/pocket-guides/session-replay/replay-vision) watch sessions for you.

## Where to start

This guide aims to give you a conceptual understanding of Session Replays.

- **[Section 1](/pocket-guides/session-replay/101)** introduces what Session Replays can do.
- **Sections 2-4** cover key configurations to make sure you get complete, accurate recordings.
- **Sections 5 onwards** cover common use cases and patterns to watch and analyze replays.

Don't sweat the details. Once you know what to ask for, point your agent to the full [session replay docs](/docs/session-replay). This guide tells you what to ask for.

Every page assumes a PostHog SDK is in your app with recording switched on. If it isn't yet, run the wizard first or [install it yourself](/docs/session-replay/installation):
