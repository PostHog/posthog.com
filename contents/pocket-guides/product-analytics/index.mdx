Twig is a vacation-rental website. People browse stays, choose a destination, and save places they'd like to visit. Its engineers want to understand how people use these features and where they get stuck.

They start with the destination filters: Forest, Coast, and City. Which do visitors choose? To answer that question, the team needs to decide what to record. Follow their work, starting with the first filter click.
