Product engineers build things and test whether they work. A product can do what its code says. People (and agents), inconveniently, do what they want. They change their minds, take detours, and sometimes leave without doing what the builder expected. The interface still works. The team still has questions.

This guide follows the product engineers at Twig as they make those choices visible with <Term name="product analytics">Product Analytics</Term>. They start by recording an action on the site, adding the detail that makes it useful, and reading what the resulting data can tell them.

## Meet Twig

Twig is a bird-run vacation-rental website for humans looking for a getaway. It's the product at the center of this guide. Different parts of Twig appear as the engineers work on them, so there's no need to memorize the whole site before page one.
