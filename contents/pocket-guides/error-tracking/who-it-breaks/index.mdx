# Find the errors your best accounts hit

Every <Term name="exception" /> is an <Term name="event" />, so it carries the <Term name="person" /> it happened to. That is the difference between knowing an error fires 391 times and knowing it fires for eleven accounts, three of them on your largest plan.

Occurrences is the number the <Term name="issue" /> list sorts by out of the box. It is also the number that lies to you the most.

## What the scout watches

The questions that rank an issue are further out than error tracking:

- How many people did it reach?
- Are any of them paying you?
- Did they come back afterwards?
- What do those accounts pay, in total?

Every one is answerable because an exception is an event and carries the person it happened to.

## What makes it report

All of this is a triage pass you would rather not repeat every morning. A <Term name="scout" /> runs it on a schedule and only writes when an issue concentrates in accounts worth caring about.

## What lands in your inbox

It files a <Term name="report" /> in your <Term name="inbox" /> with paying accounts' share of the issue against their share of your traffic.
