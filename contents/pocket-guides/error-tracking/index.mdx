## Where to start

Error tracking is one of the tools that lets your product <Term name="self-driving">self-drive</Term>. Error tracking takes you through discovery and fixing exceptions in three steps:

1. **Instrument** – capture exceptions, and make them readable and grouped.
2. **Report** – size what broke, for whom, and whether it is worth waking up for.
3. **Act** – route it to an owner, get agents to report on high-impact errors automatically or open pull requests.
