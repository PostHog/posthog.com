# Error Tracking

Error Tracking captures the exceptions your code throws, with their stack traces, and groups them into <Term name="issue">issues</Term> you can assign and resolve.

PostHog is the context layer for your product. Each exception is an event, stored next to your analytics, session replays, logs, and the business data you sync from [sources](/docs/data-warehouse/sources) like Stripe, Postgres, or HubSpot. You and your agents can query exceptions alongside that data, so you can rank issues by the accounts they hit and what those accounts pay you.

PostHog can also <Term name="self-driving">self-drive</Term>. <Term name="scout">Scouts</Term> watch your exceptions and file reports in your inbox. When a report has a clear code change, an agent opens a pull request for you to review.

## Where to start

New to Error Tracking? Start with [Error Tracking 101](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/101). It covers what an exception carries and how it becomes an issue.

Jump to the page for what you are working on:

**Capture**

- One bug split across several issues, or several bugs in one: [Grouping](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/group-exceptions)
- Minified stack traces you can't read: [Stack traces](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/stack-traces)
- Noisy exceptions you don't want stored or billed: [Keep noise out](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/keep-noise-out)

**Query**

- Charting exceptions, or joining them to people, sessions, and accounts with SQL: [Exceptions as events](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/exceptions-as-events)
- Asking your coding agent about exceptions in plain language: [Query exceptions with MCP](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/query-with-mcp)
- Choosing which issue to fix first by revenue, conversion, or experiment results: [Measure impact](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/measure-impact)

**Automate**

- Assigning each issue to the team that owns it: [Triage](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/triage-issues)
- Posting to Slack when an issue reopens or spikes: [Alerts](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/alerts)

**Self-drive**

- Catching error spikes and stuck loops without reading every issue: [Spikes and stuck loops](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/errors-as-signals)
- Finding the errors your paying accounts hit: [Errors by account value](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/who-it-breaks)
- Tying an error burst to the release or flag that caused it: [Deploy regressions](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/what-changed)
- Checking whether a fix held: [Verify the fix](/pocket-guides/error-tracking/fix-while-you-sleep)
