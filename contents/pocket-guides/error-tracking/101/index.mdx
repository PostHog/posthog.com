# Error Tracking 101

Error Tracking takes exceptions thrown by your application and aggregates them into issues in context of your product data.

This lets you answer questions you can't with just a stack trace:

- Which customers are impacted?
- Does it impact conversion and retention?
- Can this error be tied to loss in revenue?
- Were they part of any feature flag or experiment rollouts?
- What's shown on the user's page before and after the exception is thrown?

With this context, Error Tracking makes your product <Term name="self-driving">self-driving</Term>. Agents will have the context to triage the issues based on impact and open PRs to fix them.

## Exceptions and issues

Like everything else in PostHog, each instance of an <Term name="exception" /> is captured as a `$exception` <Term name="event" />. Similar exceptions are aggregated into <Term name="issue">issues</Term> <SeeFig n={1} />. Those issues are the base unit of work: you diagnose, triage, assign, and fix them, not individual exceptions.

## Path to self-driving

Error tracking takes you through discovery and fixing exceptions in three steps:

- **Observe** - First, make every issue a useful signal. Capture the right exceptions, group one failure once, and attach readable traces and the path to the error.
- **Report** - Then, include exceptions into your insights and dashboards, triage the issues that matter, and put the right one in front of the right person.
- **Self-drive** - Finally, let scouts watch for specific failure patterns, investigate their impact, and hand actionable reports to agents that can open pull requests.

The goal is to get actionable reports and a PR to address the exception automatically, so you can ship fixes while you sleep <SeeFig n={2} />.

This pocket guide will explore all of these usecases in detail.

## Install and turn it on

None of it works until a PostHog SDK is in your app. The wizard installs and configures one for you, or you can [install it yourself](/docs/error-tracking/installation).

Capture is a project setting as well as an SDK one, so switch on [exception autocapture](https://app.posthog.com/error_tracking/configuration#selectedSetting=error-tracking-exception-autocapture) before you expect to see anything.
