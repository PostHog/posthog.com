# Catch error spikes and stuck loops

A <Term name="scout" /> runs on a schedule rather than continuously, explores rather than following one stream, and decides what clears the bar you set. It can rank, cluster, weigh who is affected, and stay quiet on a day when nothing important happened.

## What the scout watches

The error tracking scout watches error spikes, stuck loops, and <Term name="fingerprint" /> clusters, triaged by how many users they hit.

## What makes it report

A scout writes a <Term name="report" /> only when something clears its bar. Unlike a source, it can weigh the evidence and stay quiet when nothing important happened.
