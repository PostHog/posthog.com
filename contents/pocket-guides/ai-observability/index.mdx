# AI Observability

Your AI feature works until it doesn't, and the failure mode is often hidden. This volume is about seeing what your model actually does in the wild, what it costs, and whether the outputs it returns are what users want.

## Where to start

**New to AI Observability?** Start with [AI Observability 101](/pocket-guides/ai-observability/101) on the next page – it covers what gets captured on every LLM call and how to make sense of the data.

**Already instrumented?** The use cases in the **Contents** each include a prompt you can run in PostHog AI to build the thing the guide describes.
