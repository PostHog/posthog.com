# Which onboarding steps turn trials into paying customers?

Every onboarding flow has steps that matter and steps that don't. The trick is knowing which is
which. This skill builds the onboarding funnel, flags who reached a first Stripe payment, and
compares payers against non-payers step by step.

## What the answer looks like

The skill looks for patterns like <SeeFig n={1} />: only 9% of non-payers ever create a second
project, versus 52% of payers, the biggest gap in the funnel. Inviting a teammate looks
important on its own, but the gap is smaller once you weigh it against how many people do it
anyway.

## Doing this by hand

1. **Build the [funnel](/docs/product-analytics/funnels) natively.** Go to Product analytics >
   Funnels and add your onboarding steps in order. This part needs no join; PostHog does it out
   of the box.
2. **Connect [Stripe](/docs/cdp/sources/stripe)** via
   [Data pipeline > Sources](/docs/data-warehouse/sources) to get first-payment dates.
3. **Create a "paid" [cohort](/docs/data/cohorts).** Under Cohorts > New cohort, match users with
   a Stripe invoice `status = paid` (a warehouse condition), then use it as the funnel's
   breakdown.
4. **Compare completion rates by eye**, or open the [SQL editor](/docs/data-warehouse/sql) if you
   want the lift computed for you in one query instead of reading two funnels side by side.

## How the Skill works

The agent finds your signup-to-activation events and each user's first successful Stripe
payment, joined on email. From there it either builds a funnel broken down by paid vs unpaid, or
computes the completion-rate lift of each step among payers versus non-payers, whichever fits
what you asked. The caveat always ships with the answer: this is correlation, a step that
correlates with paying isn't guaranteed to cause it.

This looks at the onboarding *path* in aggregate. For scoring which individual leads sales should
call right now instead, that's [Lead scoring](/pocket-guides/context-warehouse/lead-scoring).
