# Which leads deserve the sales team's time?

A CRM full of leads doesn't tell sales who to call first; product usage does. This skill scores
CRM leads by recent product usage and surfaces the warmest ones sales hasn't yet reached: a
product-qualified-lead list.

## What the answer looks like

The skill looks for patterns like <SeeFig n={1} />: Priya was seen 6 hours ago and is still
listed as a trial, not yet a marketing-qualified lead: exactly the kind of engaged, uncontacted
account this list exists to surface before someone else notices.

## Doing this by hand

1. **Connect [HubSpot](/docs/cdp/sources/hubspot) or [Salesforce](/docs/cdp/sources/salesforce)**
   via [Data pipeline > Sources](/docs/data-warehouse/sources).
2. **Define your key [events](/docs/data/events)** under Data management > Events: the handful
   that signal real intent.
3. **Check each lead's activity by hand** in the [Persons](/docs/data/persons) explorer, one at a
   time: workable for a short list, impractical for a full pipeline.
4. **For a real ranked list**, open the [SQL editor](/docs/data-warehouse/sql) to score every lead
   in one query instead of clicking through them individually.

## How the Skill works

The agent defines, with you, the handful of events that signal real intent. Then it scores each
lead by recency and frequency of those events, weighted however you like. It joins the score to
CRM lead records on email and filters to leads not yet marked contacted. The scoring stays
transparent on purpose: a score sales doesn't understand won't get used.

This ranks individual leads to action right now. For analyzing which onboarding *steps* drive
trial-to-paid conversion in aggregate instead, that's
[Onboarding conversion](/pocket-guides/context-warehouse/onboarding-conversion).
