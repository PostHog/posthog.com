# Getting HogPilled

The pull request opened at 4:12 in the morning. It was a simple fix: four lines in `tiles.ts` and one regression test. Nobody had filed a ticket. Nobody had been paged.

Bristle Quillfeather is Unter's only backend engineer. She read the PR over coffee five hours later, and it was how she learned the coverage map on Unter's safety page had been broken for 11 days. 412 people hit the broken page, but no one reported it.

Unter is a ride-share network for hedgehogs, used in this guide to illustrate PostHog in the concrete, rather than the abstract.

This guide covers what it means to have your product instrumented with PostHog, and ultimately, how an instrumented product becomes <Term name="self-driving">self-driving</Term>.
