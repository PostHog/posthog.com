# Getting HogPilled

The pull request opened at 4:12 in the morning. It was simple: four lines in `tiles.ts`, one regression test, and a description of the root cause. Nobody had filed a ticket. Nobody had been paged.

Bristle Quillfeather is Unter's only backend engineer. She read the PR over coffee five hours later, and it was how she learned the coverage map on Unter's safety page had been broken for 11 days. 412 people hit the broken page, but no one reported it.

Unter is a ride-share network for hedgehogs, and it does not exist (although we wish it did.) The storytelling elements of this guide are invented for your amusement, but the trail is not. 

This guide covers what a product records as a side effect of being instrumented with PostHog, and how a product becomes <Term name="self-driving">self-driving</Term>.
