# Getting HogPilled

The pull request opened at 4:12 in the morning. Four lines in `tiles.ts`, one regression test, three paragraphs of root cause. Nobody had filed a ticket. Nobody had been paged.

Bristle Quillfeather is Unter's only backend engineer. She read it over coffee five hours later, and that is how she learned the coverage map on her safety page had been broken for eleven days. Four hundred and twelve people hit it. Not one wrote in.

Unter is a ride-share network for hedgehogs, and it does not exist. Everyone here is invented, and so is every number. The trail is not: all of it is what a product records as a side effect of being instrumented.
