# What counts as activation

By February, Unter needed one number to take into their Bizness Board Meeting. They were hunting for the magic number: the one that meant the product was actually being used.

Signups seemed like the obvious answer, but they were wrong – <SeeFig n={1} />.

A person can sign up, look around, and never cut a hole. For Unter, that person had not really used the product.

## A hole cut is the event

So activation became a hole cut, tracked from sign up to logs – <SeeFig n={2} />.

With tangible proof that a signup meant activation, Unter went to their Bizness Board Meeting with a real number of people who had used Unter. And when the outage arrived in March, this number helped them understand the impact.

## The number was already there

Producing the activation number didn't require more instrumentation. The events Unter had been collecting since January already described the path from booking to a finished hole. A funnel simply put those events in order and added meaning to them.
