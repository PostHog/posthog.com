# Warehouse data, and what a fix is worth

Unter had one backend engineer and a list of things wrong with the site, and no way of ranking them <SeeFig n={1} />. A slow form and a broken link both read as a ticket. Neither says what it costs to leave alone.

Answering that needs something product data cannot supply on its own.

## Three kinds of thing, in one place

What people did in the product, which Unter had collected since January <SeeFig n={2} />. What sat in the warehouse, which for them meant Stripe charges and the subscription table. And the rest of what a company knows: support tickets, the CRM, the issue tracker.

Land all three in one place and the question stops being a project <SeeFig n={3} />. A host who never cut a hole is a subscription that does not renew, and the join that says so is a join.

## Why it compounds

A host who cannot see coverage is a fence that never gets cut, and every uncut fence is a route Unter cannot offer to the hedgehogs that would have used it. One missing hole costs more than one hole. That is the part the ranking has to account for, and it is why a supporting element on a quiet page can be worth more than a broken button on a loud one.

## Defined once, asked three ways

Revenue metrics live as managed views, so the number in the Monday email, the number in the SQL editor and the number PostHog AI returns when asked in English are the same number. In a company of six people that is not governance. It is the difference between one argument and three.
