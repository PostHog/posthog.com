# One installation, many tools

By the middle of February, Under had replay, errors, flags, experiments, surveys and logs running across the site <SeeFig n={1} />. The booking page had not been rebuilt for any of them. They had wired the site once, and kept adding ways to look at what happened.

Session replay was one of those additions. Turn it on and you can watch the form being used, including the moments when someone hesitates or corrects themselves. The addresses are <Term name="masking">masked</Term>, but the struggle with the form remains.

## The same page, a different question

Product analytics looked at the same page another way <SeeFig n={2} />. The See prices button became a named event, with the distance, number of gaps, and selected tier attached to it.

That meant the team could ask who reached that step, who went on to request a ride, and where the conversion fell away. The page stayed the same. The record underneath it got more useful.

## One page, several ways to look at it

An <Term name="experiment">experiment</Term> put a different headline in front of some visitors <SeeFig n={3} />. The <Term name="feature flag">flag</Term> decided which version they saw, and the exposure was recorded alongside the outcome.

A feature flag could ask a different question again <SeeFig n={4} />. A risky reservation feature could go to a small <Term name="cohort">cohort</Term> first, while the team watched for successful crossings and <Term name="exception">exceptions</Term> before giving it to everyone.

Replay showed what people did. Product analytics counted it. Experiments compared it. Flags controlled who got it.

The tools were different. The page underneath was not.

## What that bought, in February

Not much, yet.

At this point the setup was mostly infrastructure: a page that could be watched, measured, compared, and changed without instrumenting it again each time.

The reason to build it was waiting in March.
