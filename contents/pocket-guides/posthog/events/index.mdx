# Custom events and identify()

Three events at Unter were written deliberately <SeeFig n={1} />. The one that mattered most was the signup, for the reason every team eventually learns the hard way: autocapture would have caught that click as a press on a button with some classes on it, and the March redesign would have taken it away.

An event written by hand survives a redesign, because it is named after what happened rather than after what it was wearing at the time.

## Tying a session to a person

The last thing they did that afternoon was call `identify()` after signup <SeeFig n={2} />. Which tied everything a stranger had done before making an account to the account they had just made.

In March this is what made it possible to say that the people who hit the broken map were disproportionately new, and that almost none of them came back.

## One person, two devices

Unter's hosts book on a phone and read the safety page on a laptop <SeeFig n={3} />. And the same call that joins a stranger to an account also joins those two sessions to one person, which is why the eleven days could be counted in people rather than in browsers.
