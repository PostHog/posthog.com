# One installation, many tools

By the middle of February, Unter had [Session Replay](/docs/session-replay), [Error Tracking](/docs/error-tracking), [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags), [Experiments](/docs/experiments), [Surveys](/docs/surveys) and [Logs](/docs/logs) running across the site <SeeFig n={1} />. The booking page had not been rebuilt for any of them. They had installed PostHog once, and kept adding tools to monitor product usage.

With Session Replay, Unter could watch how forms were being used, including the moments when someone hesitates or corrects themselves. The addresses are <Term name="masking">masked</Term> for privacy, but the user behavior becomes watchable.

## Using different tools on one page

[Product Analytics](/docs/product-analytics) looked at the same page another way <SeeFig n={2} />. The "See prices" button became a named event, with the distance, number of gaps, and selected tier attached to it.

That meant the team could ask who reached that step, who went on to request a ride, and where the conversion fell away.

## Experiments and feature flags

An <Term name="experiment">experiment</Term> put a different headline in front of some visitors <SeeFig n={3} />. The <Term name="feature flag">flag</Term> decided which version they saw, and the exposure was recorded alongside the outcome.

A feature flag could ask a different question again <SeeFig n={4} />. A risky reservation feature could go to a small <Term name="cohort">cohort</Term> first, while the team watched for successful crossings and <Term name="exception">exceptions</Term> before giving it to everyone.

## What the data told Unter

Session Replay showed what people did. Product Analytics counted it. Experiments compared it. Feature Flags controlled who got it.

But the data didn't tell Unter much, yet.

At this point the setup was mostly infrastructure: a page that could be watched, measured, compared, and changed without instrumenting it again each time.

The reason to build it was waiting once their users started arriving.
